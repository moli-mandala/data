# Pass 107: elder brother, morning and evening

44 records accepted: fourteen elder-brother forms, fourteen evening forms, eleven bihān-type morning forms, three savār-type and two savēl-type morning forms.

Full primary readings distinguish *savāra 13290 (Gujarati savār) from *savēla 13291 (savel/saver, savere). The bihān family 11813 carries Turner's alternative MIA vibhāyana explanation explicitly in the evidence. Dada/daju use the documented kinship comparisons in *dādda, with the nursery-family limitation retained.

Morning/evening compounds, consonant-bearing santo/sānto forms, short ben/bhen and haver, learned-looking sandhyā responses, and bihan records mixing morning with yesterday/tomorrow remain outside this exact pass. Phonetic proximity and related time senses alone do not resolve all those analyses. The initial searches for bhor and sakāl were followed by full entries, but no targets from those families were saved here.
