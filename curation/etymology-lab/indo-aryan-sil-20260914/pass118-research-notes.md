# Pass 118: fire families

21 records selected across eastern joy/zoi/jai and agni-family āīg/aⁱg/aig/aghi. Complete CDIAL 5300, 6606 and 55 articles were read after homepage discovery. The accepted notes retain the eastern vowel/sibilant variation and the high-vowel placement or aspiration in the agni forms.

Ten Hajong džui records are held after a substantive competing-etymon check: CDIAL 5300 includes Assamese zūi under jyotis, but 6606 addendum gives Assamese jui (phonetic z-) under dyuti. The user’s preference to link uncertain Indo-Aryan borrowing routes does not choose between these distinct etyma. Additional primary historical evidence is needed.

Baste/basti and agṭhi forms still need their fuller histories. Homepage vadi matches lead to existing Rajasthani node f_b2mg2ivqwhom6, whose description explicitly says representative regional lexical family with unresolved ultimate ancestry; this is not a reconstructed ancient root. No new claim of direct borrowing from that node was made. Bishnupriya ji was not assigned from its short form alone.
