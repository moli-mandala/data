# Pass 126: short face forms

17 records selected under mukha 10158. The complete primary article gives mouth and face senses across Prakrit muha, northwestern mũ(h), Maiya mũ, Bengali mu, Oriya muhã and mui-type forms elsewhere. The selected breathy/nasal and western glide forms retain their exact spelling and phonetic qualifications.

Western mvi/moi are comparative family links with glide/final-i variation explicitly recorded; no assertion is made that Turner quotes those precise Bhil forms or that a specific donor route has been proved. The period in mũ. remains in the stored response.

Excluded: head-derived munḍā/munḍho face forms, compound responses such as mukpat and mūkh+eye, extended mũhḍā, and rup/śekel appearance words. They were not collapsed into mukha based solely on their English face gloss. No records newly held in this pass.
