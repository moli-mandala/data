# Pass 102: self-pronouns and fruit-flower expressions

58 accepted records, 84 links: 29 bare self/polite/inclusive-pronoun forms; three polite-you plus people expressions; 23 fruit-plus-flower expressions; three regional bare fruit nouns. Nine personal-pronoun uses of own-like forms held for branch analysis.

CDIAL 1135.1 explicitly supports the semantic development self → polite you/inclusive we. It separately promotes *ātmanaka own, whose āpṇũ/apnā-like forms can resemble these pronouns. The nine holds preserve that real branch distinction. No uniform assignment based on the English gloss was made.

The full phal-phul response is represented with two ordered components, 9051 fruit and 9092 flower. Source vowel length and word boundaries are unchanged. Collective fruit meaning is the survey gloss; the components do not individually both mean fruit.

Longer dual expressions (āpā̃ donyɔ and relatives), apansarv, apʌnsʌb and other expressions remain available for separate full constituent analysis. No unexamined response was marked held simply to reduce the remaining inventory.
