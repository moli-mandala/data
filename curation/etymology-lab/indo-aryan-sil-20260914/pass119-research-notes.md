# Pass 119: moonlight and moon

33 records selected under CDIAL 5301.1 jyotsnā. The full article and addenda support Jaunsari jhūn, Nepali jun, Halbi jon, Assamese zonāk/jonāk and the Hindi/Awadhi/Braj junhāī/jõdhaiyā series. Accepted notes retain moonlight-to-moon sense extension, the Assamese -ak extension, and local vowel, nasal, glide and h/dh variation in the extended Tharu/Bagheli responses.

The complete dina 6328 and sūra 13574 articles were also checked. They do not by themselves establish the shortened northwestern dī̃/dẽ/deõ sun forms or every homepage sūraj match. Those need the competing day-word histories and exact sūra/sūrya/sūriya subsections checked; no root-level assignment was made merely from the search result. Retroflex-looking Bhil ḍihi and other source-glyph questions remain separate.

No source spelling or segmentation was changed. No sun or sky senses were folded into the moon batch.
