# Pass 121: sunshine and sūrya

20 records selected: five Nepal-area ghām sun responses and fifteen surj/sury-family sun responses. Complete CDIAL 4445 and 13574 were read. Platts pp. 697–698 explicitly connects Hindi sūraj/sūrj to Sanskrit sūryaḥ, supporting the choice of 13574.2 rather than the generic sūra head. Full public query results and URLs are preserved in pass121-platts-research.json and the primary-article archive.

Qualifications: ghām’s sun sense extends regional sunshine/solar heat; surj/surz/surc/sury forms retain voicing, aspiration, palatal and vowel notation. Learned or regional contact influence remains unresolved. Short sur/sure/sūrū and compounds with Nārāyaṇa remain outside this exact set.

## Eastern bel/beli still open

Full CDIAL 12115 velā explicitly discusses probable Dravidian origin, citing Gadaba vēle sun/daytime, Kuwi vēḍa sun, Malto béru and Kurukh bīṛī sun/time. It distinguishes the velā and *veḍā branches and gives Assamese beli time, turn, year and Oriya beḷa daytime. This makes an eastern sun comparison plausible but does not establish whether each survey bel/beṛ/beli follows the IA word or another contact route. No blanket etymology or donor was assigned from resemblance alone. The primary article is preserved for follow-up.
