# Pass 99: Pothwari phonation and verb valency

56 accepted records in four families (14 each): smoke, wash, stay/live and chicken. Fifteen burn responses held.

CDIAL 6886 provides unusually specific support: Pothwari tō- ‘wash’ with low rising tone. The survey breathy-vowel notation is retained in the evidence rather than silently normalized away. The exceptional tṳ vowel is qualified individually through its exact form plus family evidence.

Pothwari re̤ ‘to live’ is read as stay/remain, supported by Punjabi rahiṇā and Western Pahari rēhṇā and the explicit ‘live’ gloss in the addendum. The precise elicitation context remains qualified.

Burn requires separate treatment: CDIAL 6654 explicitly labels *dvalati intransitive, while the survey elicits burning wood. These may be local ambitransitive forms, reduced causatives or elicitation mismatches. The current evidence does not choose among these. One bāḷ record combines hair and multiple burn glosses and additionally needs source-boundary review. No source correction was attempted.

Longer/slash-separated smoke and chicken responses and unexplained koŋaṛi/tuŋo forms remain outside the accepted set. None is counted as adjudicated by mere similarity.
