# Pass 96 research qualifications

Five families accepted with exact target sets in pass96_prepare.py. All records retain source spelling and language identity.

- Hajong tśuk ‘eye’: comparative connection is supported by CDIAL 4560 Bengali cok(h), Assamese saku and Prakrit cakkhu. The u vowel and unaspirated final k are explicitly qualified. Turner says the eastern forms are probably Prakrit loans; the family edge does not claim settled inheritance.
- Lime uses CDIAL 4889 sense 3 within the existing cūrṇa node. There is no promoted 4889-3 node. This is lime used with betelnut, not a citrus fruit.
- Maternal uncle uses 10055 māma, a nursery family. The source’s ‘older’ elicitation is preserved but is not attributed as inherent word meaning. Homepage search also returned a maternal-uncle attestation attached to 10056 māmaká ‘mine’; full primary reading shows that is not a suitable parent. No pre-existing graph edge was changed.
- The camra-type skin nouns use promoted extension 4701-2. Extra medial-b forms (cāmbəḍu, cambaḍo and others), slash-separated responses and unusual cor are not covered by this batch.
- Full CDIAL 6298 dāru wood was inspected after homepage discovery. Bhatri/Halbi daṛu needs further regional evidence for the retroflex flap; it was not automatically equated with dāru. The Gawri dāṛu question occurs in the dāruka subsection and does not establish the Bhatri/Halbi analysis.
- Semantic expansion discovery returned 42 records. Most have previously recognized source-glyph questions. They remain unresearched unless separately accepted; candidate similarity alone is not adjudication.
