# Northern Dhule milk: source glyph mismatch

Physical p. 109 (printed p. 101), item 91, was independently inspected as a complete page and in a 400-dpi close-up. Plain/dental d plus dental subscript marks have been stored as retroflex ɖ/ḍ. All twelve survey milk records link to CDIAL 6391 dugdha; source forms and IDs remain unchanged.

This finding is limited to item 91. Other items require independent glyph checks. A lexical-source correction requires the source-ingestion checklist and identity/graph reconciliation; none was performed during this etymology pass.

Exact IDs and PDF identity are in `dhule-milk-source-audit.json`.
