# Pass 117: needle derivatives and source verification

Four records selected: three Palula selenī responses as variants of the existing Strand silʹêni noun, and one Khowar śūnź under CDIAL 13551.2 *sūñcī.

## Palula evidence resolved

The homepage connected silḗni to CDIAL 13444. The complete Turner entry gives the sewing verb but no sileni noun. Inspection of Strand’s original cached HTML (.cache/strand-legacy/Phal-s.html, needle paragraph) establishes the missing analysis: silʹêni, needle, sil-ʹân-i, OIA sīvyati, T. 13444.1. Therefore the existing database connection has primary support, but attribution to Turner alone would be misleading. The accepted records link to the same-language lexical noun and cite Strand. A live page request failed; the preserved original cache was used. Liljegren’s installed raw vocabulary independently has siléeni needle, but no extra ancestry claim was taken from it.

## Remaining questions

CDIAL 13443 distinguishes sīvana/sīvanī from sēvana/sēvanī. Jaunsari seuṇə/seuyṇə and similar forms need the branch distinguished; surface e alone is insufficient, since the entry also documents vowel variation. Bshk sīlen/sīlīn needs a local derivational or donor account rather than automatically borrowing the Palula analysis. Khowar śūny needs its final glyph interpreted before equating it with śunǰ. Tipni/tipəni still lacks a primary match. These remain research leads; no held-record count was inflated to make them look complete.

The generic English-origin label attached to another Khowar needle/syringe entry was not adopted. The accepted śūnź cites the actual Khowar needle comparandum in CDIAL 13551.2.
