# Pass 116: thorns and needles

57 records selected: five short eastern kaṇṭa thorn forms, nineteen kaṇṭaka thorn forms, thirty-one sūcī needle forms and two *sūñcī needle forms. The complete primary entries and addenda were read after homepage discovery.

Qualifications retained: dental/retroflex notation in kā̃t and koṇta/kə̃nta, nasal loss and final u in Bhil kaṭu, western s/h weakening and glide/vowel variation in needle, and the explicitly analogical Nepali siyo formation. Bengali śuc is placed in *sūñcī because Turner explicitly lists denasalized suc there; Oriya sũn.ci follows suñci. The thorn noun is not linked to the similarly shaped adjectival kāṇṭaka thorny entry.

Not resolved: Bishnupriya huci needs the retained medial consonant and correct subsection established; Khowar śūny/śūnź and the selenī/seuṇə needle groups need their own extension histories. Tipni/tipəni and long/compound responses remain separate. No source spelling was changed and no universal dental/retroflex substitution was used.
