# Pass 122: bow and rainbow

38 records selected across bare dhanus and indradhanuṣ. Platts p. 548 explicitly gives dhanush bow and rainbow, supplying the semantic evidence absent from most of the CDIAL 6726 comparanda. CDIAL 1577 directly gives indradhanuṣ rainbow. Complete primary articles and the full Platts dhanush response are preserved.

The full compound is retained for Indra forms; this does not strip off the deity name and attach the whole expression to the bow root. Source vowel, rhotic, aspiration and final-sibilant variants are qualified in the notes. Local Indo-Aryan transmission and learned influence remain unresolved.

The broad Platts bān query returned many homonyms. The relevant arrow entry on p. 126 gives reed-shaft, arrow, rocket, but no rainbow sense. CDIAL 9203 likewise establishes arrow, not the survey rainbow shift. Thus bare bāṇ remains open despite the homepage’s existing rainbow comparison; the bow-and-arrow compounds need their own semantic/compositional treatment. No link was made merely by copying the homepage graph.

Rām-, coloured-, carding-bow and other long rainbow expressions remain separate research leads. No records were newly held in this pass.
