# Pass 101: plural pronouns and collective expressions

98 records, 122 assignment rows: 74 bare pronoun records and 24 two-component expressions.

Bare hamu/ami forms use asmad 986. Bare tum/tumi/tumu/tame forms use yuṣmad 10511. The final-u variants and inclusive/exclusive or singular-polite use are qualified in the evidence, with all original elicitation labels preserved. Root ancestry does not assert that those distinctions existed in the earlier language.

Compounds are segmented as hamsab/ami səbu = first-person pronoun + all; tūsab/tū̃sab = tū + all; tumlog/tūmlog/tum log = tum + people; hemlog = hem + people. Each has two ordered component edges. These are etymologies of the components, not claims of inherited Sanskrit compounds.

Tū and tum are kept apart: CDIAL 5889 versus 10511. The search implementation can return tum-related matches under both, but the full entries support the selected distinction.

Remaining third-person expressions, āp log, thē log, t ūmlog with unusual internal spacing, retroflex-marked ṭum log, and longer or inflected sab expressions require their own constituent analysis; they are not marked adjudicated in this pass.
