# Pass 112: short-form comparisons

Eight records selected after reading the complete primary entries: Dotyali məṭi soil and dāju elder brother; two Kochila məmə maternal-uncle responses; Bengali tʃok eye; Bhili hamũ exclusive we; Adivasi Oriya sil stone; Dogri pārə heavy.

The primary articles retain the nursery-word qualifications for the kin terms, probable Prakrit transmission for eastern eye, local number/exclusivity distinctions for the pronoun, and regional onset/vowel qualification for heavy. Exact maternal-uncle seniority is not projected back to the ancestor.

Discovery came from the previously preserved pass108-expansion-candidates.json, followed by complete CDIAL articles in pass112-primary-articles.json. This is manual selection, not acceptance of the similarity queue. The remaining retroflex-looking milk, tooth, leaf, seven, river and blood forms need their own evidence; corrections verified for Noiri or Northern Dhule do not establish a correction in another source.
