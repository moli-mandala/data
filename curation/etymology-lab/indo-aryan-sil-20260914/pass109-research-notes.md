# Pass 109: quantity and entirety

41 records linked: 24 western all/whole forms under akṣata 28, ten Pothwari baõ many under bahu 9187, five dental dhere under 5599-2 and two retroflex ḍher under 5599.

Full primary evidence supports the dental/retroflex split: Turner explicitly assigns Nepali dher/dherai to subsection 2. Gujarati ākhũ and Marathi ākhā support the western all/whole family; the original all glosses and final vowels remain unchanged.

Initial searches for muc many, huṭu small, ḍāṅgar large and olpo little returned no linked comparandum useful for a decision. Those words still need primary regional research. Search failure is not an etymology, and they are not counted as adjudicated by this pass. Related-looking habbi/hubai all forms and longer quantity expressions also remain outside this exact set.
