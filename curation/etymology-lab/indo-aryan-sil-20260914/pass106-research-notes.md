60 records: fifteen two, twenty-five nine, ten northwestern three under 5994-2, and ten western n-final three under 5994-3.

Exact source stress, syllabicity, nasalisation and vowel notation are retained. The evidence explicitly distinguishes an attested dictionary comparator from a local survey vowel outcome, especially eastern noi/nɔi nine. No phonetic spelling was rewritten.

Classifier-bearing duita/tinta/no ta/nou ta, western ben/bene extensions, Khowar yū, Kullui palatalised three variants and unexplained Gowro ʦ̣vā remain outside this exact set. These require additional morphology or sound evidence; they are not treated as researched holds here.
