# Pass 120: day words naming the sun

42 records selected under CDIAL 6333 divasa. The full article explicitly supports the sun sense in Awan dèõ, Lahnda dēhũ, Punjabi dĩh/dẽh and regional dihāṛā. The earlier dina-only investigation did not establish this ancestry; the divasa article now supplies the relevant comparisons.

Qualifications retained: h weakening/loss and breathiness, nasal-vowel notation, diũ vowel variation, and Bhil final u/o and initial retroflex notation. The longer dahaḍu/dahəḍo family is supported by Old Marwari dihāḍo/dhyāḍo and Old Gujarati dihāḍaü, alongside the regional sun senses. No universal dental/retroflex correction is asserted. Turner’s discussion of MIA h as possible crossing with ahar is retained in the longer-family notes.

Bare nonnasal dī, short ḍihi/dihi and other source-glyph questions remain outside this selected set. Sky-glossed din was not folded into it. The graph records the family while leaving precise local Indo-Aryan transmission unresolved.
