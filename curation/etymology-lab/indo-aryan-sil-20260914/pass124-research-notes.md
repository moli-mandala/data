# Pass 124: eggplant branches

13 records selected: nine Bhatri baṇga under vaṅga (11503.5), and four Kaithal/Gojri/Oriya longer forms under vātiṅgaṇa (11503.1). Full primary articles 9369 and 11503 were read, including the explicit Bastar Oriya bāṅgā comparison. Source nasal notation and vowel/velar variation are retained. Probable deeper Dravidian origin in the article does not establish direct Dravidian borrowing by these modern survey varieties.

The discovery route initially missed 11503 because the modern canonical spelling uses ŋ, while the cited article uses ṅ, and the root node has ancestry rather than entry status. A Wiktionary reference link was used only to locate CDIAL entry 11503, then the complete original cached CDIAL article supplied the evidence. This is not a missing-source diagnosis.

Remaining western riŋgaṇ/ringaṇ forms need the initial r development checked; they were not assigned simply because the gloss matches. Bishnupriya bei̯noŋ/bei̯loŋ and Vasavi beŋge/beŋgõ need their branch or extension histories resolved. SSNP responses containing mūm/mūŋ remain outside this pass pending source-boundary review.
