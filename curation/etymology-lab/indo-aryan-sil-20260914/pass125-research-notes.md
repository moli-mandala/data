# Pass 125: body and head

17 body records selected: nine Hajong gau under gātra 4124 and eight çarir/saril under śarīra 12335. The complete articles give the eastern gā/gāw/gāo body series and Pali/Prakrit sarīra/western Pahari sarīr. Notes retain diphthong, sibilant and r/l variation and unresolved learned or local transmission.

Eleven munḍ/munḍi/mūnḍ head records are held. Full CDIAL 10247 explicitly discusses the unaspirated head forms as perhaps deriving from or crossing with 10191 muṇḍa shaven. Therefore mere matching to the homepage’s muṇḍa head links does not resolve ancestry. This is a competing-root question rather than just uncertain cross-IA borrowing, and needs comparative review.

Longer munḍka/munka/munko forms remain separate: the 10247 addendum supplies western Pahari mvṇḍke but does not by itself determine the Bhil suffix history. Retroflex-looking gaṭ body is also outside the accepted set. Other unmatched cheek/face terms remain unresearched.
