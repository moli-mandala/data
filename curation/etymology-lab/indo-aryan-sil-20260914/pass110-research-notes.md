# Pass 110: eastern white, bird, dog and buffalo

76 accepted records: thirteen white, seventeen bird, sixteen dog, thirty buffalo.

Full CDIAL articles and addenda support the comparative families. Evidence explicitly retains Hajong pukhi u versus Bengali pakhi a, Bishnupriya pahiya velar weakening and -iya extension, dog lateral/flap variation, and the buffalo family's m/bh/b and nasal/sibilant variation. Possible learned/contact transmission for mahiś-type forms remains open rather than being represented as a verified donor route.

Kovkuṛ dog, very short mos/mus buffalo, buffalo paḍi/ḍubi families, and dob/dhob white without the lateral remain outside this exact set. Raṅga red discovery did not identify a useful linked red entry; the unrelated suraṅga comparison was not used. These remaining forms are research leads, not automatically adjudicated records.
