| **ṭunḍe** | leaf | DEDR3310 gives Telugu tuṇḍe piece and Parji ṭunḍa cloth strip, not leaf; Bhattacharya52 gives eg/egil leaf. Survey initial retroflex ṭ and leaf gloss preserved. No attested semantic bridge or exact candidate found. |
| **kappe** | mountain | DEDR matches to kappe are frog/toad, not mountain. Installed Ollari dictionary yielded no exact mountain match. Do not assign the homograph or invent a hill/frog semantic history. |
| **cālu** | mouth | DEDR2664 attests Gadaba sollu/sol mouth and Naiki salla mouth/face; this is the closest family lead, but the long ā and single l of Ollari cālu need substantiation. | **kanḍ** | neck | Exact Ollari kanḍ in Bhattacharya54 and DEDR1298 is stone, not neck. DEDR1366 gives Ollari gaḍli neck and an Indo-Aryan kaṇṭha comparison;1215 gives Gadaba kandi bead/necklace. | **rasta** | road | DravLex groups this with Kannada/Kodava/Yeruva raste, each explicitly loan-tagged, but does not tag Ollari or identify its donor. Broad search confirms widespread road forms; that does not choose the immediate donor. No jump to a remote Persian source and no copying another language’s borrowing chain. |
| **puṭ** | star | Scoped form+star search locates only this survey record. DEDR puṭ- hits concern birth, smallness and anthills; none supports star. Bhattacharya61 tāra star is a different form. Further field-source or regional lexical evidence needed. |
| **kindir** | tail | Bhattacharya56 independently attests kinḍir tail and explicitly compares Kui kenḍa extreme end and kinḍoṛi curved/bent. Mudhili survey kinḍīr tail corroborates the lexeme. Preserve Bhattacharya’s qualified comparison without inventing an ancestral parent. |
