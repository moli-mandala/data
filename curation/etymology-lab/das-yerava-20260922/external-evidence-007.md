# Arrack comparison

The University of Madras Tamil Lexicon entry cārāyam was read in a reproduced dictionary page: https://agarathi.com/word/சாராயம் . It identifies arrack and lists Telugu/Kannada, Malayalam and Tulu counterparts; its Sanskrit sāra note is a comparison, not a demonstrated immediate donor for Ravula Charaya. The source response remains held. Search results from Wiktionary were discovery only and were not used as primary evidence. The distinct short-vowel Tamil tax/land homonym was not substituted.
