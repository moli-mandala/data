# Schmidt and Kaul: Poguli and Siraji basic numerals

Fourteen reflex records across nine numeral families; standing autonomous authorization. Table 3, printed pp. 298–300 (PDF pp. 68–70), visually checked.

| # | Poguli / Siraji forms and meaning | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **aht / atʰ** ‘eight’ (2) | **aṣṭā**, CDIAL **941** | Turner941 explicitly gives Poguli āhṭ eight and regional aṭh/āṭh. The source Poguli aht and Siraji atʰ fit this branch; their consonant order, aspiration and unmarked length remain as recorded. |
| 2 | **pā̃ʦ** ‘five’ (1) | **pañca**, CDIAL **7655** | Turner7655 explicitly gives Poguli pā̃ċ five beside Kashmiri panċ and Pahari panċ. Poguli pā̃ʦ preserves the nasal vowel and affricate spelling; the more complex Siraji form is held. |
| 3 | **nãŭ / nao** ‘nine’ (2) | **nava²**, CDIAL **6984** | Turner6984 explicitly gives Poguli nāu and Pahari nao nine. Poguli nãŭ and Siraji nao fit these contracted regional forms; nasalisation and diphthong notation are retained. |
| 4 | **ekə** ‘one’ (1) | **\*ēkka**, CDIAL **2462-2** | Turner2462 section2 gives Pahari and regional ek/ēk one from the reinforced *ēkka stem. Siraji ekə retains k and final ə, fitting that branch; the alternative unreinforced section1 is not selected from the surface spelling alone. |
| 5 | **sat** ‘seven’ (1) | **sapta**, CDIAL **13139** | Turner13139 gives Poguli satt seven alongside regional sat/satt. The source Poguli sat has the diagnostic s-t frame; its single t is preserved. The Siraji spelling with an additional r is held. |
| 6 | **śeh / śah** ‘six’ (2) | **ṣaṣ**, CDIAL **12803** | Turner12803 section1 gives Kashmiri śeh and Ḍoḍi śah, and also lists Poguli śe. The source Poguli śeh and Siraji śah belong to this ś-initial branch, rather than the separate Indo-Aryan ch/cha branch in section3; the h is retained. |
| 7 | **dah / daś** ‘ten’ (2) | **daśa**, CDIAL **6227** | Turner6227 explicitly gives Kashmiri dah and neighbouring Pahari daś ten from daśa. Poguli dah and Siraji daś retain their distinct final consonants; no modern donor is inferred. |
| 8 | **trey** ‘three’ (1) | **trayaḥ**, CDIAL **5994** | Turner5994 section1 gives NiDoc treya/tre and neighbouring Ḍoḍi trēī three. Siraji trey fits the tr-initial masculine series; its written final y is retained. Poguli cayi instead needs the c-initial section2 correspondence checked. |
| 9 | **dih / duí** ‘two’ (2) | **dva**, CDIAL **6648** | Turner6648 explicitly gives Poguli dīh and regional dui/dūī two. The source Poguli dih and Siraji duí fit these two branches of the same dva entry; their vowel length and final stress are preserved. |

Six records held:
- **păʦʰ** ‘five’: The printed form has a distinct vowel and aspirated affricate. Determine whether this is the expected local pañca outcome before copying Poguli pā̃ʦ.
- **ʦarvar** ‘four’: Printed form appears tsawar; compiled ʦarvar needs transcription-profile review and the whole formation needs a specific catvāri branch.
- **ʦut** ‘four’: Printed form appears tsur; compiled ʦut differs at the final consonant. Source/profile review is needed before selecting a branch.
- **yah** ‘one’: Turner gives Poguli yakh under *ēkka; the source yah lacks k. Establish whether the difference is regular loss or a transcription/lexical distinction before linking.
- **sartʰ** ‘seven’: Printed form appears satʰ; compiled sartʰ contains an additional r. Resolve source/profile discrepancy before assigning sapta.
- **cayi** ‘three’: Turner5994 section2 explicitly gives Poguli cāē, but the reviewed source writes caji and compiled cayi. Resolve that vowel/glide correspondence before assigning a section.
