# Grangali survey: six basic-vocabulary families

Fifteen reflex records across six families. Buddruss printed pp. 27, 29–30, 33–34 were checked against rendered source images; the full Turner articles were read.

| # | Forms and meaning | Parent | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **sūrə / śūrə / śūrə** ‘dog’ (3) | **śuna**, CDIAL **12528** | Turner12528 directly lists Ningalami šũṛə and Shumashti šū̃ṛə. Buddruss explicitly says Grangali sūrə is assimilated from the same šūrə form; the source sibilants and unmarked nasalization are preserved. |
| 2 | **āt / āt** ‘flour’ (2) | ***ārta²**, CDIAL **1338** | Turner1338 directly lists Shumashti āt flour in the *ārta/aṭṭa family. Buddruss gives identical Grangali and Shumashti āt; this is the flour word, not an unrelated homophone. |
| 3 | **darím / dārim** ‘pomegranate’ (2) | **dāḍima**, CDIAL **6254** | Turner6254 section1 directly gives Shumashti dā́ṛim pomegranate. Buddruss pairs it with Grangali darím; the regional Dardic pair belongs with this section, while Turner section2 covers a separate set of regional dārim forms. |
| 4 | **gā̃s / gā̃s** ‘grass’ (2) | **ghāsa**, CDIAL **4471** | Turner4471 directly lists Shumashti gā̃s grass, and Buddruss gives the same Grangali and Shumashti form. Both retain the source nasalization; no modern donor is inferred. |
| 5 | **nadí / nandí / nādí** ‘river’ (3) | **nadī**, CDIAL **6943** | Turner6943 directly gives Ningalami nandī and Shumashti nädī within the early-nasalization series. Buddruss pairs these with Grangali nadí. The three source spellings and vowel quantities remain distinct. |
| 6 | **ṣā̃rə / śārə / ṣoũkrə́** ‘head’ (3) | ***śrāya**, CDIAL **12694** | Turner12694 directly lists Ningalami ṣoũkṛə and Shumashti šāṛe as enlarged forms of the *śrāya head family. Buddruss pairs these with Grangali ṣā̃rə, noting its retroflex sibilant; the extra nasalization and suffixal differences are retained. |
