# Grangali survey: stone, red, sister, rain, sand and louse

Eleven reflex records across five families plus one explicit Pashai-to-Shumashti loan. Buddruss printed pp. 32–34 visually checked; full Turner articles read.

| # | Forms and meaning | Parent | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **bōt / bōt** ‘stone’ (2) | ***varta³**, CDIAL **11348** | Turner11348 section1 directly lists Ningalami bōt stone. Buddruss records identical Grangali and Ningalami bōt and explicitly contrasts this stone word with the cooked-rice homophone. |
| 2 | **artə́ / vartə́ / aratə́** ‘red’ (3) | **ārakta / uparakta**, CDIAL **1296, 2209** | Turner1296 directly gives Shumashti aratə́ red under ārakta; Buddruss identifies Grangali artə́ with the same arakta family. Buddruss groups the three source forms under arakta generally; the prefixed Ningalami assignment follows Turner’s more specific analysis. |
| 3 | **pas / pas** ‘sister’ (2) | **svasṛ**, CDIAL **13913** | Turner13913 directly lists Ningalami pas sister under svasṛ, alongside neighbouring Dardic pas forms. Buddruss records the identical Grangali and Ningalami pas pair. |
| 4 | **boṣ / vas** ‘rain’ (2) | **varṣa**, CDIAL **11392** | Turner11392 directly gives Shumashti wāṣ rain and other Dardic b/waṣ forms. Buddruss Grangali boṣ and Shumashti vas fit this rain family. Turner cautions that some Dardic long-vowel forms could instead continue vārṣa; that historical alternative is retained in the note. |
| 5 | **seóu / sīu** ‘sand’ (2) | **sikatā**, CDIAL **13386** | Turner13386 directly lists Shumashti sī́u sand alongside Pashai sēo/sīu. Buddruss Grangali seóu belongs to this regional formation. Turner warns that Iranian influence can affect the s-initial Dardic forms; this assignment does not settle the deeper contact history. |
