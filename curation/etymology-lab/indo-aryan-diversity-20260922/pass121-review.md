# Darai: short, axe, pillar, stool, and wall

Five source-backed reflex records across five etyma.

| # | Darai form | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **hõʦo** ‘short’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 46]) | ***hōccʰa-**, CDIAL **14084-5** | Dhakal p. 46 prints /hõʦo/ “short.” Turner14084 section 5 explicitly gives Nepali hõco and hoco “low, short,” plus Jaunsari hochā “short.” The nasal vowel and affricate distinguish this branch from the article’s other defective-word sections. |
| 2 | **ṭaŋgi** ‘axe’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 53]) | **ṭaṅga-**, CDIAL **5427-2** | Dhakal p. 53 prints /ṭaŋgi/ “axe.” Turner5427 section 2 gives Bengali ṭāṅgi “spade, axe,” Oriya ṭāṅgi “battle-axe” and Bhojpuri ṭāṅī “axe”; Darai -gi belongs to the ṭaṅga section rather than section 1 ṭaṅka “chisel.” |
| 3 | **tʰunʰi** ‘pillar of wood’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 55]) | **sthū́ṇā**, CDIAL **13774** | Dhakal p. 55 prints /tʰunʰi/ “pillar of wood” and notes breathy /nʰ/ as a predictable intervocalic feature. Turner13774 gives Old Awadhi thūnī “small pillar,” Bhojpuri thūnhī “prop,” and Pali/Prakrit thūṇā “post, pillar”; these support the Darai wooden-post noun. |
| 4 | **pirʰa** ‘low wooden stool’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 56]) | **pīṭha**, CDIAL **8222** | Dhakal p. 56 prints /pirʰa/ “low wooden stool” and explicitly treats breathy /rʰ/ as nonphonemic. Turner8222 section 1 gives Nepali pirā, Oriya piṛhā, Maithili pīṛhā and Hindi pīṛhā “stool”; the Darai sense and stem match this section. |
| 5 | **berʰa** ‘wall’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 56]) | **vēṣṭá**, CDIAL **12130** | Dhakal p. 56 prints /berʰa/ “wall” in the same nonphonemic breathy-/rʰ/ set. Turner12130 gives Assamese ber “wall of house,” Oriya beṛā “wall of house,” and Maithili beṛ “hedge, wall”; the Darai noun corresponds to this enclosure/wall branch. |
