# Grangali survey: higher numerals

Seventeen reflex records across six numeral families. Buddruss printed pp. 28, 31–34 visually checked; full Turner articles read.

| # | Forms and meaning | Parent | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **bas / bas / bās** ‘twelve’ (3) | **dvādaśa**, CDIAL **6658** | Turner6658 section1 directly lists Ningalami bas and Shumashti bās twelve. The source Grangali bas matches that regional b-s shape. The distinct duv- section2 is excluded. |
| 2 | **zas / zas / zās** ‘eleven’ (3) | **ekādaśa**, CDIAL **2485** | Turner2485 directly lists Shumashti zās eleven alongside western Dardic z/ž formations. Buddruss gives Grangali and Ningalami zas with the same z-s frame; all source vowel quantities are retained. |
| 3 | **ʦōdḗs / ʦaudēs / ʦãudas** ‘fourteen’ (3) | **caturdaśa**, CDIAL **4605** | Turner4605 section1 directly lists Ningalami ċaudēs and Shumashti ċäudas fourteen. Grangali ʦōdḗs follows the same c-d-s family, with its source long vowels preserved. |
| 4 | **sōrā́s / surōs / sorās** ‘sixteen’ (3) | **ṣoḍaśa**, CDIAL **12812** | Turner12812 section1 directly lists Ningalami ṣurós and Shumashti ṣōṛā́s sixteen. Grangali sōrā́s matches the latter Dardic formation; the source sibilant and r spelling remain unchanged. |
| 5 | **ṣu / ṣo** ‘six’ (2) | ***ṣuvaṭ**, CDIAL **12803-2** | Turner12803 section2 directly lists Ningalami ṣō six and neighbouring Ashkun ṣu. Grangali ṣu fits that u-vowel branch; the section1 śeh and section3 cha branches are excluded. |
| 6 | **λēvā́s / slevās / λāvaṣ** ‘thirteen’ (3) | ***trayēdaśa**, CDIAL **6001-2** | Turner6001 section2 directly lists Ningalami ṣlewás and Shumashti λäṷas thirteen. Buddruss Grangali λēvā́s belongs to this lateral-initial Dardic series; its vowel and long ā are retained. |
