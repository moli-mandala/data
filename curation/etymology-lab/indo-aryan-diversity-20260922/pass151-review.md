# Darai: good, road, and waist cloth

Three source-backed reflex records across three etyma.

| # | Darai form | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ramrə** ‘good’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 60]) | **ramyà with -ḍ- extension**, CDIAL **10636-2** | Dhakal p. 60 prints /ram.rə/ “good.” Turner10636 describes the -ḍ- extension and explicitly gives Nepali rāmro “beautiful, good” and Western Pahari rāmṛā “good”; Darai ramrə has the same regional shape and sense, selecting the established extension node 10636-2. |
| 2 | **pəĩḍa** ‘road’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 53]) | ***padadaṇḍa**, CDIAL **7753** | Dhakal p. 53 prints /pəĩḍa/ “road.” Turner7753 gives Punjabi paĩḍā “road, distance,” Maithili paĩṛā “road” and Hindi paĩḍā “track, path”; the nasalized Darai road noun closely matches this family. |
| 3 | **pəṭka** ‘a piece of cloth worn round a waist by woman’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 68]) | **paṭṭa with -kk- extension**, CDIAL **7700-2** | Dhakal p. 68 prints /pəṭ.ka/ “a piece of cloth worn round a waist by woman.” Turner7700 gives Hindi paṭkā “loincloth” and Pali paṭṭaka “girdle”; the established -kk- extension node 7700-2 captures the -ṭk- cloth form, while the source specifies the wearer. |
