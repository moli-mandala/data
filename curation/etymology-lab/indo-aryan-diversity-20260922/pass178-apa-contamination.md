# Existing Andersen *apa* edge: homograph contamination

Andersen printed p. 138 (PDF p. 136) identifies **apa** (source form `f_3fqxqfrk5xlgy`) as the preverb “away from, off,” compares RV *ápa* and Pali *apa*, and cross-references *apacāy-* and *apacāyanā-*. The page image `tmp/pdfs/andersen-etymology/p136.png` was visually checked.

The installed `cldf/edges.csv` has accepted rank-1 edge `(Child_ID=f_3fqxqfrk5xlgy, Parent_ID=1188, Kind=reflex)`. Turner’s complete article 1188, retained in `andersen-primary178.json`, is **āpa** “in compounds, ‘obtaining’” from √*āp*. It is a different word and sense from the source’s *apa* preverb; the matching bare preverb was not found as a standalone Turner head in the local CDIAL index. No replacement CDIAL ID is proposed here.

This is an existing compiled edge, outside the editable Andersen etymology sidecar. It was not changed. The tempting *apacāy-* decomposition was excluded from pass178 to avoid propagating the wrong ancestor.
