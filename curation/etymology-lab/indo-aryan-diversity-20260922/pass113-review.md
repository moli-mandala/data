# Darai: bile, ginger, and calf

Three source-backed reflex records across three etyma.

| # | Darai form | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **pittə** ‘bile’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 58]) | **pittá**, CDIAL **8181** | Dhakal p. 58 prints /pittə/ “bile.” Turner8181 gives Old Indo-Aryan pittá, Pali and Prakrit pitta, Oriya pita and Assamese pit “bile”; the Darai dental geminate and meaning match. Turner notes possible ultimate Munda origin, but this Indo-Aryan word is the relevant ancestor. |
| 2 | **ədva** ‘ginger’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 59]) | **ārdraka**, CDIAL **1341** | Dhakal p. 59 prints /ədva/ “ginger.” Turner1341 section 1 gives Nepali aduwā, Garhwali ādu, Bengali ādā and Oriya adā “ginger”; Darai ədv- aligns especially with the Nepali aduwā form while the eastern comparanda establish the wider inherited family. |
| 3 | **baʦʦʰi** ‘a calf’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 60]) | **vatsá**, CDIAL **11239** | Dhakal p. 60 prints /baʦʦʰi/ “a calf.” Turner11239 gives Pali and Prakrit vaccha “calf,” Nepali bācho/°chi, Oriya bāchī “heifer calf,” and Bhojpuri bāchī “calf”; the Darai feminine -i and geminate affricate match this branch. |
