Three held records:
- **kirmo** ‘worm’: Turner 3438 lists European Romani kirmo 'worm' but explicitly warns that Romani may instead have borrowed Persian kirm; the source p. 128 §2.3.14 only discusses palatalization and does not resolve the immediate donor.
- **murś** ‘man’: Turner 9828 compares European Romani murš to manuṣyà crossed with púruṣa, while the source itself explicitly compares Zargari murš with Sanskrit māriṣa- on p. 147. The conflicting historical branches need editorial adjudication.
- **dār** ‘door’: The source prints dār 'door' on p. 131, but Turner’s door comparanda distinguish inherited European Romani vudar from Indo-Aryan dar; direct Persian dar contact remains possible.
