# Darai: tether, wash, across, and small

Four source-backed reflex records across four etyma.

| # | Darai form | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **dauri** ‘cord for tethering beast’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 47]) | **davara**, CDIAL **6225** | Dhakal p. 47 prints /dauri/ “cord for tethering beast.” Turner6225 gives Hindi daurī “rope to which a string of cattle are fastened,” Nepali ḍori “rope, tether” and regional ḍorī/ḍori “rope.” The unnasalised daur-i shape and precise cattle-tether sense favor this article over the nasal dãurī branch under 6283. |
| 2 | **dʰoulə** ‘washed’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 47]) | ***dhauvati**, CDIAL **6886** | Dhakal p. 47 prints /dʰoulə/ “washed.” Turner6886 gives Prakrit dhō(v)aï “washes,” Nepali dhunu, Maithili dhoeb and Bhojpuri dhowal; Darai -lə is the source-described past ending, leaving the corresponding dʰou- washing stem. Turner6833 itself places Nepali dhunu probably under 6886. |
| 3 | **par** ‘across’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 51]) | **pārá**, CDIAL **8100** | Dhakal p. 51 prints /par/ “across/COND.PART”; the installed record retains the “across” sense. Turner8100 explicitly gives Kumaoni par “at a distance,” Nepali pār “on the other side of” and Maithili/Hindi pār “across.” This assignment addresses the spatial sense only; the source’s conditional-particle use may be a separate grammatical development. |
| 4 | **nanʰi** ‘small’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 55]) | **ślakṣṇá**, CDIAL **12732** | Dhakal p. 55 prints /nanʰi/ “small” among breathy-nasal examples. Turner12732 gives Prakrit laṇha “small,” Punjabi nannhā and Hindi nānh/nānhā “small,” plus Maithili nānhiṭā “childhood.” These comparanda support the Darai nanʰ- stem despite the wider ślakṣṇa family’s multiple regional shapes. |
