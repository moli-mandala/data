# Schmidt and Kaul: Poguli and Siraji body vocabulary

Fourteen reflex records across seven families; standing autonomous authorization. Table 3, printed pp. 283–284 (PDF pp. 53–54), visually checked.

| # | Poguli / Siraji forms and meaning | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **də̄ṛ / dāṛī́** ‘beard’ (2) | **\*dāṁṣṭra**, CDIAL **6250** | Turner6250 explicitly groups Kashmiri dör and regional dāṛī beard with MIA dāṭhikā/dāḍhiā. Poguli central-vowel də̄ṛ and Siraji dāṛī́ fit those regional outcomes. Turner discusses competing explanations of the deeper cluster and possible Dravidian/Munda influence; this link retains that uncertainty. |
| 2 | **aḍʸe / haḍī́** ‘bone’ (2) | **haḍḍa**, CDIAL **13952** | Turner13952 gives Kashmiri aḍa bones, aḍyul large bone and aḍij small bone alongside regional haḍḍī. These support Poguli’s h-less palatalised aḍʸe and Siraji haḍī́ in the same bone family. The source ending and palatalisation are preserved, without assigning a number not specified by the table; Turner doubts a connection with asthi. |
| 3 | **aś / acʰʸ** ‘eye’ (2) | **akṣi**, CDIAL **43** | Turner43 gives Kashmiri achi, Rambani acch and Kishtawari acchī alongside the regional aṣī-type sibilant outcome. These support Poguli aś and Siraji acʰʸ as eye-family reflexes; palatalisation and the source distinction between ś and aspirated c remain intact. |
| 4 | **anglʸe / ãŋglī́** ‘finger’ (2) | **aṅguli**, CDIAL **135** | Turner135 section1 contains Kashmiri onguj, Bhalessi aṅgoli and contracted regional uṅglī/ā̃gḷī forms. Poguli anglʸe and Siraji ãŋglī́ retain the lateral of this branch, rather than selecting section2 aṅgūḍi. Preserve the source ending, nasalisation, stress and palatalisation. |
| 5 | **nam** ‘fingernail’ (2) | **nakha**, CDIAL **6914** | Turner6914 section1 explicitly gives Kashmiri nam nail under early nasalisation, querying an intermediate *naṅg. Both table nam responses match that distinctive regional form; the proposed intermediate remains uncertain, and these are not the geminate-nakkha subsection. |
| 6 | **māz / mās** ‘flesh’ (2) | **māṁsa**, CDIAL **9982** | Turner9982 explicitly gives Kashmiri māz/mās flesh or meat and neighbouring Pahari mās. Poguli māz and Siraji mās match these voiced and voiceless regional outcomes; no meaning or source pronunciation is normalised. |
| 7 | **āht / hat** ‘hand’ (2) | **hasta**, CDIAL **14024** | Turner14024 explicitly gives Poguli āht hand and neighbouring Pahari hat/hatth. This directly supports the table’s Poguli metathesised form and Siraji hat, with their distinct consonant order and vowel quantity preserved. |
