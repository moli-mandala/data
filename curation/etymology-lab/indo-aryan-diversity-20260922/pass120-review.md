# Zargari: one and in the middle

Two source-checked reflex records in two historical families. Standing autonomous authorization. Original PDF printed pp. 127, 143 and the numeral paradigm p. 137, glyph audit and imported keys, homepage searches and full CDIAL articles/addenda were checked. No lexical import or rebuild.

| # | Zargari form and meaning | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **yekʰ** ‘one’ | ***ēkka-**, CDIAL **2462-2** | Turner 2462 §2 explicitly lists European Romani yek 'one' under *ēkka-. The source prints Zargari jekh [jek] 'one' (p. 127 §2.3.5) and explains that final aspirates may deaspirate. The same lemma recurs in the source numeral paradigm (p. 137 §3.5.1). Its separately imported yek spelling already has a rank-1 variant edge to this jekh record; that existing variant is not duplicated. |
| 2 | **mośgār** ‘in the middle’ | **mádhya**, CDIAL **9804** | Turner 9804 explicitly lists German/Hungarian/Welsh Romani maškər/maškár/maskal 'in, between' under mádhya. The source prints Zargari mośgār 'in the middle' among adverbs of place (p. 143 §3.20.1.3). Source §2.3.1 (p. 127) documents merger of voiceless nonaspirates with voiced consonants; the k/g difference is therefore source-compatible, while the vowel details are retained without a new sound-rule claim. |

Two held records:

- **pāng** ‘to break’: The source gives pāng/pāngl 'break' as present/perfect stems (p. 138), and Turner 9361 §2 has European Romani phag- 'to break'; the added nasal and vowel correspondence remain unexplained.
