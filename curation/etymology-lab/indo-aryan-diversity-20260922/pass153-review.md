# Lehr Darrai Pashai: eight direct Turner comparanda

Eight previously unlinked Darrai Pashai records have direct comparanda in full Turner articles. Exact Lehr raw rows and compiled identities are audited in `pass153-source-audit.json`; full primary articles are in `pashai-primary023.json`. The source has no page locator.

| # | Lehr Darrai | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **suncek** ‘sewing needle’ | ***sūñcī-*, CDIAL 13551 §2** | Turner lists Darrai and Wegali Pashai *sūnčék* “needle”. |
| 2 | **ḍamatā** ‘rope’ | ***dā́man*, CDIAL 6283 §1** | Turner lists Darrai *damaṭā́* “rope” as an extension; initial consonant notation differs. |
| 3 | **śāl** ‘unharvested rice’ | ***śāli*, CDIAL 12415** | Turner lists Darrai *šāl* “growing or unhusked rice”. |
| 4 | **karāṛ** ‘wolf’ | ***káḍāra / karāla*, CDIAL 2655** | Turner lists Darrai *karā́ṛ* “wolf” directly. |
| 5 | **maṭēx** ‘frog’ | ***maṇṭrakka- / *maṭrakka-*, CDIAL 9746 §5** | Turner lists Darrai *maṭḗx* “frog”; the wider group is reshaped by taboo and expression. |
| 6 | **pēlā** ‘yellow’ | ***pītala*, CDIAL 8233 §1** | Turner lists Darrai *pḗla* “yellow”. |
| 7 | **tar-** ‘look at’ | ***stharati*, CDIAL 13743** | Turner lists Darrai *tar-* “look, see”. |
| 8 | **aśpāṛ** ‘rice’ | ***ākṣapāṭa-*, CDIAL 1020b (Add. 14264)** | The printed addendum lists Darrai *aṣpā́ṛ* “reaped rice”; Lehr gives broader “rice”. |
