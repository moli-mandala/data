# Darai: touch verb

Three source-backed reflex records in one etymon.

| # | Darai form | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ʦʰiulə** ‘touched’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 47]) | **chupáti**, CDIAL **5055** | Dhakal p. 47 prints /cʰiulə/ “touched” as an /iu/ diphthong example; p. 73 identifies -lə as the past marker. | 2 | **ʦʰike** ‘to touch’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 54]) | **chupáti**, CDIAL **5055** | Dhakal p. 54 prints /cʰike/ “to touch” among aspirated affricates, distinct from unaspirated /cirike/ “to split.” Turner5055 gives Nepali chunu, Maithili chūyab and Hindi chūnā “touches”; Darai cʰi- is the same touch stem. |
| 3 | **ʦʰike** ‘to touch’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 67]) | **chupáti**, CDIAL **5055** | Dhakal p. 67 repeats /cʰi.ke/ “to touch” in the attested-onset table. Turner5055 lists regional cʰu-/cʰui- touch verbs, including Oriya cʰũibā and Maithili chūyab; the independently cited token is the same Darai stem. |
