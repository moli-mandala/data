# Darai: six, below, sand and numeral classifiers

Seven reviewed source records, with ordinary reflexes distinguished from source-labeled classifier derivatives.

| # | Darai form | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ʦʰə** ‘six’ (2 records) | ***kṣaṭ**, CDIAL **12803-3** | Dhakal pp. 65 and 68 prints /cʰə/ six. Turner section 3 has Prakrit cha and Nepali cha from *kṣaṭ, contrasting section 1 ṣa/ṣe-type forms and section 2 ṣu-type forms. The house transcription cʰ→ʦʰ is retained; both source examples share this lexical numeral. |
| 2 | **hẽṭʰ** ‘below / below, down’ (2 records) | ***adhiṣṭāt**, CDIAL **248-2** | Dhakal pp. 53 and 68 explicitly gives /hẽṭʰ/ below/down with final aspirated retroflex. Turner section 2 gives Pali/Pk heṭṭhā, Punjabi heṭh and Bhojpuri heṭhā̃ under *adhiṣṭāt. The source nasalization is preserved, and section 1 adhastāt is not selected. |
| 3 | **bəlva** ‘sand’ (1 records) | **vālukā**, CDIAL **11580** | Dhakal p. 62 prints /bəl.wa/ sand. Turner11580 gives Prakrit vāluā and Nepali bāluwā/baluwā, matching the b-l-w sequence and sense. Darai vowel length is noncontrastive (p. 42); the first vowel and contracted middle syllable remain as printed. |
| 4 | **ekṭa** ‘one’ (1 records) | **ek + classifier -ṭa**, derived from Darai **f_cz67uv3xekayw** | Dhakal p. 59 explicitly labels /ek.ṭa/ one-CLF. The earlier linked Darai ek is the base; -ṭa is a source-attested classifier, so this is a synchronic derived link to the same-source lexical numeral rather than a direct reflex of the whole form. |
| 5 | **ʦarṭa** ‘four’ (1 records) | **ʦar + classifier -ṭa**, derived from Darai **f_iysprsaey63zi** | Dhakal p. 62 explicitly labels /car.ṭa/ four-CLF. The earlier linked Darai ʦar is the base; the source c→ʦ house transcription and classifier -ṭa are retained. Link the complete form as a synchronic derivative of the same-source numeral. |
