| # | Zargari forms and meaning | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **kon / kos / kosku / kostār / kostu / kon** ‘interrogative and indefinite 'who'’ (6) | **kaḥ punar**, CDIAL **2575** | Turner 2575 explicitly cites European Romani kon 'who' and, in its addendum, an oblique kɔs in a Pahari paradigm. The source prints Zargari kon/qon (nominative), kos/qos (oblique), kosku (dative/genitive), kostār (ablative/instrumental), and kostu (locative) together in §3.3.5.1, plus indefinite kon/qon 'somebody' in §3.3.6.1 (p. 136). The case endings and indefinite use are source-level morphology of the same inherited pronoun; no distinct etymon is inferred for each case form. |
| 2 | **ij** ‘yesterday’ (1) | ***hīyas**, CDIAL **14108-2** | Turner 14108 §2 expressly lists Romani Italian yidž and Hungarian īž/ič as 'yesterday' under *hīyas. The source prints ij/iž as the Zargari word for 'yesterday' (§2.3.21 and §3.20.1.2, pp. 128, 143); its pronunciation discussion accounts for the imported glyph variant. The section-2 etymon is the precise parent. |
| 3 | **teli / teli / teli** ‘bottom / below / under’ (3) | **tala**, CDIAL **5731** | Turner 5731 §1 gives European Romani tel, tele 'under' and other Indo-Aryan bottom/below forms under tala. The source prints Zargari teli as 'bottom' (§3.1.15, p. 132), 'below' (§3.20.1.3, p. 143), and 'beneath; under' (§3.20.2.1, p. 144), supporting all three lexical/syntactic attestations. |
| 4 | **nā** ‘no’ (1) | **ná**, CDIAL **6906** | Turner 6906 includes European Romani na 'not' and explicitly notes 'no' in its addendum; the source prints Zargari nā 'no' among negative adverbs in §3.20.1.7 (p. 144). The long vowel is retained from the source transcription, not inferred from the Turner Romani form. |
