| 1 | **kʰur / kʰor** ‘foot’ (2) | **khura**, CDIAL **3906** | Turner3906 section1 explicitly gives Poguli khur and Kashmiri khor foot, matching the two source forms. Seven records held:
- **kʰur / kʰor** ‘foot’ (2): khura3906 is lexically supported, but its existing ancestry reaches cycles 3893↔3748 and 3243↔3894. No graph repair or bypass. The first table row is a held proposal, not an accepted link.
- **masti** ‘hair’: The mastika/head comparison does not yet explain the source hair sense and formation.
- **zuṭó** ‘hair’: Jūṭa5258 explains knot-of-hair forms with intervocalic ṛ/ḍ; the source stop and broad hair sense need further evidence.
- **hōṭ** ‘lip’: Discriminate 245a adhaōṣṭha from 2563 oṣṭha; the h-initial form alone does not settle the competing histories.
- **kʸāliz** ‘liver’: Turner3103 marks Kashmiri kalēja as borrowed from Lahnda. Resolve the immediate history of this Poguli palatalised form before choosing an inherited or borrowed edge.
- **kālzó** ‘liver’: The kālēyaka family is plausible, but its distribution includes loans and questioned Pahari forms; establish the Siraji route before linking.
