# Pashai Darrai: seven source-matched families

Seven reflex records in the Lehr survey; standing autonomous authorization.

| # | Pashai form | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **vēl** ‘night’ | **vikāla**, CDIAL **11625** | Turner gives Pashai Lauṛi vyāl, Arang viyōl and Kuṛangal vel for night, explicitly indicating Shumashti wyel is a loan from Pashai. Darrai vēl belongs to this Pashai regional family; its unmarked source form is preserved. |
| 2 | **ṣoṛ** ‘sixteen’ | **ṣōḍaśa**, CDIAL **12812** | Turner section 1 explicitly gives Pashai Darrai ṣōṛ for sixteen. Section 2 has other Pashai c̣hui/xūi forms and section 3 has Arang/Kuṛangal ṣⁱēṛ/ṣē̃; the Darrai form selects the main section. |
| 3 | **mās** ‘forehead’ | **masta**, CDIAL **9926** | The source itself records stem *mask-*. Turner section 1 gives Pashai Wegal maskē forehead beside Lauṛi māsk brain under mastaka; section 2 is a distinct mastra formation. Darrai mās is a shortened member of the *mask- set, with the truncation noted rather than normalized. |
| 4 | **bēn** ‘honey’ | ***bhēna**, CDIAL **9614** | Turner explicitly lists Pashai Lauṛi and other dialects bēn honey, and Arang bēᵉn, under *bhēna. The same form in the Darrai source supports a family reflex; Turner calls the deeper Indo-European comparison uncertain. |
| 5 | **tārā** ‘star’ | **tāraka**, CDIAL **5798-4** | Turner section 4 explicitly gives Pashai Darrai tārá star. The section contrasts the tārā head, tā́rakā and *tārikā branches, so the exact Darrai witness determines the narrower node. |
| 6 | **kaṛek** ‘tree’ | **kuṭa**, CDIAL **3228** | Turner explicitly gives Pashai Darrai kaṛék tree, oak beside Lauṛi kuṛā́ tree, under kuṭa. The source gloss only says tree, so the source record is not expanded to oak. |
| 7 | **ṣe** ‘six’ | **ṣaṣ**, CDIAL **12803** | Turner section 1 gives Pashai Darrai ṣε῀ six and related Laghman x̌e and Shutul ṣä. The Darrai source writes ṣe without vowel nasalization; this is retained, and section 1 is chosen over the distinct c̣hā section 3. |
