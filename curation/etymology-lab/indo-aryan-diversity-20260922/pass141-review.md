Two still-unlinked Andersen records, two proposed reflex rows under standing autonomous authorization. The printed dictionary pp. 150 and 167 were checked on scans, and complete Turner articles 5193 and 11048 are retained in `andersen-primary141.json`.

| Ashokan Prakrit | Proposed link | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **jān** ‘to know’ | **jānā́ti, CDIAL 5193** (reflex) | Andersen printed p. 150 compares RV/Pali *jānāti* and gives the Ashokan optative *jāneyu/jānevu*. Turner lists Pali *jānāti* and Ashokan imperative/future forms of the same present stem. |
| **likʰ** ‘to write’ | **likháti, CDIAL 11048** (reflex) | Andersen printed p. 167 compares *likh-* and Pali *likhati*, and gives causative *likhāpayatha* with *likhite/likhitaṁ*. Turner lists Pali *likhati* and Ashokan causative/writing forms under *likháti*. |

The Buddhist *saka* contamination correction remains separate.
