These are ordinary inflections of its inherited perfect stem, preserving ö/e alternation and person/gender endings. |
| 4 | **mulo / muli** ‘died’ (2) | **mr̥tá**, CDIAL **10278** | Turner 10278 explicitly gives European Romani mulo under mr̥tá and other Indo-Aryan gendered 'died' outcomes. Three held records:
- **isipey** ‘to be’: The source defines this auxiliary 'to be' (pp. 141–142), but Turner 9416 lists Romani asom/som as crossing with ásti rather than supplying a direct is- branch. The specific source stem and possible Persian contact require review.
- **jeyipey** ‘to go’: The source calls this an irregular infinitive of džā- 'go' (p. 142), but the precise historical present-stem etymon is not established by Turner's separate gatá past entry 4008. It should not be assigned merely from the past forms.
- **ayili** ‘he came; she came’: The source gives 'he/she came' as another irregular perfect (p. 140); the matching historical branch and whether this is the same stem as present āv- need verification.
