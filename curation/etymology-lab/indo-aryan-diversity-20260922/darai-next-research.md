# Darai continuation research

Pass001 saved 18 records in ten etyma. Full primary articles for the next shortlist are in darai-primary002.json; discovery uses the actual homepage query implementation in darai-search002.json.

- **bagʰ** ‘tiger’: CDIAL 12193 section 1 vyāghra; Pk vaggha, Nepali/Maithili bāgh. Ready for a decisions batch after rechecking current overlay.
- **bʰok** ‘hunger’: CDIAL 9286 bubhukṣā, via the reduced bhukkhā family; Nepali bhok(h), Assamese bhok. Note Turner questions the exact intermediate reconstruction.
- **kaṭʰi** ‘wood’: CDIAL 3120 kāṣṭha includes kāṣṭhikā and Hindi/Sindhi kāṭhī wood. Check how the final i is represented by the existing etymon structure before saving.
- **ʣanike** ‘to know’: CDIAL 5193 jānāti, compare Nepali jānnu and Maithili jānab. Verify source infinitive -ike before treating the ending as ordinary inflection.
- **til** ‘sesame’: do not choose 5827 over 5827-3 solely by vowel length. Dhakal p. 42 says length is noncontrastive, while Turner separates Maithili/Bihari tīl under *tilla from Nepali til under tila. Requires regional history.
- **bʰar** ‘load’: 9393 bhara is a candidate, but compare bhāra before assignment because Darai does not preserve vowel length contrast.
- **ãkʰi**, **bãsi**, **beŋgo**, **goḍ** still need broadened homepage spelling queries and exact parent resolution. Zero narrow-search hits are not negative etymological evidence.

Two stone records are held in pass001-decisions.json because the family's neighboring forms include loans; no immediate Darai donor has been established.
