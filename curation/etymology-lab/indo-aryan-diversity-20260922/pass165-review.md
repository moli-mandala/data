Two still-unlinked Ashokan glossary records, two proposed variant rows. The printed dictionary pages 162–163 were visually checked against the scan. The exact installed source keys and the full Turner 9942 article are retained in `andersen-primary165.json`.

The common source parent already has accepted rank-1 compiled ancestry to Turner 9942 *mahātman*, whose full article includes Pali *mahattā* ‘great-souled’. These source-exact cross-references are recorded as variant edges, preserving their inscriptional forms without treating either as a fresh direct reflex.

**Mahatata** ‘greatness’ is held: Andersen compares Sanskrit *mahātmatva-* and Pali *mahantatā-* and cross-references *maha-/mahapta-*, while Turner 9933 *mahattva* is only suggestive for its precise formation. **Mahāpa** is also held because its printed “see mahāpakāraṇa-” target has unresolved ancestry.
