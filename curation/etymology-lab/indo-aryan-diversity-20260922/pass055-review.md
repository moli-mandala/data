# Tamil Nadu: remaining basic numerals

Eight reflex records across five families; standing autonomous authorization. Source images checked at printed pp. 868–871 (PDF pp. 888–891).

| # | Survey forms and meaning | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ek** ‘one’ (1) | **\*ēkka**, CDIAL **2462-2** | Turner2462 section2 explicitly gives Marathi and Gujarati ek, retaining k through MIA ekka. Katkari ek selects this branch; Turner allows emphatic doubling or a MIA Sanskrit loan as the origin of the reinforced stem. |
| 2 | **sat / sə̄tu** ‘seven’ (2) | **sapta**, CDIAL **13139** | Turner13139 gives Marathi/Gujarati sāt and Konkani sāta through MIA satta. Katkari sat and Saurashtra sə̄tu fit the regional sat- family; the reported central long vowel and final u are retained, as in the survey’s other u-final numerals. |
| 3 | **ãṭ** ‘eight’ (1) | **aṣṭā**, CDIAL **941** | Turner941 gives Marathi/Gujarati āṭh, Bengali āṭ and nasalised aṣ forms elsewhere in the family. Katkari ãṭ fits the eight family; its nasal vowel and unaspirated retroflex stop are preserved, without claiming that Turner prints this exact Katkari spelling. |
| 4 | **nɔo / nəvvu** ‘nine’ (2) | **nava²**, CDIAL **6984** | Turner6984 gives Marathi nav/naū, Gujarati and Konkani nav, and regional nao/nɔu forms. Katkari nɔo matches the contracted type and Saurashtra nəvvu retains v; source gemination, central vowel and final u remain unchanged. |
| 5 | **dəs / dəssu** ‘ten’ (2) | **daśa**, CDIAL **6227** | Turner6227 gives MIA dasa and Gujarati/Marathi das beside Marathi dahā. Both source forms fit the s-retaining ten family, with central vowels and Saurashtra ss/u preserved rather than normalised. |
