# Weinreich Ḍomaakí: moon forms

Three source-defined moon forms saved under standing autonomous authorization. The [Weinreich article](https://repo.bibliothek.uni-halle.de/bitstream/1981185920/94072/1/weinreich_2008_domaaki.pdf), printed p. 311 §8.22, separates two Nager forms from one Hunza form. The source audit and installed records preserve these distinctions. Jambu homepage search found the CDIAL family; the complete Turner article is cached in `domaaki-primary001.json`.

| # | Ḍomaakí form | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ʦ̣onʦ̣** ‘moon’, Nager | **candrá**, CDIAL **4661**, §2 | Turner explicitly cites Ḍomaakí *ċōnc̣* ‘moon’, from a Dardic *ċanJ̣* branch of *candrá*. Weinreich gives this Nager nominative separately and records its genitive *č̣anẓéi*. |
| 2 | **ʦ̣ónʦ̣a** ‘moon’, Nager | **candrá**, CDIAL **4661**, §2 | Weinreich gives this as a second Nager nominative with separate genitive and inessive forms; it shares the same inherited moon stem while retaining its source-defined ending. |
| 3 | **ʦonʦ̣** ‘moon’, Hunza | **candrá**, CDIAL **4661**, §2 | Weinreich separately records the Hunza nominative *ċonč̣* and its genitive *ċanẓéi*. Turner’s explicit Ḍomaakí comparator supports the family across both varieties. |

Three accepted `reflex` rows affect three records. CDIAL 4661 is installed as one article node; its moon subsection is specified in the notes. **tíir** ‘arrow’ remains held because Weinreich identifies Persian/Urdu comparanda without resolving the immediate donor. **khakaí** ‘walnut’ has Shina and Burushaski comparanda of uncertain direction, while Turner 48 pertains to a different Domaaki walnut form. **bras** ‘rice’ matches Burushaski, whereas Turner 12233 concerns a different Nager rice form. Weinreich cautions that his comparative references help orient the data and do not by themselves establish immediate origins.
