# Andersen (1990): two verbal source heads

Two still-unlinked glossary heads, two proposed reflex rows. Andersen’s printed pages 139 and 148 were visually checked against the scan, as were full Turner articles 977 and 3955, retained in `andersen-primary178.json`.

| Source head | Turner parent | Direct evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **as** ‘to exist, to be’ | **977** *ásti* | Andersen explicitly compares RV *asti* and Pali *atthi* and discusses the first-person *sumi*. Turner 977 gives √*as*, Pali *atthi*, Ashokan *asti/athi*, and first-person *asmi*. The source head is a root-stem label for this inherited verbal family. |
| **gam** ‘to go’ | **3955** *gácchati* | Andersen explicitly compares RV *gam-* (*gacchati*) and Pali *gacchati*. Turner 3955 marks √*gam* and cites Pali *gacchati* and Ashokan *gacheyaṁ/gachema/gachati*. This links the source’s root-stem label, not an alleged finite inscriptional form *gam*. |
