# Darai: bring, change, break and ordinals

Seven source-backed reflex records across five lexical families.

| # | Darai form | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **anike** ‘to bring’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 49]) | **ā́nayati**, CDIAL **1174** | Dhakal p. 49 prints /anike/ “to bring”. Turner1174 section 1 gives Pali āneti “brings, fetches”, Bhojpuri ānal, Old Awadhi ānab and Hindi ānnā. The Darai -ike is the ordinary infinitive suffix, illustrated in the source morphology. The nonlong a is consistent with the source statement that vowel length is not distinctive. |
| 2 | **pʰerike** ‘to change’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 45]) | ***phērayati**, CDIAL **9078-2** | Dhakal pp. 45 and 51 print /pʰerike/ “to change” in minimal pairs. Turner9078 section 2 is transitive *phērayati; it specifically lists Nepali phernu “to change” and Old Awadhi pheraï “turns, changes”. This contrasts with the separate Darai /pʰorike/ “to break”. |
