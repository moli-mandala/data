# Darai: sesame, stone, come, bird, and together

Six source-backed reflex records across five etyma.

| # | Darai form | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **til** ‘sesame’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 44]) | **tilá**, CDIAL **5827** | Dhakal p. 44 prints /til/ “sesame” in the /i/ versus /e/ minimal pair. Turner5827 section 1 gives Pali tila and Prakrit tila, with Nepali and Bengali til “sesamum seed”; this is the common seed noun, not the homonymous mole term at 5828. |
| 2 | **til** ‘sesame’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 44]) | **tilá**, CDIAL **5827** | Dhakal p. 44 repeats /til/ “sesame” in Table 3.1 for medial /i/. Turner5827 section 1 gives Nepali, Bengali and Assamese til “sesamum seed,” alongside Pali and Prakrit tila. The distinct installed example record has the same lexical parent. |
| 3 | **patʰər** ‘stone’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 53]) | **prastará**, CDIAL **8857** | Dhakal p. 53 prints /patʰər/ “stone.” Turner8857 explicitly gives Maithili pathar, Bengali pāthar and Oriya pathara “stone,” with corresponding Pali and Prakrit patthara; the Darai stem and meaning match this branch. |
| 4 | **aike** ‘to come’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 48]) | **āpayati**, CDIAL **1200** | Dhakal p. 48 prints /aike/ “to come,” where -ike is the Darai infinitive ending. Turner1200 lists Prakrit āvēi/āvaï “comes,” Nepali āunu, Oriya āibā and Old Maithili āvaï; the Darai ai- stem matches this widespread “come” family. |
| 5 | **ʦərəi** ‘bird’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 54]) | **caṭaka**, CDIAL **4571** | Dhakal p. 54 prints /ʦərəi/ “bird.” Turner4571 section 1 gives Oriya caṛāi and Bengali caṛāi “bird,” with Nepali caro and an eastern -āka extension. The Darai form matches the eastern bird noun rather than section 2 ciṛ- “sparrow.” |
| 6 | **satʰ** ‘together, accompany’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 54]) | **sā́rtha**, CDIAL **13364** | Dhakal p. 54 prints /satʰ/ “together, accompany.” Turner13364 section 3 gives Nepali sātha, Bengali sāthe, Oriya sātha and Maithili sāth in the “with/in company” sense; Darai satʰ continues that adverbial/postpositional branch. |
