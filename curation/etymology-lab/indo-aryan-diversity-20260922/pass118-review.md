# Darai: marriage, big, and first

Three source-backed reflex records across three etyma.

| # | Darai form | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **byaha** ‘marriage’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 57]) | **vivāhá**, CDIAL **11920** | Dhakal p. 57 prints /byaha/ “marriage” as an example of the initial by- cluster. Turner11920 gives Sanskrit vivāhá, Prakrit viāha, Maithili biāh, Bihari biyāh and Hindi byāh “marriage”; the Darai byah- form matches this broad Indo-Aryan continuation. |
| 2 | **bəḍkə** ‘big’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 66]) | ***vaḍrakk-**, CDIAL **11225-2** | Dhakal p. 66 prints /bəḍkə/ “big.” Turner11225 gives Prakrit vaḍḍa “big” and Old Awadhi baṛkā “big”; the installed Turner-derived 11225-2 is specifically the inferred -kk- extension of vaḍra corresponding to the Darai -ḍk- adjectival stem. |
| 3 | **pəhele** ‘first’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 69]) | ***prathilla-**, CDIAL **8652-2** | Dhakal p. 69 prints /pəhele/ “first.” Turner8652 section 2 gives Prakrit pahilla-, Kumaoni pailo, Nepali pailo, Oriya pahila and Hindi pahilā “first”; the Darai pəhel- form matches this -ll- branch rather than section 1 *prathila. |
