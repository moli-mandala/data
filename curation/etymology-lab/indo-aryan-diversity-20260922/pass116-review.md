# Zargari: go and throw

Two source-checked reflex records in two historical families. Standing autonomous authorization. Original PDF printed pp. 128–129, 142, glyph audit and imported keys, homepage searches and full CDIAL articles/addenda were checked. No lexical import or rebuild.

| # | Zargari form and meaning | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **jeyipey** ‘to go’ | **yā́ti**, CDIAL **10452** | Turner 10452 explicitly lists European Romani ǰal 'goes' from yā́ti. The source specifically identifies Zargari džejipej 'to go' as an irregular infinitive from džā- (§3.17.3.2, p. 142), distinguishing this present stem from the gatá-derived irregular past linked in pass100. The infinitive formation is source morphology, not a separate etymon. |
| 2 | **cuv** ‘throw!’ | **chupáti (crossed with kṣipáti)**, CDIAL **5055** | Turner 5055 explicitly lists European Romani čiv- 'to throw, put' in its section crossed with kṣipáti. The source prints Zargari čuv [čuw] 'Throw!' in §2.3.20 (p. 128), while giving final /v/ > [w]. The i/u vowel difference is retained as a local form, not explained by an invented sound rule. This short imperative is assigned to the attested Romani branch with Turner's crossing retained in the note, not to a bare kṣipáti node. |

- **āssayipey** ‘to laugh’: Turner 14048 gives European Romani asel 'laughs', and source p. 127 prints āssajipej 'to laugh'; the extra -aj- stem formation and whether it reflects a causative or local inflection remain unresolved.
- **ayili** ‘he came; she came’: The source prints the irregular past 'came' (p. 140), but the exact āv-/ay- branch and its relationship to the inherited present stem remain unresolved; do not transfer Turner's go etymon to it.
