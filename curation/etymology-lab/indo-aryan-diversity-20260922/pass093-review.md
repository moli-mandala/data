# Darai: grow, meet, bury, melt and search

Five source-backed reflex records across five etyma.

| # | Darai form | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ubʣike** ‘to grow’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 58]) | **útpadyatē**, CDIAL **1814** | Dhakal p. 58 prints /ubdzike/ “to grow.” Turner1814 section 1 explicitly gives Nepali ubjanu, Oriya upajibā, Bihari and Maithili upajab “to grow”; the Darai ubʣ- cluster fits the regional ubj-/upaj- stem. -ike is the ordinary infinitive. |
| 2 | **bʰeṭlə** ‘met’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 59]) | ***bhēṭṭ-**, CDIAL **9490-4** | Dhakal p. 59 prints /bʰeṭlə/ “met.” Turner9490 section 4 lists Nepali bheṭnu, Maithili bheṭab and Oriya bheṭibā “to meet,” whereas sections 1–3 concern other bhiṭ-/bhēṭ- developments. Darai -lə is the source-described past suffix, so this is an inflected reflex of the section-4 stem. |
| 3 | **gaḍlarə** ‘bury’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 59]) | ***gaḍḍ-**, CDIAL **3979** | Dhakal p. 59 prints /gaḍlarə/ “bury-PROS”; the installed gloss shortens it to “bury.” Turner3979 gives Nepali gāṛnu, Bengali gāṛā, Oriya gāṛibā and Maithili gāṛab “bury.” The prospective suffix is source morphology, not a separate etymon. |
| 4 | **pəglike** ‘to melt’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 60]) | **pragalati**, CDIAL **8468** | Dhakal p. 60 prints /pəglike/ “to melt.” Turner8468 section 1 gives Nepali paglanu, Kumaoni pagalṇo and West Pahari pɔgəḷno “to melt”; these match the Darai pagl- stem and intransitive sense, rather than section 3 transitive pagālnu. |
| 5 | **kʰoʣlə** ‘searched’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 68]) | ***khōjjati**, CDIAL **3929-2** | Dhakal p. 68 prints /kʰodzlə/ “searched.” Turner3929 section 2 gives Nepali khojnu, Bengali khojā, Oriya khojibā and Maithili khōjab “to search”; section 1 is the noun “footprint.” Darai -lə is the source-described past suffix, leaving the matching khōj- stem. |
