- **baḍḍa** ‘morning; big’: One compiled record combines the source's ‘morning’ and ‘big’ responses. Turner 11225 clearly supports big, but assigning the whole mixed-sense record would also cover morning without resolving it.
- **manukʰ** ‘man’: Turner 9828 and 10049 have competing manuṣya/mānuṣa continuations and extensions; the -kʰ outcome needs a closer Dogri comparison before branch choice.
- **go** ‘cow’: Turner divides cow/ox gō and gau forms among several branches; the Dogri monosyllable does not choose among 4093, 4147 and 4255.
- **suraj** ‘sun’: The Dogri sun form contains final -j, while Turner 13574 sections distinguish sūra, sūrya and sūriya; no exact local derivation has been verified.
