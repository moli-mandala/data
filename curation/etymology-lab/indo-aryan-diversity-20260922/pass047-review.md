# Grangali survey: hearing, eating and household vocabulary

Eight saved reflex records across five families; standing autonomous authorization.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ṣū́r / śuni** ‘hear’ (2 records) | **śṛṇoti**, CDIAL **12598** | Buddruss p28 item50 explicitly derives Grangali ṣūr from *śrun-, compares the Ashokan śruṇeyu type and insists on the source retroflex sibilant. | 2 | **xo / xuy** ‘eat’ (2 records) | **khādati**, CDIAL **3865** | Buddruss p31 item87 explicitly identifies Grangali xo/xoa and Ningalam xuy as continuations of khāda-. | 3 | **gē** ‘house’ (1 records) | **geha**, CDIAL **4251** | Buddruss p31 item86 explicitly identifies the Grangali/Ningalam house word with Sanskrit geha and corrects an alleged Waigali comparison. | 4 | **arṣə́** ‘mirror’ (1 records) | **ādarśa**, CDIAL **1143** | Buddruss p31 item88 explicitly derives Grangali arṣə from Middle Indo-Aryan *ādariṣa and compares Prakrit ādarisa and Shumashti reṣə.
