# Darai: hide and thick

Three source-backed reflex records across two etyma.

| # | Darai form | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **lokike** ‘to hide’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 56]) | **lúpyatē / *lukka- replacement**, CDIAL **11083** | Dhakal p. 56 contrasts /lo.ki.ke/ “to hide” with /ro.ki.ke/ “to stop.” Turner11083 section 3 lists the MIA *lukka- replacement, Nepali luknu and Oriya lukibā “hide,” plus Old Awadhi lokāvaï with the same o-vowel as Darai. The Darai lo- stem belongs to the hide family. |
| 2 | **baklə** ‘thick’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 59]) | **valkala**, CDIAL **11418** | Dhakal p. 59 prints /bak.lə/ “thick.” Turner11418 lists Nepali and Kumaoni bāklo “thick, coarse” and Western Pahari bāklu, a semantic extension of “bark”; Darai vowel length is noncontrastive, and the bakl- shape is close. |
| 3 | **baklə** ‘thick’ (dhakal2011darai[p. 68]) | **valkala**, CDIAL **11418** | Dhakal p. 68 independently repeats /bak.lə/ “thick.” Turner11418 directly gives Nepali bāklo and Kumaoni bāklo “thick, coarse,” matching the Darai adjective. |
