| 1 | **gar** ‘house’ (1) | **ghara**, CDIAL **4428** | Turner4428 gives Kashmiri gara, Poguli gar and neighbouring Pahari ghar house. | 4 | **nūn / lūn** ‘salt’ (2) | **lavaṇa**, CDIAL **10978** | Turner10978 gives Kashmiri nūn/lun and regional lūṇ/nūṇ salt through MIA loṇa/lūṇa. Poguli nūn and Siraji lūn fit these established nasal/lateral initial variants. Source dental n and vowel length remain unchanged. |
