# Danuwar: numerals one to ten

Fifty saved reflex records across ten numeral families; standing autonomous authorization.

| # | Danuwar forms and meaning | Proposed etymology | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ek** ‘one’ (5 records) | ***ekka**, CDIAL **2462-2** | Turner2462 section2 explicitly places Nepali and Maithili ek under the MIA ekka replacement, not the vowel-only descendants of section1 eka. The source retained k matches that later branch; Turner’s alternative explanations of the MIA gemination remain open. |
| 2 | **dui** ‘two’ (5 records) | **dva**, CDIAL **6648** | Turner6648 gives Nepali, Maithili and Bhojpuri dui two, alongside older duvi/duve forms. All five source dui responses fit this regional diphthongal continuation. |
| 3 | **tin** ‘three’ (5 records) | **trīṇi**, CDIAL **5994-3** | Turner5994 section3 gives Nepali tin and Maithili tini through MIA tiṇṇi. The nasal-final source forms select trīṇi rather than the trayas/trāyas branches with preserved or differently developed r. |
| 4 | **cār** ‘four’ (5 records) | **catvāri**, CDIAL **4655-2** | Turner4655 section2 explicitly gives Nepali cār and eastern cāri, via MIA *cayāri and Apabhramsha cāri. The section’s special numeral phonology is retained; the source forms are not assigned to the caur branch. |
| 5 | **pā̃c** ‘five’ (5 records) | **pañca**, CDIAL **7655** | Turner7655 directly gives Nepali, Maithili and Hindi pā̃c five. The source nasal long vowel and affricate correspond to the regional reduction of pañca. |
| 6 | **cha / chau** ‘six’ (5 records) | ***kṣaṭ / *kṣvaṭ**, CDIAL **12803-3** | Turner12803 section3 includes Nepali cha, Maithili chao and the addendum’s J. chau. Both source cha and chau therefore belong to this branch, distinct from section1 ṣaṣ and section2 *ṣuvaṭ. |
| 7 | **sāt / sāt̺** ‘seven’ (5 records) | **sapta**, CDIAL **13139** | Turner13139 gives Nepali, Maithili and Bhojpuri sāt through MIA satta. Both unmarked and explicitly dental source t forms are preserved. |
| 8 | **āt̺h** ‘eight’ (5 records) | **aṣṭā / aṣṭau**, CDIAL **941** | Turner941 gives Nepali, Maithili and Bhojpuri āṭh through MIA aṭṭha. The survey marks the stop dental in āt̺h; retain that source articulation instead of silently replacing it with Turner’s retroflex sign. This is the regional numeral family assignment. |
| 9 | **nau** ‘nine’ (5 records) | **nava²**, CDIAL **6984** | Turner6984 directly gives Nepali and Maithili nau nine through MIA nava. The diphthong fits the numeral entry, not the homonymous new adjective. |
| 10 | **das / d̺as** ‘ten’ (5 records) | **daśa**, CDIAL **6227** | Turner6227 gives Nepali, Bihari and Maithili das ten through MIA dasa. The source dental marking on d is retained where supplied; no aspiration or sibilant normalization is introduced. |
