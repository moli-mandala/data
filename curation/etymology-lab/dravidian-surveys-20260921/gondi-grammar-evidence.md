# Gondi morphology evidence

Primary source: Benny Kurian, Mary B. Kurian and Jo Penny, [Southern Gondi grammar](https://www.gondwana.in/sites/www.gondwana.in/files/pdf-files/SGondi%20%28Aheri%29%20Grammar%20final%202000%20%28Unicode%29.pdf). PDF downloaded read-only to `/tmp/gondi-aheri-grammar.pdf`; SHA256 a4d6453796b85f9e4ed6d93999d6476c027c44d87add3385b8bd7ba861e73039.

Printed pp.24–25 and27–28 were visually checked against rendered PDF pages31–32 and34–35. The former distinguish participle-plus-man auxiliary constructions; the latter identify -maḍ/-uḍu verbal nouns. Accordingly, clear auxiliary responses receive ordered root + be components, while transparent gerunds remain ordinary root reflexes. These are comparative applications to specified survey forms; ambiguous roots, extra material and pronoun-led clauses are excluded. No source was ingested and no new dictionary node or bibliographic record was created.
