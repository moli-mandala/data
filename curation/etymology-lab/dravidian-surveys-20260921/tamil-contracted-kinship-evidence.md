# Contracted person nouns: primary check

Source: *தமிழ் 11* (Tamil, year 11), Sri Lanka Educational Publications Department textbook; publisher identified in the preface, PDF p.5. Edition/year not established from inspected front matter. Public scan: https://noolaham.net/project/795/79443/79443.pdf .

Visually inspected printed pp.112 and115 (PDF pp.120 and123). In its colloquial/standard correspondence table, p.112 pairs ஆம்பிளை with ஆண்பிள்ளை, and p.115 pairs பொம்பிளை with பெண்பிள்ளை. These are evidence for contracted male/female + child formations; the textbook’s prescriptive headings are not adopted as judgments of survey usage.

The constituents are independently attested by full DEDR399 (āṇ male),4395 (peṇ female),4198 (piḷḷai child). Component proposals compare the related survey contractions; they do not determine whether any particular survey variety borrowed the whole formation from Tamil. Source forms, vowel variants and glosses remain unchanged. Extended Muthuvan forms with final āḷ are left for separate analysis.
