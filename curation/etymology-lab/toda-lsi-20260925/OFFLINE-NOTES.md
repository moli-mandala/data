# LSI Toda first-50 offline candidate preparation

These are provisional candidate articles, not proposals. `discovery-001-requests.json` contains the first 50 exact forms for the required homepage `fetchLemmaList` search; that read-only query has not yet run. `survey-inventory.json` and `source-record-evidence.json` preserve all 154 residual compiled records and their original source rows.

Full DEDR articles inspected locally suggest numerals under 2826 (five, To. `üζ`), 784 (eight, To. `öṭ`), 1025 (nine, To. `wïnboQ`), 990 (one-family forms but no direct `vadd`), 3918 (ten, To. `pot`), 474 (two, To. `e·ḍ`), and 3655 (four, To. `no·ng`). The survey's `anpaθ` nine and `nānk` four are closer in shape to Tamil/Kannada forms within their DEDR articles than to the quoted Toda reflexes; their family-level interpretation will need an explicit qualification. The `vadd` one form has no secure direct Toda form yet.

Other promising family articles: 399 (To. `o·ḷ` man vs survey `āl`), 1873 (To. `ku·x` girl/daughter), 4616 (To. `mox` child/son/daughter), 5152 (To. `o·ḍ` goat vs survey `ādu`), 1017 (To. `wiḷy` good vs survey `ulle`), 1006 (To. `walo(Q)` bad vs survey `holāivi`), 5086 (To. `me·l` up/high), 5128 (To. dialect `mok` up), and 516 (To. `eṟk` down). The survey `kadare` horse matches Kannada `kudare/kadare` more closely than DEDR 1711 Toda `kïQïr`; `hōri` bull likewise looks closer to Kannada than a Toda reflex. Their possible donor path needs care.

Kin terms `eyan/enin` father, `av` mother, `ennon etud/kinnud` brother, `akka` and `enor vøt kux` sister, plus `kuigʸō`/`kaṭṭvodi kux` wife are not securely matched yet. Several two- and three-word responses will need each lexical part justified or remain held. The original source's phonemic `+` markers confirm token boundaries; no compound has been silently reduced to its final noun.

The later source rows 51–154 have been inventoried offline as well. Direct Toda article evidence for their paradigms includes DEDR 5160 To. `o·n (en-)` I/my, 3684 `ni· (nïn-)` thou/thy, 3688 `nïm` you/your, 5154 `em` exclusive we and `om` inclusive we, 1 demonstrative `aQ` and plurals, 480 `ïr-` sit/remain/live, 4534 `pïy- (pïs-)` beat, and 4572 `pï·x-` go. Other likely verbs are 5270 To. `po·r-` come, 3675 `nïl-` stand, 2053 `kwïṛ-` give to a third person, and 3098 `to·r-` give to a first or second person. This gives a reason to keep the survey's two distinct “Give” rows separate. DEDR 2426 quotes To. `soy-` die, but the survey's `keṭṭ fo` is not a direct match. DEDR 1041 gives To. `wï·ḍ-` run, unlike survey `vōr`.

The final material terms may be productive: DEDR 1278 quotes To. `kabïn` iron, 5496 To. `piḷy` silver, 5152 To. `o·ḍ` goat (possibly relevant to “shepherd”), and 333 To. affirmative forms. Later homepage searches and full article checks remain required before decisions.
