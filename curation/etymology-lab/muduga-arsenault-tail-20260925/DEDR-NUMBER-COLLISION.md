# DEDR 2835/2885 dove collision — research hold

Arsenault and Abraham (2022, preprint §3.2) explicitly compare Muduga **cōṟe** ‘dove’ with Kannada (Gowda) **cōrE** ‘pigeon’ and cite **DEDR 2885**. The current Jambu DEDR index has no `d2885` node. Its cached article audit instead contains **two different articles with Entry_ID `d2835`**:

- Printed p. 246: `2835 Ta. coṭṭu ... to fall in drops`, with the drip family and the node's displayed reconstruction `*coṭ-`.
- Printed p. 250: `2835 Ko. co·r dove ... Ka. (Gowda) cōrE pigeon`, a distinct bird family. The paper's number 2885 fits the page sequence and is likely the intended printed number; the cached `2835` label is suspect.

This is not a whole-article-head mismatch. It is an apparent **entry-number collision joining two separate articles**. Linking the bird to `d2835` would conflate unrelated families, so Muduga **cōṟe** remains a research hold pending a separate DEDR source audit/correction. The unrelated Muduga **cōre** ‘blood’ is already under DEDR 2883 and must not be used as a fallback. No source data or accepted etymology was edited here.

Evidence retained in [`primary-evidence.json`](primary-evidence.json): both complete cached articles and their page numbers. The source's own printed comparison is in `data/data/other/forms/raw_data/muduga_2022/audit.jsonl`, key `muduga2022:s3.2-dove`.
