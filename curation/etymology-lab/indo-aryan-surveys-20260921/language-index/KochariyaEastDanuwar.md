## Explicit holds

- **cʰāni** ‘roof’ (`f_vtyrxcaxyrjc6`): Roof: channa 4989 vs \*channi 4992 is not distinguished by the local i-ending. Hold for a precise morphological history.
- **jʰarnā** ‘firewood’ (`f_s2mbbe7d4zf2w`): Possible wood/fuel family, but the recorded consonant difference needs direct dialect evidence.
- **jʰarlo** ‘come down’ (`f_7fz3yjz2tp64y`): CDIAL 5328 explicitly allows Nepali jharnu to derive either from \*jhaṭati or \*jharati 5346. The survey does not choose between those histories; long-vowel transitive-looking variants add uncertainty.
