## Explicit holds

- **oda** ‘wet’ (`f_vs67opk4b2m2k`): Turner 2554 gives \*odra/\*audra but explicitly allows all these wet forms to come instead from odman, especially because of the Hindi/Oriya nouns. Hold the competing parent analysis.
- **sos** ‘was thirsty’ (`f_ku4oafozr6plg`): Turner śoṣa explicitly supports Oriya sosa thirst, but the cached CDIAL duplicates number 12642 with śośucat shining. Existing śoṣa node 12642-2 is incorrectly attached to shining node 12642 in the current graph. Hold these responses until that parent identity/ancestry is reviewed; no source reparse or ancestry repair in this run.
- **usaso** ‘light’ (`f_tdkyaizytf26s`): Oriya usāsa relief/ease under CDIAL 1868 competes with the causative adjective uśāsiā lighter under 1866-2. The short survey adjective does not safely distinguish these nominal and causative histories; defer rather than selecting a branch by meaning alone.
