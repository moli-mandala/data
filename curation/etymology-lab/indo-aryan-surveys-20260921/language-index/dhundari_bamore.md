## Explicit holds

- **gābɔ** ‘cloth’ (`f_wcpna3fnh7su6`): Cloth gābā/gabbā requires separating gabba filling (CDIAL 4023) from garbha interior (4055), whose Marathi gābhā also denotes cloth under a turban in Molesworth and Aryabhushan. The meanings overlap but do not establish which history explains the unaspirated Rajasthan survey family; hold pending regional lexical evidence.
- **bāste** ‘fire’ (`f_6lidoxannvqxs`): The cached Bagri original explicitly lists bast̪e/bast̪i under item 55 fire (printed p. 34), so this is not silently treated as the for postposition. A historical parent for the fire word remains unestablished; do not force the homonymous vāste loan or a bellows etymology.
