## Explicit holds

- **kahā** ‘where’ (`f_mybcksysqayau`): Homepage search and full kuha/kutaḥ articles do not establish the historical derivation of this particular kā̃/kahā̃ formation. Preserve the unresolved locative formation instead of treating initial k and a matching gloss as a direct reflex.
- **dāī** ‘mother’ (`f_7baymndu7z3f4`): The mother form dāi is not safely settled by comparison with Hindi dhāī nurse (6774) or contracted dāi under the elderly-relative family dādda (6261). Both semantic extension and the loss/absence of medial d need regional evidence. Existing graph placement of another Tharu dāi is a discovery aid, not independent proof.
