- **ūḍayde** ‘fly!, it flew’ (`f_doz2uzvunrveu`): User approved 1697-2 for the fly lexical stem. This whole response still requires its auxiliary, paired-answer, causative, or local inflection analysis; no incomplete root-only assignment has been saved.
- **gābā** ‘cloth’ (`f_ey5ybk2k3ofhy`): Cloth gābā/gabbā requires separating gabba filling (CDIAL 4023) from garbha interior (4055), whose Marathi gābhā also denotes cloth under a turban in Molesworth and Aryabhushan. The meanings overlap but do not establish which history explains the unaspirated Rajasthan survey family; hold pending regional lexical evidence.
- **kʰāḷi** ‘river’ (`f_l6fgcrjnbucwa`): Turner gives the creek/river family under both \*khaḍḍa (3790) and \*khāḍa (3860), explicitly cross-listing Marathi khāḍī as an alternative. The survey form does not resolve this competing reconstruction.
- **pūraṇ** ‘whole’ (`f_vz4bx62wp2qiw`): Whole: nasal-final puron/pūraṇ may involve learned pūrṇa rather than pūra, and eastern puron also raises a possible old/whole confusion with purāṇa. The n-final forms are not automatically folded into the transparent pūrā full family.
- **apūrṇ** ‘broken’ (`f_5p7zatukkutra`): Broken/incomplete: apūrṇ appears learned from apūrṇa (493); adūrā/adūro may represent a different half-full/incomplete formation. Do not force them into simple ardha half without checking the relevant history and donor route.
- **bāstī** ‘fire’ (`f_64nhna3mqxide`): The cached Bagri original explicitly lists bast̪e/bast̪i under item 55 fire (printed p. 34), so this is not silently treated as the for postposition. A historical parent for the fire word remains unestablished; do not force the homonymous vāste loan or a bellows etymology.
