# Old Punjabi muccu: direct lexical evidence

On 21 September 2026, inspected printed p. 243 (PDF p. 278) of Christopher Shackle, *A Guru Nanak Glossary*, second edition, in the existing local PDF:

`/Users/aryamanarora/Documents/Linguistics/Indo-European/Indo-Aryan/NIA/Northwestern/A_GURU_NANAK_GLOSSARY_2ND_EDITION.pdf`

The entry **muccu** is explicitly an adjective meaning abundant/plenty, big/broad, and an adverb meaning very. Its printed etymological bracket is **[10178 *mucca-]**. This verifies both the semantic bridge from CDIAL's lump/heap family to the survey adjective and the actual etymological number; it does not rely on the already imported graph edge. The two following entries are different words: moustaches and lop/cut up, with 10180 and 10179 respectively. They are excluded.

The survey Awankari/Gujari muc/mūc many forms can therefore be grouped with 10178 as a regional comparative family. The evidence does not decide the exact extent of historical diffusion among neighbouring varieties. No source re-extraction, OCR, importer change or rebuild was performed.
