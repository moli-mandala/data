# Platts ant cross-references

Primary dictionary text consulted through Rekhta’s explicitly labelled Platts section, 2026-09-21.

- [cimṭā, cimṭī](https://www.rekhta.org/urdudictionary?keyword=chimtaa&lang=ur): pincers/tongs, with cimṭī also explicitly denoting an ant. This substantiates the insect sense absent from CDIAL 4822’s comparanda.
- [cīṅṭī](https://www.rekhta.org/urdudictionary?keyword=chii.ntii): diminutive of cīṅṭā; the latter cross-refers cimṭā and cimaṭnā. A separate cūṅṭī etymology in the same search result invokes Prakrit cuṇṭiā/Sanskrit cuṇṭa.
- [cyūṅṭī](https://www.rekhta.org/urdudictionary?keyword=chyuu.nte): documents the ciuṅṭī variants but does not resolve the alternative root histories by itself.

Pass040 is therefore restricted to transparent cimṭ-/cimt- forms. The broader nasalized dental and u-vowel series remain unassigned. No new lexical nodes are added.
