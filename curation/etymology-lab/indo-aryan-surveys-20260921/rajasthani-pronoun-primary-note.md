# Rajasthani pronoun comparison

Grierson’s historical table in Encyclopaedia Britannica 1911, XII, printed p. 712 explicitly gives Apabhraṃśa tumhē → Rajasthani thē/tamē and oblique tumhahã → thā̃/tamā̃. It also gives ēho → yō and plural ēi → ē/yē. Used as supplementary primary grammatical evidence alongside CDIAL 10511 and 2530; no new lexical donor or bibliographic import is made.
