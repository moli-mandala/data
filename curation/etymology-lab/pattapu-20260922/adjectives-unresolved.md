- **nētu** ‘long’: DEDR3738 has Tamil/Malayalam neṭu, Tulu neḍi and Kannada niḍidu; DEDR3692 has Malayalam nīṭu, Tulu nīṭa/nīṭu and Tamil nīṭu. Both articles cross-reference each other. Pattapu's long ē is not an exact match to either quoted basic formation. A local paradigm or comparative source is needed to distinguish the short-vowel neṭu family from the long-vowel nīḷ/nīṭu family. Do not confuse this record with nēt̪u ‘yesterday’, proposed under3758.
