**Caveats.** The census donor nodes (#31, #33, #35) are the only Nepali attestations of these words in Jambu. They come from the Himachal Pradesh Nepali census lists, which carry the `uncertain` tag, so they stand in for “Nepali” and are not a Dang-area donor. #32 treats Nepali jaŋgal as a CDIAL 5177 reflex even though Turner cites only Hindi there; an alternative would make it a borrowing from Hindi jaŋgal (`f_l4n3zkflnqjni`).
