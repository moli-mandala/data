# Original Belari sketch verified

[Bhat (1971), The Koraga Language, appendix pp.119–123](https://dnshankarabhat.net/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/Koraga_Language.pdf), PDF pages126–130. All five pages have now been rendered and visually inspected. The earlier cache and DNS failures were resolved by an authorized network download. See visual-source-audit-016.json for the local PDF path, hash and page coverage.

The prose discusses both possible shared native vocabulary and borrowing; it does not settle individual donor routes. Initial Tulu p corresponds to Belari h in the examples, but this does not establish h-prothesis before vowel-initial belly comparanda. Printed p.121 describes the correspondence between Proto-Dravidian ẓ and Belari ḷ.

Printed p.119 gives iḷa ‘get up’, supporting the qualified DEDR851 proposal alongside the compatible installed ‘wake up’ gloss. Printed p.120 has moḷesɨ ‘chilly’ in the household/food noun list. That wording is present in the scan, not an OCR error. The pepper/chilli family DEDR4867 is now a strong lead, but interpreting it as a food noun conflicts with installed ‘cold’: retain a research hold and preserve the source record pending semantic adjudication.

The final pronoun list has one undifferentiated we form; preserve the two installed clusivity records with this limitation. Female singular and plural second-person forms are described as identical. Printed gender distinctions support retaining the separate source records. The paradigms confirm bī cook, mī bathe, māḷ do and būḷ fall. They also show productive plural morphology, without by themselves resolving every held pronoun formation.

Spatial ōḷu ‘there’ resembles Tulu interrogative ōḷu ‘where’ in discovery, but the semantic mismatch remains unresolved. No installed glosses, spellings, article heads or graph rows were changed.
