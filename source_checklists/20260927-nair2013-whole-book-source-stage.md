# Nair 2013 whole-book source-stage handoff

All thirteen chapters of Ravi Sankar S. Nair's *Tribal Languages of Kerala*,
*Language in India* 13:7 (July 2013), have reviewed canonical source files.
The published 58-page book is accounted: front matter pp. 1–3 is documentary;
the contiguous chapter ranges cover pp. 4–58. This closes the published book's
source stage, not the broader unfinished research project mentioned in its preface.
Paniya is expressly omitted by the author and is not an extraction gap.

through the registered base/lect metadata where justified. Historical site
coordinates and modern language identifiers are not guessed for unresolved
source labels. Auxiliary bibliography is attributed as reported unless actually
collated. The shared PDF remains local review evidence: no explicit reuse licence
was established, and only attributed linguistic facts and metadata enter the
canonical inputs.
