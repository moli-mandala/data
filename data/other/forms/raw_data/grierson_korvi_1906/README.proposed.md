# Grierson 1906 Korava / Yerukala: whole-source recovery

This package recovers the complete chapter on printed pp. 318–331, its five interlinear specimens, all 241 Korvi (Belgaum) comparison-table prompts including 22 sentences, and explicit Korava comparisons on pp. 414–415, 479 and 623. The original 1906 volume is public domain. `facsimile-provenance-20260926.json` identifies the retained original scans and hashes; DSAL grayscale originals control readings where the binary DjVu loses fine marks.

The whole-source stage contains **990 form rows from 1,237 audited units**: 89 chapter grammar units, 899 aligned specimen units, 241 table prompts, four source-wide comparisons and four separately attested later corrections. Fifteen other-language controls and 20 bound-morphology units remain audit-only. Seven explicit optional-material expansions yield 1,227 emissions before 237 exact repeated attestations are reused with every citation and alias preserved. The 899 editorial specimen units represent 901 physical fragments after two explicit line continuations are joined. All 156 previously installed keys survive.

Canonical identity is Yerukula (`yeru1240`, ISO `yeu`). Source labels remain distinct: Kōrchari of Belgaum (specimens I–II), Korvī of Belgaum (III and the table), Korvī of Jamkhandi State (IV), and Korvāru of Bijapur (V). Shared chapter examples are not assigned an invented locality. Coordinates remain blank. The source's historical classification and grammatical comparisons are attributed source claims, not new modern classification or donor-link assertions.

`Original` retains the literal Roman transcription, including vowel length, nasalization, retroflexion and underbar contrasts. Individual aligned tokens omit surrounding running-text punctuation; whole sentences retain their transcribed punctuation. `Native` and `Phonemic` remain blank because no separate native-script or phonemic layer is supplied. The source-specific profile follows the explicit transliteration key and Jambu house conventions: aspiration digraphs, ch, v/w, ṅ and underlined ts are mapped while unsupported distinctions remain literal. The profile review records every rule's shared-policy validation.

The entire grammar, five specimens and comparison table received second literal readings. A source-wide commentary review preserves grammatical restrictions, historical comparison claims and typed gloss uncertainty. Independent review checked 20 randomly selected units against 11 original images, plus chapter grammar, fine diacritics and the later errata. The final metadata reports preserve all reviewer corrections and exact byte deltas. Multiple table answers alone do not establish variant edges: prompts 86, 87 and 93 retain separate answers without such links. The neuter oṇḍ is also distinct from ort. Explicit phonetic alternatives and optional material retain justified variant links.

Later bound-in *Addenda Minora*, IV, printed p. 18 (Internet Archive image leaf 708), is a separate undated authority. Its four differing Korvi readings have separate records; original 1906 readings remain represented. Three already matching readings retain both citations. The container's date is not assigned to the later sheet, and a possible 1927 identification remains unproven.

Historical bounded inventories, first readings and failed or superseded review artifacts are retained for provenance. The whole builder consumes reviewed source-local JSONL files, with exact regeneration and source-unit accounting checked independently of database construction.

Full CLDF/database generation, compiled identity and graph checks, the full test suite and browser QA are deferred under the user's explicit no-build/no-remote instructions. This is source-stage recovery only; representative app entries require an authorized database refresh.
