# Grierson 1906 Korvi (Belgaum) comparative vocabulary

George A. Grierson, *Linguistic Survey of India*, IV, *Munda and Dravidian Languages* (Calcutta, 1906), [Wikimedia Commons DjVu](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Linguistic_Survey_of_India_Vol_4.djvu), 701 image pages, SHA256 `33e9aaa220db22fcde712705581a69f7edcfc7e20b3e7a09347dc969768020f1`. Wikimedia identifies the original edition as public domain. The full scan is cached locally at `tmp/pdfs/LSI-V4.djvu`; nine cropped source columns, one for each table page, are preserved under `images/`. Printed pp. 646–678 correspond to DjVu pages 666–698 (offset +20).

The source-stage install covers the entire **Korvi (Belgaum)** column: 241 numbered prompts on the nine alternating printed table pages. `full-scope-inventory.tsv` and generated `audit.jsonl` account for every source cell. Of those cells, 153 supply 156 installed form rows in `20260925-grierson-korvi.csv`; three cells have two legible alternatives each. Another 66 cells are transcription holds because at least one glyph cannot be read securely from the available scan. Item 29 contributes its clear second answer `Āga`; its first alternative is held. The final 22 cells are full sentences, outside the lexical attestation scope. The adjacent Kaikadi, Tamil, and other control columns are separate or excluded. Kaikadi has its own complete source package under the same bibliography key.

The old `transcription.tsv` and `comparator-1928.tsv` preserve the 32–79 pilot and its original 20-cell sample audit. The full importer now reads `full-scope-inventory.tsv`; `python import_source.py --check-scan --install` regenerates the complete CSV and audit. It preserves all 39 accepted pilot entry keys. Original-scan rereading corrected five pilot forms: 40 Head `Tali` → `Teli`, 54 Child `Guṇṭi` → `Guṇṭ`, 62 Sun `Pōdu` → `Pōda`, 69 Cow `Āki` → `Ākḷ`, and 71 Cat `Pōni` → `Pūni`. The final lateral-like glyph in 69 is still soft at scan resolution, so the installed `Ākḷ` carries an uncertainty tag. Items 86, 87, and 93 each preserve both legible printed alternatives. No unresolved cell is silently treated as a blank.

The 1906 book calls this lect Korvi (Belgaum); nearby introductory text identifies Korava/Yerukala. [Glottolog's bibliography mapping](https://glottolog.org/resource/reference/id/304344) assigns Korava/Yerukala of Belgaum to [Yerukula](https://glottolog.org/resource/languoid/id/yeru1240), Glottocode `yeru1240` (ISO `yeu`). We use canonical `Yerukula`, preserve the source lect in dialect `korvi_belgaum_lsi1906`, and record Yerukala as an alternate spelling. The historical Belgaum label does not establish speaker coordinates, so language and dialect coordinates remain blank. The existing Jambu clade `S. Dravidian I` is used.

The DjVu lacks a usable text layer. Source pages were rendered with `ddjvu`, cropped to the Korvi column, enlarged 4×, and visually read. Checked-in Tesseract 5 OCR under `ocr/` is sequencing evidence; the original scan controls installed glyphs. `comparison-1928-full.tsv` records a later [Lexibank LSI digitization](https://github.com/lexibank/lsi), commit `ac29409`, CC-BY, of Grierson's **1928** comparative vocabulary. It flags differences but is neither an independent attestation nor authority for overwriting 1906 typography. The independent stratified review in `sample-audit-20260925-full.json` checked 24 accepted cells across eight printed pages and found zero material reading or alignment errors. The older pilot audit in `sample-audit-2026092601.json` checked 20 cells with zero material errors before the five later corrections; the full-column audit supersedes those old readings.

`Original` preserves 1906 Romanization. The sound profile lowercases and applies the Jambu house spelling `ṅ` → `ŋ`, retaining other diacritics without asserting IPA. `Native` and `Phonemic` are blank because the table does not provide them separately. Printed prompts supply stable entry keys and exact page/item citations. These are unlinked attestations: the table makes no explicit etymological or borrowing claim, and co-listed answers are not assigned directional variant links. There were no pre-existing Yerukula forms to deduplicate.

Source-local importer, audit, dated CSV/YAML, sound profile, bibliography, canonical/dialect registration, and focused checks are installed.
