# Nair 2013: complete Adiya chapter, pp. 4–8

The complete five-page chapter is installed at the local source stage: 29
prose/table/paradigm/kinship/bibliography blocks, 174 physical witness slots,
and 171 rows. These comprise 136 explicitly attributed Adiya forms and whole
expressions on the existing Ravula base plus 35 labelled Malayalam/Kannada
comparisons. Three community names remain documentary audit records. This is
one chapter of the shared physical book, not a claim of complete book ingestion.

Source doubled vowels, underdots, tildes, 17 drawn l/n/rr lowlines, anomalous
internal spaces and original marker characters are preserved. Eight reviewed
boundary-marker repairs retain bare a, printed en dashes and actual ASCII
stem hyphens; bound categories do not depend on an invented dash. Native and
Phonemic are blank; `convert: false` is the registered route. Internal p4 word
spacing remains a typed source-typography uncertainty.

Twelve source-explicit slash/free-variation links are installed. Different
address/reference terms, shared kinship relations and cross-language
correspondences do not gain invented links. Four p8 theses are registered as
reported auxiliary authorities only; their original works were not consulted.
All five titles are protected for bibliography formatting. Adiya (Wayanad) is
a source lect under Ravula/ravu1237, with blank dialect Glottocode and
coordinates, rather than a newly inferred languoid.

Independent final review passed a disjoint 20/20 actual-output original-page
sample and all 12 graph endpoints, following full marker-class repair and
retained historical freezes. `post-install-validation.json` records byte-exact
five-target receipt equality, unchanged durable identity bytes and five passing
focused installed-source tests. No OCR transcription was used; native PDF
text served as a locator alongside manual original-page reading.

Use `import_source.py --install` for approved regeneration. It verifies the
approval-pinned inputs and original source, rejects drift, journals interrupted
writes, and returns a verified no-op on a completed install. The first-reading
and candidate scripts are review generators; altering their outputs invalidates
approval until reviewed. `revision1/`, `revision2/` and `revision3/` preserve the
historical freezes. `source-stage/` adds the actual importer command and
canonical provenance without changing approved rows.

Full CLDF/database builds, compiled graph/identity/reference survival, full
suite and browser QA remain deferred under the authorized source-stage scope.
This is not an end-to-end completed Jambu ingestion or production release.
