Tharu Wordlists and Lexical Comparison

 
Contact the Archives
Files:
TharuWordlistComparison.xlsx
Tharu.wsv
Researchers:
Mitchell, Jessica
Eichentopf, Stephanie
Date Created:
2011 - 2013
Extent:
295 lexical items
Description:
This is a wordlist comparison between eight Tharu wordlists: Dangaura and several closely related varieties, Kathariya, and Kochila. This combines data collected on two different surveys. Biodata and audio recordings have been archived with the appropriate survey data and are not included here.
