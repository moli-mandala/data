The fifth text, a Sadri story, called School story, was collected from Delatolli village in Ranchi, 
Jharkhand, during a previous survey (Abraham 1995). This variety of Sadri is said to be the standard 
Sadri variety and geographically closer to Korwa region. The surveyors tried using this story to test 
Sadri comprehension levels among Pahadi Korwa speakers.  
After having tested eight subjects, however, the testing was discontinued. Because the quality of 
the recording was not good and because testees found the story difficult to understand, they seemed 
to lose interest in the test. They showed little patience with the testing procedures and achieved only 
very low scores.7 
It was found that the Sadri variety spoken in the Pahadi Korwa region is different from that of 
Ranchi. Therefore, the researchers decided that the local Sadri variety spoken in the Pahadi Korwa 
region should be tested instead to explore Sadri comprehension levels. For that purpose, Dumortoli 
village, which is situated around five kilometres from Bagicha town in Jashpur district, was chosen 
as a reference point. Here a test called Life story was recorded from a mother tongue speaker of 
Sadri; to validate the test it was also hometown-tested in Dumortoli.
