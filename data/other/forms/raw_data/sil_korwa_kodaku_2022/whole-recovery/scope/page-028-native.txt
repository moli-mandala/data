This local Sadri RTT from Dumortoli was used for Sadri comprehension testing in Rakkaya 
village. This village is situated around twelve kilometres from Sankargarh town in Surguja district. 
The wordlist from this village is the one that shares the highest lexical similarity percentages with 
the other Korwa wordlists. A Pahadi Korwa story (called Job story) was developed in this village and 
used as a practice story before the local Sadri story was played. 
In addition to the local Sadri text, a story in standard Sadri (the Brave Hunter story) was used 
for Sadri comprehension testing. This story had also been collected during  the mentioned, previous 
survey, namely in Kesramal village in Rourkela, Orissa (Abraham 1995:App C-11). This Sadri RTT 
was tested in Kotagohna, a Dihari Korwa settlement, to test comprehension of standard Sadri. The 
Life story in local Sadri was used as a practice story since the Sadri spoken in Kotagohna is similar to 
that spoken in Dumortoli village.

HTT results 
The Pahadi Korwa Job story scored a high average of 94 percent, with a low standard deviation of 7. 
Post-HTT responses suggest that the language used in this text is good and pure with no mixing of 
other languages. 
The local Sadri story achieved a high average score of 97 percent, with low standard deviation 
of 4. These results, together with the post-HTT responses, indicate that the local Sadri RTT is suitable 
for Sadri comprehension testing among Korwa speakers in different locations. All the subjects 
reported that the language used in this story was good and pure Sadri with no mixing of other 
languages. This story was also tested among the Dihari Korwa, which averaged 97%, with a low 
standard deviation of 6. In their post-HTT responses, the subjects stated that this story is in their own 
language. 
The Kodaku Marriage story scored a high average of 97 percent, with a low standard deviation 
of 4. According to the post-HTT responses, that the language used in this story is good and pure with 
no mixing of other languages.
