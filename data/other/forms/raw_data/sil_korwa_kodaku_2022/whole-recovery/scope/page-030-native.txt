5.2 
Dialect area 
The Pahadi Korwa and the Dihari Korwa use different languages as their mother tongues, Korwa and 
Sadri, respectively. 
Korwa and Kodaku are different languages spoken by two different people groups. Korwa is a 
structurally coherent unit with only slight dialectal variations across the three districts of 
Chhattisgarh where it is spoken. Korwas as spoken in the Rakkaya area is suggested as the reference 
variety for language development. Likewise, Kodaku shows no marked dialectal variations across the 
various districts of the states, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, and Uttar Pradesh where it is spoken. Kodaku 
as spoken in Chhattisgarh, ideally in Wadrafnagar village, is recommended for as the reference 
variety for language development. 
5.3 
Bilingualism 
With the acknowledgment of the limitations of using the RTT methodology for bilingualism testing, 
the following conclusions are drawn for the bilingual situation among the Korwa and the Kodaku 
people. 
Among the Korwa: The Pahadi Korwa subjects, mostly male, were able to understand the 
recorded story in local Sadri. Given the limitations of the RTT methodology, however, no conclusions 
can be drawn as to how bilingual these subjects are in Sadri. The Dihari Korwa people speak a local 
variety of Sadri as their mother tongue. The testees understood the recorded text in standard Sadri 
well, maintaining that standard Sadri is only a little different from local Sadri. 
Among the Kodaku: Kodaku people had a low level of understanding of standard Sadri text. 
Most testees claimed, however, high levels of local Sadri comprehension; no testing was conducted to 
verify this claim.
