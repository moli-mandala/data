 
Preface 
The research presented in this report on the sociolinguistic situation of the Korwa and the Kodaku 
people groups was carried out by Gangadhar Behera, Jose P. Mathew, Binzy Joseph George, Liju 
Kuriakose, Sajayan V. Chacko, and Symon George. The survey was carried on with several off-and-on 
field trips, beginning in mid-June 2004 to September 2005. This study was conducted under difficult 
circumstances, without which the study would have been more efficient. Moreover, one of the 
experienced surveyors experienced severe health problems which enhanced the challenges of this 
study. In addition, the team carried out the research without the usual assistance of an experienced 
language assessment consultant. The researchers own great gratitude to all those who contributed to 
this study in different ways, especially the respondents for patiently taking part in the research, for 
giving us their time and hospitality, and for giving us insights into their culture and language.
