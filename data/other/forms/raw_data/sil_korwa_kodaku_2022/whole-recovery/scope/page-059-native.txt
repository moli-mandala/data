After pairs of items on two wordlists had been determined to be phonetically similar or 
dissimilar according to the criteria stated above, the percentage of items judged similar is calculated. 
This procedure is repeated for all linguistic varieties under consideration in the survey. The pair by 
pair counting procedure is greatly facilitated by use of the Wordsurv computer program (Wimbish 
1989). It should be noted that the wordlist data as well as transcribed texts included in the 
subsequent appendices are field transcriptions and have not undergone phonological and 
grammatical analysis. 
Note: Two glosses, (23) ‘urine’ and (24) ‘feces’, were disqualified and removed from the 
wordlists. One gloss, (11) ‘breast’, was replaced with ‘chest’. These words were considered 
inappropriate in most of the elicitation situations.
