# Gaita Koitor / Far Muria pages — analysis only

The CC-BY [numeralbank/channumerals v1.0.1 archive](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15649312), raw download dated 12 February 2019, contains `raw/Muria-Far-Eastern.htm` (27,542 bytes, SHA256 `f0becd593cd828e30869cee95a4bfc76964b8f511e2570eb6617cf1917744d64`) and `raw/Muria-Far-Western.htm` (165,567 bytes, SHA256 `aa0d2ef57967499dabdb19114d0e3f3daf684bc67e3f72bdd834802b52db9035`). Their first numeral tables each have 20 rows × two cells. All **40/40 cell strings are identical**, with the same SHA256 `136ecece2c2b61e510b7a312b3ddbe760079a87baeabaefcdd5433faaeeb7b12` over JSON-serialized cell text. Both credit Thomas Mathew, Gaita Koito Bible translator, 28 January 2019, and identify Gaita Koitor in the prose, but one heading says **Far Eastern Muria** and the other **Far Western Muria**. The Western page adds a longer 1–100 table.

[Glottolog](https://glottolog.org/resource/languoid/id/farw1235) lists Far Western Muria as a spoken L1 (`farw1235`). The current Gondi classification does not supply a matching Far Eastern Muria L1. The duplicated table and conflicting headings do not securely assign the 40 cells to two distinct canonical lects. No CSV/YAML/Bib/registry entry is installed from either page; the expanded Western table also remains held until its source identity can be reconciled. This is a provenance/mapping hold, not a transcription judgment. It is separate from the Riezen 2013 Eastern Muria table (`east2340`).
