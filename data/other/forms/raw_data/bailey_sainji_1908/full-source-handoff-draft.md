# Sainji whole-source recovery — pending independent audit

The complete Sainji chapter is printed pp. 52–56, PDF pages 74–78 in Bailey’s original 1908 book. The chapter boundary was checked against the preceding Inner Siraji and following Kului headings. This is the first part’s pagination, not the later part containing Rambani.

The draft accounts for 241 source units: 237 accepted units produce 270 forms; three general patterns or cross-references and one explicitly different Mandi lect comparison are excluded. Included material comprises all noun/pronoun and verbal paradigms, adverbs/prepositions, locally printed prose examples, 46 glossary lines, 20 numerals, and all 22 translated sentences. All 55 prior source keys survive. No graph relationships are inferred.

The four forms of the unglossed bhīṇ paradigm have intentionally blank lexical glosses and typed gloss uncertainty; the source supplies no English meaning. The plural column contains ellipses, so no unattested plural rows were created. Source stem-plus-ending paradigms and explicit daughter suffix -ī are expanded with their printed stems, with exact patterns retained. Italic vowels are documented by exact span in Notes. Underlining, nasal marks, underdots, diaeresis and the double macron in be͞uhṇi are literal; no speculative phonological conversion is imposed. The profile applies only the existing house w→v and ṅ→ŋ conventions.
