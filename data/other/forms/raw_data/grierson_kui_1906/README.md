# Grierson 1906: Kui

Source: George Abraham Grierson, *Linguistic Survey of India*, volume IV,
“Kui, Kandhi, or Khond,” printed pp. 457–471, plus its complete 241-prompt
Khondmals comparison column and explicitly attributed outside-chapter comparisons.
The original volume is the 1906 Calcutta edition. Later bound-in addenda retain
their separate source identity. Four auxiliary authorities are cited as reported
by Grierson, without claiming independent collation of those works.

The [Commons source record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=File:Linguistic_Survey_of_India_Vol_4.djvu&oldid=923381906)
identifies the scan as public domain (checked 2026-09-27). The cached original
`tmp/pdfs/LSI-V4.djvu` has 701 physical pages and SHA256
`33e9aaa220db22fcde712705581a69f7edcfc7e20b3e7a09347dc969768020f1`.
The cached Nordic keyed OCR was useful for discovery but omits tables and
interlinear material; original images and individual alternate-scan leaves were
therefore reviewed. Source-local review files preserve raw readings, corrections,
locators and source hashes. Large page images remain in workspace `tmp/`.

## Source-stage output

The installed source files contain 1,832 rows from 1,859 accounted units: 241 table
prompts, 432 grammar units, seven grammar supplements, 890 physical specimen
atoms, 77 whole expressions, 34 outside Kui occurrences and 178 comparands.
Two physical word continuations join to produce 888 atomic emissions. Explicit
alternates become separate keyed rows; no optional notation is expanded into
unattested forms. Six source-supported ordered component links are retained.

The audit records 18 blank units, 15 unresolved language identities, eight
documentary units, three unresolved references, twelve exact existing Naiki
occurrences and two joined physical tails. These are accounted dispositions,
not missing extraction. Ambiguous Bhili of Basim, Korava, Burgandi and Koi
identifications are not silently assigned to modern languages. Hypothetical
forms and ambiguous reference expansions do not become ordinary attestations.

The rows use thirteen existing base languages. Four historical Kui dialects
are registered: Gumsur/Bod, Khondmals, Chinna Kimedi and Kalahandi. Kalahandi's
historical Kui attribution and conflicting modern identification remain explicit.
No historical coordinates are invented. Exact comparator dialect tags are reused
only where supported. Smith's text and Letchmajee's analyses remain provenance,
not new dialects.

Original preserves exact source spelling, punctuation and case. The display
profile lowercases and maps source ṅ to ŋ, following the same-volume convention.
Other ambiguous symbols and quantities remain literal with typed uncertainty;
there is no blanket phonological conversion. Native and Phonemic are blank.
Comma alternatives are expanded only by reviewed source decisions; automatic
parser splitting is disabled. Stable source-unit keys and durable identity state
are preserved.

## Reproduction and validation

`integration/build_candidate.py` produces the combined candidate and audit from
reviewed source-local inputs. Its `status.json` records those input hashes.
`import_source.py` validates the approved candidate by default; `--install`
installs the pinned CSV, YAML, profile, audit, bibliography additions and dialect
registrations. It requires `root-installation-approval.json`, checks canonical
baselines, and retains a recoverable installation journal. A successful
`installation-receipt.json` records installed hashes. Until that receipt exists
and verifies, this package remains a candidate. The source-stage installation
was performed on 2026-09-27; its receipt pins the accepted files. Repeating the
installer verifies the transaction without rewriting those files.

Final field and bibliography checks live in `final-output-review/`; real parser
NFC/NFD checks in `metadata-profile-proposal/`; isolated transaction regressions
in `installer-review/`. Independent original-reading reviews, whole-source
dispositions, graph decisions and explicit uncertainty records remain beside
these directories. `SCOPE.md` retains chronological discovery history, including
intermediate states superseded by the final reports.

Full CLDF/database builds, compiled graph/identity checks, the full repository
test suite, browser refresh and representative app inspection are deferred under
the user's instructions. No publication is implied. Source-stage acceptance
must not be described as end-to-end ingestion completion.
