## Editorial distinctions

- Pure grammatical labels are tags/analysis rather than invented English lexical definitions.
- `*tammunu` retains its printed star. Kolami `avnd` is explicitly hypothetical in source prose but lacks a printed star; it is not silently respelled with one.
- Source comparison or tentative origin does not produce an ancestry edge. Every proposed record has an empty graph-edge list.
- Source labels Korava, Kōi and Burgandi are preserved pending exact registry routing; the source's historical classification is not automatically a modern identity decision.
- The existing Naiki reuse checks compare language, literal form, gloss and exact printed occurrence key. Identical forms in unrelated sources or at other loci are not deduplicated automatically.
- Tamil underbar characters, compressed quantities and four Juang g/j cases require independent detail review. The current originals and all comparison crops are pinned by `outside-comparands-status.json`. In particular, Gondi five on p481 has a compact upper quantity mark that should be checked as `Saiyuṅg` versus `Saiyūṅg`; do not install either as independently certified yet.
- The original young-man crop was overwritten before unique crop naming was enforced. `outside-291-crop-continuity-20260927.json` explicitly preserves that incident and the new witness hash; no historical report was altered or retroactively matched.

No DB, full build, registry or canonical CSV changes were made for this proposal. The generator's structural and exact-reuse assertions pass; original-reading approval and integration remain separate gates.
