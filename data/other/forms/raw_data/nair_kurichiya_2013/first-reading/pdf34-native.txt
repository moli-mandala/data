KURICHIYA


The Krurichiyar are aprominent tribe of Kerala residing in Wayanad and Kannur district.
They are belived to be plains people who migrated to the hilly tracks from South Kerala.
Kurichiyar fought for Pazhassi Raja, a native king in his revolt against the British East India
Company. The Kurichiyar speak a dialect of Malayalam with many phonological
peculiarities. Only marginal morphological and syntactic variations from Malayalam are
observed in the Kurichiya speech.

Word initial, word medial and word final a of Malayalam has e in corresponding words of
Kurichiya speech.
