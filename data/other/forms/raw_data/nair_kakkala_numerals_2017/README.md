# Nair 2017 Kakkala numerals

The [Kakkala numeral page](https://lingweb.eva.mpg.de/channumerals/Kakkala.htm) in Eugene Chan's *Numeral Systems of the World's Languages* credits **Dr. Ravi Sankar S. Nair**, Central University of Kerala, as data provider on **24 February 2017**. The HTML was downloaded 25 September 2026 and checked in as `Kakkala.htm` (27,926 bytes, SHA256 `7e95db06a6c38b46d30b33ebb7fa921d457bf6518768739553425fa34de172b7`). The [numeralbank/channumerals v1.0.1 archival extraction](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15649312) is CC BY 4.0. The importer parses the checked-in primary page directly.

The table has **20 rows × 2 displayed cells = 40 numbered prompts**. Numeral 10 prints two complete readings and 1000 prints three, so **43 lexical readings are installed**. These have independent stable keys; no directional variant relation is inferred. The page's prose says numerals 9–90 can have `-ji/-cci` alternation and gives examples at 9 and 60, but its full alternate paradigm is not printed in the cells. These illustrative/unprinted forms are **not generated** as lexical rows. The 20-item seeded raw-versus-output audit found zero material errors; first/last rows, the two multiple-reading cells, and source colon/length typography are checked separately. There are no unreadable or held table cells. Chinese commentary and inferred components are excluded. No etymological or borrowing link is asserted.

The source names **Kakkala** and calls its speech `Kuḷupe:ccu`. [Glottolog](https://glottolog.org/resource/languoid/id/kakk1234) identifies Kakkala as a distinct spoken L1 language (`kakk1234`), absent from Jambu's prior canonical registry. Nair's 2013 *Tribal Languages of Kerala* independently describes `kuluva peeccu` as the people's name for their language, so `Kuḷupe:ccu` is kept as an autonym/alias rather than made into a dialect. The 2017 page supplies only Kerala/Malabar distribution; exact elicitation site and source-locality coordinates are unknown. A canonical `Kakkala` row uses Jambu's existing `S. Dravidian I` clade, with coordinates blank and no dialect row.

The raw CSV `Form` and `Phonemic` intentionally contain the same principal source transcription; compiled `Original` derives from that exact string. The display profile maps source `ɹ` → Jambu `r` and source `a:`/`aː`, `u:`/`uː` → macrons. It preserves `j` as printed; the page does not provide enough phonetic detail to force an affricate/glide reanalysis. `Phonemic` and `Original` retain source notation. The profile inventory has no unmapped symbols or replacements. `Native` is blank. Numeral IDs supply entry keys/locators, and `num` is the sole grammatical tag.
