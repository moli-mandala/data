# Grierson 1906 Kaikadi (Sholapur) comparative vocabulary

George A. Grierson, *Linguistic Survey of India*, IV, *Munda and Dravidian Languages* (Calcutta, 1906), [Wikimedia Commons DjVu](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Linguistic_Survey_of_India_Vol_4.djvu), 701 image pages, SHA256 `33e9aaa220db22fcde712705581a69f7edcfc7e20b3e7a09347dc969768020f1`. Wikimedia marks this original edition public domain. The complete scan is locally cached at `tmp/pdfs/LSI-V4.djvu`; all nine narrow-column page crops used for this ingest are checked in under `images/` so every reading remains reviewable even without that cache. Wikimedia copied its file from the Internet Archive `LSIV0-V11` item; the latter's former direct DjVu download URL now returns 404.

The included table portion is the **Kaikāḍī (Sholapur) column**, all 241 numbered English prompts on printed pp. 646–678 (DjVu pages 666–698; printed-to-DjVu offset +20). The column appears every fourth printed page, with 25 prompts on the first page and 27 on each later page. The column is independent of the adjacent Korvi (Belgaum) and Tamil (Poona) controls. Glottolog's Grierson 1906 bibliography explicitly maps its Kaikadi lect to canonical Kaikadi (`kaik1244`, ISO `kep`), distinct from its mapping of Korvi/Yerukala. Jambu's existing `S. Dravidian I` clade is used. The Sholapur survey label does not specify a speaker's exact site, so both new canonical and dialect coordinates are blank. The book makes no etymological claim for these rows.

The source inventory covers **all 241 target cells**, including the original 32–79 pilot in `transcription.tsv`: 162 cells have reviewed readings, which yield 164 form rows because items 14 and 20 each give two printed alternatives; 17 cells are printed blanks; 40 lexical or paradigm answers are held for uncertain transcription; and 22 full-sentence prompts (220–241) are audited but excluded from lexical forms. `full-scope-inventory.tsv` is the complete source-unit ledger, and the deterministic importer emits `audit.jsonl` with all 241 decisions. Printed prompt numbers remain stable entry keys and citation locators. The adjacent languages and other book sections are separate sources, not extra Kaikadi cells.

This DjVu has no usable text layer. Transcription was visually checked against the original page images and enlarged Kaikadi column crops. Tesseract 5 English `--psm 6` output for the original pilot is retained under `ocr/`, but its errors make it unsuitable as the source of truth. `comparison-1928-full.tsv` aligns the later Lexibank LSI transcription to 1906 prompts when possible, solely as a comparison witness, never as an unchecked replacement for the 1906 image. `sample-audit-2026092601.json` records the pilot's earlier seeded 20-cell comparison; `sample-audit-2026092602.json` records a second seeded 23-cell check across all newly extended pages with zero material errors. The full-scope audit and source-image evidence reside in `full-scope-inventory.tsv` and `images/`. The 40 held cells need a sharper scan or a reliable independent witness before insertion.

`Original` preserves the survey's Romanization. The display profile lowercases and maps source `ṅ` to IPA `ŋ` while preserving other clear diacritics; it does not invent a phonemic analysis. `Native` and `Phonemic` are blank. The printed alternatives at items 14 and 20 are separate form rows under the same prompt, with no asserted variant direction. All rows are unlinked, with no inferred borrowing or cognate edge.
