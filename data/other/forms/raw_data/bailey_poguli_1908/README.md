## Transcription and structure

Readable source symbols are retained literally, including breves, macrons, tilde stacks, diaereses, underdots, and underlined digraphs. Raised source vowels are represented by their literal letter and accent, with their placement recorded in Notes. Explicit stem–suffix paradigms and ditto signs are expanded; unsupplied forms after “etc.” are not invented. Source disagreement between sections is preserved: e.g. long `yīun` in the passive discussion versus `yiun` in the verb heading, and `gāū̃` in glossary item51 versus `gāŭ` in item88. Final `ĭ` in item84 is distinct from the long vowel in the grammar paradigm. Plain versus dotted consonants are retained independently.

The installed preservation profile covers every literal input symbol; display converts only house `w → v` and `ṅ → ŋ`, preserving the original spelling separately. Grammatical case, gender, number, person, tense, voice and causative information use registered tags. Whole expressions receive `multiword-expression`. Real interlinear translations and source patterns remain available. No etymological or donor relation is inferred from similarity or isolated multilingual comparison.

The source remains mapped to canonical `pog` and registered dialect `bailey1908-poguli`, without point coordinates. Bailey locates it east of Ramsuh, south of Banihal Pass, but does not supply an elicitation site. The existing canonical/dialect metadata are reused.
