# Manual review audit — Dhurwa items 83–124

## Scope and method

- Appendix B, physical PDF p. 19 / printed p. 14.
- Items 83–124 inclusive; 42 prompts × 5 ordered response columns = 210 conceptual cells.
- Each cell was hand-keyed from a 600-dpi rendered image and visually verified. Targeted 1200-dpi crops were used for small-cap `ɪ`, retroflex segments, nasal letters, length punctuation, and the multiword cells near the foot of the page.
- OCR and PDF text did not seed, supply, or verify lexical readings.
- All fields are NFC and each row declares `hand-keyed-from-rendered-source; OCR-not-copied`.
