# Manual review audit — Noira items 190–210

## Scope and method

- Source: ESR 2015-012, Appendix A3.
- Items: 190–210 inclusive; 21 prompts × 17 ordered lists = 357 conceptual cells.
- Source coordinates: physical PDF pp. 74–78 / printed pp. 68–72; physical page, printed page, and left/right column are explicit in every row.
- Every cell was visually inspected and independently hand-keyed from 400-dpi rendered page images. Difficult vowels, dental diacritics, nasalization, and source labels were re-rendered and checked at 900 dpi.
- OCR and PDF text did not seed, supply, or verify any lexical reading. The ledger is OCR-blind and every row declares `hand-keyed-from-rendered-source; OCR-not-copied`.
- All fields are NFC. Multiple printed responses and their numeric cognate/similarity labels are retained in source order.
