## Exact lexical topology

Appendix A3, “Wordlist transcriptions,” is on physical PDF pp. 33–78, corresponding to printed pp. 27–72. It contains 210 numbered prompts and 17 consistently ordered site/language lists, or 3,570 conceptual response cells. A site cell can contain zero, one, or multiple numbered responses; those printed response numbers are similarity/cognate-group annotations and are retained separately from the phonetic forms.

The 14 regional lists (2,940 conceptual cells) are:

1. Noiri-Chillare (`NCH`)
2. Noiri-Pannali (`NPN`)
3. Noiri-Astambha (`NAS`)
4. Noiri-Gomon (`NGO`)
5. Barutiya-Mutalwad (`BMU`)
6. Dungra Bhili-Mathwad (`DBM`)
7. Dungra Bhili-Ambadungar (`DBA`)
8. Nahali-Toranmal (`NTO`)
9. Kotli-Narayanpur (`KNA`)
10. Kotli-Taradi (`KTA`)
11. Gujari-Taradi (`GTA`)
12. Nahali-Tembhi (`NTE`)
13. Tukaithad-Korku (`TKO`)
14. Nihali-Jamod (`NJA`)

The report's table 4 identifies Astambha, Mundalwad/Mutalwad, and Toranmal as
Dhule-team lists, and the prose explicitly says the checked Toranmal list was
elicited by that team. These are the same three site lists published in ESR
2013-004. Their 630 cells and 834 printed responses are retained and reconciled
in the source-local audit, but are excluded from new-form staging in favor of
the earlier primary route. Thus this package has 11 new regional target lists
(2,310 conceptual cells). The three further controls (630 conceptual cells,
audit-only) are Gujarati (`GUJ`), Marati as spelled in the source (`MAR`), and
Hindi (`HIN`).

## Complete manual review

Items 1–210 occupy physical pp. 33–78 / printed pp. 27–72 and are represented
by eight disjoint OCR-blind ledgers under `manual_chunks/`. All 3,570 cells were
inspected against 400-dpi page renders and independently hand-keyed; difficult
glyphs were rechecked at 800 or 900 dpi. No OCR or PDF-extracted lexical string
supplied or verified an accepted form. Page-spanning items retain site-specific
physical/printed page and left/right-column coordinates.

The final accounting is 3,526 attested cells, 44 source-explicit blanks, 4,385
expanded responses, and no ambiguous or illegible transcription. New-target
staging contains 2,714 responses from 2,271 attested cells; 39 target cells are
source blanks. The exhaustive audit retains all 630 republished Dhule cells and
all 630 language-control cells with explicit exclusion dispositions.
