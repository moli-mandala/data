# SIL Lahul Valley survey (ESR 2019-006)

`extract_lahul.py` freezes Appendix A.4 from the official publisher PDF's
Unicode Charis SIL/Doulos SIL text layer. It uses no OCR and asserts the PDF,
page-text, concept sequence, per-list topology, and complete response sequence.

The source prints 210 concepts for 27 lect/site lists. Jambu installs the 22
lists newly collected in Lahul by the authors. Standard Hindi and Lhasa Tibetan
controls and the previously collected Tindi Pangi, Leh Ladakhi, and Spiti Bhoti
lists remain in the audit snapshot only. Similarity-group codes are descriptive
survey annotations, not historical cognacy claims.
