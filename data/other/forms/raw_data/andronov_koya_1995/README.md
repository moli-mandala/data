# Koya numeral table (source-stage audit)

Eugene Chan's [Koya numeral page](https://lingweb.eva.mpg.de/channumerals/Koya.htm) credits M. S. Andronov (Institute of Linguistics, Russian Academy of Sciences), 23 November 1995, with additional November 1995 Bible Society of India data; it refers separately to Tyler's 1969 Gommu-dialect grammar. The exact `raw/Koya.htm` bytes in [numeralbank/channumerals v1.0.1](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15649312) control this import. That archive's `raw/README.md` dates the download to 12 February 2019 and its `LICENSE` grants CC BY 4.0. Checked-in HTML is 29,863 bytes, SHA256 `0f9955a5cf981cedf29d2461fdd1a14d1268d4fe1d2833da13d5585648587803`; the archive ZIP is SHA256 `d40ef57924f47e9d3a777955328497d4b4bfe7b4f3344211993c9b21688bfd66`. OCR did not contribute. The numbered source cells themselves, not the later archive's automatic segmentations, are parsed.

`import_source.py` reads 20 HTML rows × two numbered cells = **40 prompts** (1–30, tens 40–90, 100, 200, 1000, 2000). Their slash and tilde divisions yield **62 printed answer segments**. It installs **58 secure, complete answers** and holds four abbreviated constituent substitutions: the second segments of 22 and 23, and the first segments of 200 and 2000. In the latter two cells, `roɳɖu nuːru` and `roɳɖu veːyi` are complete and retained. The importer does not expand the fragments into unattested full expressions. The printed 100 alternative `eyŋg koːɽek` is retained under its printed prompt, but `(2 x 20)` beside it conflicts arithmetically; no structural component relation is inferred. The eight answers with explicit nearby `< Indic` parentheticals are tagged `loanword`; no source-specific donor language or etymon is asserted. `das ~ des` yields two loan-marked answers. All slash/tilde answers are separate source answers without a directional variant edge. All 40 cells, four held fragments, markup, printed labels, qualifiers, table coordinates, emitted keys and decisions are in `audit.jsonl`. A fresh seeded 20-cell comparison (`sample-audit-2026092601.json`) recorded **0/20 material errors**; the first and last cells, compact slash expressions, and 100 arithmetic annotation were additionally inspected. No cells are blank.

The page labels the lect only `Koya, India`, without a stable survey site, so no new dialect or precise coordinate is inferred. [Glottolog](https://glottolog.org/resource/languoid/id/koya1251) identifies Koya `koya1251` as a spoken L1 language; this package targets new canonical `Koya`, S. Dravidian II, with blank coordinates and broad India location. Jambu already has `koya` and `gommu` dialects under generic `Gondi`, as well as SIL 1985 Koya survey dialects; about **2,262** compiled Gondi rows bear those Koya-related dialect tags. Those attestations remain untouched pending a full-build, source-by-source identity and migration audit. Four new numeral forms (`tommidi`, `padi`, `das`, `panendu`) exactly overlap independently cited 2021 SIL Koya/Gondi forms in house display spelling and gloss; no prior Andronov/BSI 1995 table citation was found. The new source is an independent attestation, but counts must not be interpreted as 58 entirely novel lexical types.

Source IPA-like spelling is retained verbatim as `Original` and `Phonemic`; the source profile maps explicit vowel length, known retroflexes and source `w` to house `v` for Jambu display. No grammar, etymological graph edge, source-specific site, or audio is inferred. The 1969 Tyler citation is contextual only and not treated as the source of any particular installed cell.

The installed files are `data/other/forms/20260925-andronov-koya-numerals.csv/.yaml`, `conversion/andronov-koya-1995.txt`, canonical `Koya` in `cldf/languages.csv`, and bibliography key `andronov1995koya` in `cldf/sources.bib`. `source_meta.py` passed (256 settings/244 keys at the time of registration); `tests/test_andronov_koya_1995.py` together with sound-profile and dialect tests passed **25/25**. Scoped `parse_file` converted **58/58** rows with no errors, and the whole source inventory had zero Koya profile findings. Full `make all`, full suite, generated CLDF identity/reference/graph checks and browser QA remain deferred under the user's no-build/no-remote instruction.
