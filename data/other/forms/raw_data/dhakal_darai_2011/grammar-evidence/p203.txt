6.5.7 Prospective
The non-finite form -lar functions as an attributive adjective. It inflects as
-lari to code feminine gender. Its function is adjectival when it occurs in attributive
position in relative clauses. Examples are given in (102).
(102) a. ekṭa bac lar ekṭa dziular buṭi del
ek-ṭa bac-lar ek-ṭa dzi-lar buṭi
one-CLF live-PROS one-CLF live-PROS medicine
de-l
give-PST
‘(He) gave me a medicine to live and a medicine to die.' (KK.SLD.085)
b. rk kilar pakalar cidz

‘The thing to be cooked and eaten at home,' (UT.SD.018)
c. hame dereini mod kilar dzat
hame derei-ni mod ki-lar dzat

the sentences instead of a finite form of the verb. The prospective forms occur in
