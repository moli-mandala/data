003: This section outlines a sociolinguistic survey of the Darai language conducted 
004: among the Darai people in Chitwan, Tanahun and Nawalparasi districts. The purpose 
005: for the survey is to present the sociolinguistic situations of Darai in the several 
006: villages, to assess the dialect situation and to look into the language endangerment of 
007: Darai employing nine factors set by United Nations Scientific and Cultural 
008: Organization (UNESCO 2003).3 It sets out to provide objective data so as to diagnose 
009: and explain the present situation and development of the Darai language with 
010: information on their degree of knowledge and use. In addition, it tries to assess the 
011: vitality of Darai if it is still being used in important domains or is replaced by Nepali, 
012: to determine whether it will continue to future generations, to evaluate the level of 
013: bilingualism in the neighboring languages, to show the attitude of the people towards 
014: their language and to determine which village in the Darai speaking area would be a 
015: good location for someone to start a language development program.   
016: Information regarding to this language was obtained through structured 
017: questionnaire compiled and localized that is compatible with the needs of the 
018: language. The study provides synthesized data collected during the survey, describes 
019: the situation of the languages and diagnoses the basic problems affecting them. Since 
020: the language is spoken mainly in different districts, viz. Chitwan, Tanahun and  
021: Nawalparasi, 105 samples have been collected from these places. 4
