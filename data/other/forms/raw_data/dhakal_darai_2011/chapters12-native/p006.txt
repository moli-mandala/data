005: Dhakal (2007a) is an analysis of the noun phrase structure in Darai. As a 
006: background to this study, nominal morphology has been summarized in one chapter. 
007: Basic nominal morphology including the declensional features of pronouns has been 
008: presented. Similarly, inflectional features of nominal morphology such as gender, 
009: number, pronominal possessive suffixes and case markers have also been analyzed.  
010: Additionally, the structure of noun phrase of Darai has been examined. As a whole, 
011: this is a typologically-informed study. However, nominal morphology in this study is 
012: not complete. It does not discuss the indefinite marker or indefinite pronouns and data 
013: on pronominal possessive suffixes are not complete. This study provides some helpful 
014: insights of the Darai noun phrase but includes a narrow range of noun phrase 
015: constructions.   
016: Dhakal (2007b) is a trilingual (Darai-Nepali-English) glossary of 1700 entries. 
017: The glossary contains the lexicon found in Tanahun and Chitwan dialects. Each 
018: lexical entry has its transcription in international phonetic alphabets, their parts of 
019: speech and meaning in Nepali. This contains the core vocabulary of Darai and 
020: therefore proves a useful source of information for phonological analysis. Dhakal 
021: (2007c) shows that Darai lacks a prototypical passive construction. However he does 
022: not mention -inuhuk  as a passive suffix.  
023:  In addition to independent research, we find some comments on the Darai 
024: language. Van Driem (2001:1170) notes that the pattern of pronominal reference in 
025: the Darai verb has been attributed to a Tibeto-Burman or Austro-Asiatic substrata. He 
026: further reiterates that that it is a severely endangered language (2007:326). Wilde 
027: (2008:160) notes that the secondary agreement of Rajbanshi, Maithili, Darai and other 
028: Indo-Aryan languages would be an interesting feature to explore. These observations 
029: help compare some striking features of Darai with neighboring languages. They might 
030: indicate the cases of language convergence and divergence.   
031: The extant literature on the language is directly related to sociolinguistics, 
032: phonology and morphology rather than on syntax. If there is one aspect of Darai 
033: linguistic study which can benefit from close examination, it is the area of Darai 
034: syntax. These works represent the whole of linguistic analysis that has been carried in 
035: Darai. Much work remains to be done in adequately describing the language.
