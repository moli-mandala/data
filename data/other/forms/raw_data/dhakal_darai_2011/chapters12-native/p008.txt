017: 1.5.3 Theoretical framework and data analysis 
018: The description of the Darai grammar has been presented in light of general 
019: linguistic literature. This study also has its basic orientation in describing the 
020: language. It is primarily based on Givón (2001a, b) which give many insights on 
021: grammatical description on morphology and syntax in general. Givón (2001a:XV) 
022: notes, "Structure without functions are plainly senseless, functions without structures 
023: are downright lame." The grammatical terms used in this grammatical description 
024: does not belong to specific theories. They are widely used in general linguistic 
025: descriptions. Moreover, the Darai language is compared with other IA languages 
026: wherever relevant.    
027: Additionally, Shopen (1985a; 1985b; 1985c; 2007a; 2007b; 2007c), Dixon 
028: (2004; 2009) and Haiman and Thompson (1988) have also been consulted. The 
029: references in specific chapters provide the particular references consulted in 
030: presenting several topics within the grammar.  
031: We have attempted to present the description of Darai in typological features 
032: of Indo-Aryan languages. Therefore, Masica (1991), Zograph (1982) and Grierson 
033: (1903a; 1903b; 1903c; 1904) are important sources of information on Indo-Aryan 
034: grammar and structure. Masica (1991) has been widely referred to throughout this 
035: dissertation.
