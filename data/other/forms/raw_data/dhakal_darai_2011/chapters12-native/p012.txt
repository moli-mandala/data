003: Traditionally Darais earn their livelihood from agriculture. They are also 
004: involved in other professions such as animal husbandry and business. The ownership 
005: of the land is private. They are economically poor and the literacy rate is low in Darai 
006: communities (Darai 1996: 39). Khati Chhetri (2057VS:53-54) mentions that Darais 
007: are agriculturalists lacking specific skills to carry out other special jobs. They have 
008: lower socio-economic status. Lack of technical knowledge has resulted in their 
009: massive unemployment. They have comparatively small land holding sizes. About 
010: half of these people are landless (Darai 2008). Darai (1996:9) sums up the cultural 
011: information in the following extract:  
012: Darais use earthen pots for carrying water, preparing beer, keeping wine, 
013: frying corn and boiling roots. They have very few traditional iron, bronze, 
014: copper or aluminum pots. Their language, house, construction and worship 
015: separate this ethnic group from all others. Also the traditional marriage system 
016: is central to these people and different from most other ethnic groups' 
017: tradition.

019: 2.1.3 Darai villages: demography and geography 
020:  
021: The physical terrain where Darai is spoken includes the hills and inner terai. 
022: The Darai villages are located in the adjoining areas bordered by Narayani river and 
023: its tributaries. The ethnic group geographically spreads on the surrounding areas of 
024: Narayani and Madi rivers with the largest concentration in Chitwan, Tanahun and 
025: Nawalparasi districts of central and western Nepal. The geographical boundaries of 
026: the language have not changed over the years as the previous two censuses suggest 
027: (CBS 2001; CBS 2002a; 2002b).
