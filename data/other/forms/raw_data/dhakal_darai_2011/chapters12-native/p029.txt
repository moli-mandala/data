010: 2.3.2 Dialect situation   
011:  
012:  
013: All the respondents reply that the name is the same in all places where Darai is 
014: spoken. Although the Darais speak the language differently in two dialectal regions, 
015: they are intelligible between them based on the responses of the respondents. Since 
016: the Darais in Nawalparasi were migrated from Tanahun, the language they speak 
017: resembles the Tanahun dialect. Among the respondents only 17 respondents (16.1 
018: percentage) say that the dialects are not mutually intelligible, 6 respondents (5.7 
019: percentage) reply that they do not know the situation but the majority of respondents 
020: reply that the language spoken in different areas are mutually intelligible without 
021: hampering communication. Some language consultants from Chitwan add that the 
022: Darai language spoken in Tanahun is similar to Bote in accent. On the contrary, a few 
023: respondents from Tanahun and Nawalparasi feel that the Darai language spoken in 
024: Chitwan is similar to Tharu. In a similar vein, the people from Kathar add that the 
025: language spoken in Madhi area resembles Tharu and to some extent different from the 
026: language spoken in their village. The dialect situation is shown in Table (2.5).
