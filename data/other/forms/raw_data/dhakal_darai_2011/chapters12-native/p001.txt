009: CHAPTER 1 
010: INTRODUCTION 
011:  
012: 1.0 Outline 
013: The purpose of this dissertation is to present a grammatical description of 
014: Darai. The Darai language is spoken by the people of the same name primarily in the 
015: central and western Nepal in the districts of Chitwan, Nawalparasi and Tahahun. 
016: Darai-speaking areas include plains and hills. In the areas in which Darais are 
017: concentrated they constitute a minority.  
018: The total number of Darai ethnic group is 14,859 whereas only 10210 Darai 
019: people speak it as their mother tongue (Yadava 2003:165). This leads us to the fact 
020: that only 68.7 percent of the Darais speak this language. Among the Darai speakers, 
021: 8500 are bilingual in Nepali. Only a few Darai were reported to have been 
022: monolingual in our field study.  The data for this dissertation are obtained from the 
023: language consultants who are inhabitants of different villages of Chitwan district. 
024: Areally, Darai is situated along with Nepali, Bhojpuri, Tharu, Bote and Kumal of 
025: Indo-Aryan language family. By contrast, genetically unrelated languages of Tibeto-
026: Burman family viz. Tamang, Gurung, Chepang and Magar are also spoken in 
027: neighboring areas. Therefore, there might be a long history of contact with genetically 
028: unrelated languages.  
029: The previous studies do not present a substantial description of the linguistic 
030: structures of the Darai grammar. Thus, this dissertation attempts to serve the most in-
031: depth description of the language to date.  This study is largely synchronic. It 
032: discusses the sociolinguistic, phonological and morphosyntactic features of the Darai 
033: language including a note on discourse. The data used in this dissertation were taken 
034: from natural texts. Paradigms, tables and other structural information regarding the 
035: language were obtained through direct elicitation from the language consultants.  
036: The methodological contexts such as the objectives of the study, review of 
037: literature, data collection, methodology, and theoretical framework are discussed in 
038: this chapter.
