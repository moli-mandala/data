003: 1.1 Statement of problem  
004:  
005: Darai belongs to Indo-Aryan language subgroup of Indo-European language 
006: family. The language has not been adequately described and its classification has not 
007: been determined. Therefore, the main problematic research questions related to this 
008: research are: 
009: 1. What is the sociolinguistic situation of Darai ? 
010: 2. How are the Darai sounds organized within the phonological system of the 
011: language? 
012: 3. What are the morpholosyntactic features evident in Darai ? 
013: 4. How is discourse organized in Darai ?  
014:  
015: 1.2 Objectives of the study 
016: The general objective of the study is to present the grammatical description of 
017: the Darai language. The specific objectives of the study are to: 
018: 1. present sociolinguistic information of the Darai language,  
019: 2. describe Darai sound system, 
020: 3. analyse morphosyntactic features of Darai, and  
021: 4. present a discourse analysis of  the  Darai language. 
022:  
023: 1.3 Review of literature  
024: There have been very few things written earlier to 1970s about the Darai 
025: language. The earliest linguistic references to it can be found in Hodgson (1857). 
026: Hodgson compares the vocabulary of Darai with other 'broken tribes' viz. Danuwar, 
027: Chapang, Baram, Hayu and Kusuwar.  He further points out the pronominal 
028: possessive suffixes and subject and object agreement in the transitive verbs. Some 
029: adjective which end in -o in Hodgson's list are -ə ending these days.  
030: Grierson (1903c:1819) mentions that some tribes of the central Himalayan 
031: speak a corrupt khas kuraa.  However, this brief account lacks main linguistic 
032: characteristics of the language. The first mention of the ethnic name is reported to 
033: have found in the lal mohar granted by King Prithvi to Siddhabhagawantanath in 
034: 1827 VS (cited in Adhikari 2041VS:3).

036: language. Kotapish and Kotapish (1973) is a glossary of Darai vocabulary which
