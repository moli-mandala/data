014: Dhakal (2005) is a study of verb morphology of Darai. The study is divided 
015: into eight chapters. The first chapter is an introduction and setting of the study. Verb 
016: stems are discussed in chapter two. Tenses with aspects and moods are analyzed in 
017: chapter three. 'Be' verbs, auxiliaries are discussed chapter four and five respectively. 
018: Chapter six deals with causative and passive. Similarly, chapter seven outlines 
019: negative morphemes. Thus, this study is a general outline of Darai verbal 
020: morphology. It is also marked by some limitations. Firstly, the study is based on 
021: elicited data. Secondly, the discussion of verb stems does not show how the 
022: phonological structure of the stems triggers morphophonemic changes when they are 
023: attached to inflectional suffixes. Thirdly, the chapter on tense does not analyze subject 
024: as well as object agreement among others.  
025: Dhakal (2006a) is a sociolinguistic study of Darai language. The study 
026: presents some evidences of language varieties in two different locations. A 
027: comparative wordlist as well as few grammatical features have been analyzed in this 
028: study. However, sampling for bilingualism and individual sociolinguistic 
029: questionnaires is limited. It further corroborates that language variations exist in two 
030: localities.
