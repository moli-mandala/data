028: 2.3.4 Intergenerational language transmission7 
029: This is a well known fact that Darais have shifted from their native language 
030: in favour of Nepali in some places. This trend is prevalent even in the areas where 
031: Darai speakers are densely populated. The reasons for this are complex. As more and 
032: more government functions take place in Nepali, parents experience increased 
033: pressure to equip their children for a predominantly Nepali speaking environment. 
034: Since language transmission pattern is not the same in different Darai speaking areas, 
035: they are presented in Table (2.8) for comparison and contrast. 
036: Table (2.8) presents some interesting results. Firstly, language transmission is 
037: more vital in Chitwan and Tanahun than in Nawalparasi. Secondly, intergenerational 
038: transmission is weak in Nawalparasi through these four generations. In fact, there are 
039: several Darai people in Nawalparasi who have shifted to Nepali and adopted it as their 
040: first language since three or four generations. We may place these different villages in 
041: a continuum showing different shades in language transmission through generations.
