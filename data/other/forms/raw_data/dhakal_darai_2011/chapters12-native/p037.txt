013: 2.3.9 Materials for language education and literacy11 
014: Crystal (2001:113) writes: 
015: A strong literary tradition can be a source of great prestige, not only within the 
016: indigenous community but also among the society at large. Even in the case of 
017: art forms where there is no linguistic element, such as dancing, language can 
018: take advantage of their popularity; no dance has yet been invented which has 
019: not been given a name or an interpretation and language then comes to the 
020: fore.   
021:  Languages without written tradition may face loss because they rely on oral 
022: transmission. If one generation fails to learn the language, the language is not 
023: transmitted to the next generation. The Darais use Devanagari script when they write 
024: but they have no practical orthography. Similarly, there have been no attempts for the 
025: standardization of the language and orthography. Although some materials exist, they 
026: are only useful for some members of the community.
