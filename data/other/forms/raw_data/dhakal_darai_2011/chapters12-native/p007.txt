003: Some sketchy studies have been conducted on the Darai language and clearly 
004: no book-length treatment of Darai language is found. The available literature is 
005: structuralist and descriptive in its outlook and intent rather than functional and 
006: typological. Except for Kotapish and Kotapish (1973; 1975) other works lack 
007: adequate data to support the analysis. The description of various aspects of grammar 
008: till this date is mainly based on direct elicitation rather than discourse corpus.  This 
009: dissertation will therefore attempt to describe the language based on corpus data. 
010:  
011: 1.4 Significance of the study 
012: In attempt to provide a grammar of a lesser described language, it will be a 
013: detailed grammatical treatment of the Darai language. This study will be useful in 
014: preparing textbooks for children and for writing pedagogical grammar. Additionally, 
015: it will help determine the genetic affiliation of the language.  It will be helpful in 
016: safeguarding and promoting the linguistic heritage of Nepal. This dissertation would 
017: be a contribution in the study of minority and undocumented language in general and 
018: in the study of minor Indo-Aryan language in particular. Furthermore, the data 
019: presented in this study will be useful for typological studies. Moreover, the Darai 
020: language has been subclassified for the first time based on detailed grammatical 
021: features.

023: 1.5 Methodology 
024: 1.5.1 Tools 
025: The computer software programs used are Praat and Toolbox. Praat was used 
026: to obtain spectrograph picture and to analyze the formants of Darai vowels. Similarly, 
027: Toolbox was used for maintaining lexical data and interlinearizing texts.  
028:  
029: 1.5.2 Data collection  
030: Sociolinguistic information was primarily obtained from language consultants 
031: by means of a structured questionnaire compiled by the researcher. It is included in 
032: Appendix (1).  
033: The data were collected in Chitwan during 2005-2007. Our initial work on 
034: Darai began in 2005 and the data collected are also incorporated where relevant. Two 
035: texts recorded by Nepal Darai Development Society, Chitwan have also been used.
