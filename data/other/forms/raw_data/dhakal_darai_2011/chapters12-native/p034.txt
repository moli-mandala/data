008: 2.3.6 Proportion of speakers within the total population 
009: Another factor related to language vitality is the number of speakers in relation 
010: to the total population. The number of speakers within the total population is 
011: relatively low in Darai. 
012: The total number of Darai ethnic group is 14,859 whereas the number of Darai 
013: as the mother tongue is 10210 as established by the recent census (Yadava 2003:165). 
014: This leads us to the fact that only 68.7 percent of the Darais speak this language. We 
015: may take this data for granted as we do not have complete statistics obtained from any 
016: linguistic survey. Since only majorities speak the language, Darai can be categorized 
017: as ‘definitely endangered’ language.

020: 2.3.7 Trends in existing language domains9

024: vocabulary, discourse patterns and stylistic range. It is easy to see how a 
025: language could eventually die, simply because having been denuded of most 
026: of its domains, there is hardly any subject-matter left for people to talk about 
027: and hardly any vocabulary left to do it with.
