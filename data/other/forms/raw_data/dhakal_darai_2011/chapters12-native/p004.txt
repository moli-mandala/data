005: Similarly Neupane (2057 VS) is a comparative glossary of Nepali and Darai. 
006: He also compares some words of Darai with some Indo-Aryan neighbors, viz.  
007: Danuwar, Bote, Kumal and Bhojpuri.  The comparison reveals that there is a great 
008: similarity in some basic vocabulary. Some lexical items and verb stems are identical 
009: but differ greatly in the inflection. Kharal (2058 VS) mentions that some vocabularies

013: Darai (1998) includes several aspects of Darai sociolinguistics in his study. 
014: His conclusion that there is a dialectical variation between the language spoken in 
015: Tanahun and Chitwan is true. His 250 word-list from Chitwan and Tanahun and 
016: sample recorded text in interlinear format corroborate this. This study is marked by 
017: some limitations. Firstly, the study devotes little space for sociolinguistic information. 
018: Secondly, presentation of grammatical description is inadequate. Although his 
019: approach is descriptive in intent, discussion does not analyze some features typical of 
020: Darai such as pronominal possessive suffixes and verb agreement.    
021: Paudyal (2003) is a sketch grammar of Darai.  His study is divided into 6 
022: chapters. The first chapter is an introduction to the language and chapter 2 outlines 
023: people and the language. Phonology, morphology and syntax are separately dealt with 
024: in the next three chapters and the study is summarized in the final chapter. His 
025: analysis of phonology does not add new information to Kotapish and Kotapish 
026: (1975). Although we find discussion of nominal morphology such as number and 
027: some case markers, information related to nominal morphology is not adequate. For 
028: example, inflections of pronouns have not been included. Similarly, treatment of verb 
029: morphology is not exhaustive. Despite the analysis of tense, aspect and mood 
030: markers, a large part of verb morphology has been left unanalyzed. The number is 
031: also coded in the past tense which he refutes (2003:68). Adverbs, onomatopoeias, 
032: exclamations and discourse particles are not included in this grammatical sketch.
