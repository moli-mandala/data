007: 2.1.6 Genetic affiliation  
008:  
009: Darai belongs to Indo-Aryan subgroup of Indo-European language family 
010: among 92 languages native to Nepal as reported in 2001 census. It is also widely 
011: described as 'Indo-Aryan' language that is closely related to Bote and Kumal. 
012: Regarding to the genetic classification of Darai, Grierson (1903c:18-19) comments: 
013: Certain broken tribes of the central Himalayan speak a corrupt khas kuraa. In 
014: each case it can be said that they employ a genuine dialect....The tribes which 
015: adopted the incorrect khas kuraa are three in number; viz. Dahi, Dadhi or 
016: Darhi; Denwar or Donwar; and Kuswar. In the case of last named, the 
017: vocabulary is almost entirely khas kuraa, the grammar is still Tibeto Burman. 
018: In the case of other two the whole language is much more Aryan in its 
019: character. 
020: A good number of languages recorded in the recent census have not been 
021: properly classified as to their genetic relation and so are treated as unclassified 
022: languages. Yadava (2003:146) classifies the language into Indo-European and Indo-
023: Aryan but adds that the language is not fully classified due to lack of adequate 
024: description. Pokhrel (1995:1; 2050 VS:94), on the other hand, relates the language to 
025: Ardhamagadhi group of Indo-Aryan branch and mentioned that its immediate 
026: neighbours are Tharu (Tharuwani), Bote and Kumal. He further adds that Darai 
027: language characterizes the features of Magadhi and Ardhamagadhi. On the other 
028: hand, Lewis (2009:886) records this language as Indo-European, Indo-Iranian and 
029: Indo-Aryan without designating any further classification. He further adds that it has 
030: typological affinities with North Western Zone, Dardic group. The study of the Darai 
031: language including its relevant literature is at early stage in terms of its description, 
032: research and analysis. It is not fully described and classified till date.1 Asher and 
033: Moseley (2007) do not provide full genetic classification of Darai. These 
034: classifications do not differ drastically but its genetic classification has not been 
035: determined.  
036:                                                  
037: 1 National Languages Policy Recommendation Commission (1994) categorizes Darai as a language 
038: which does not have literate tradition, literature and literacy.
