003: 1.6 Organization of the study 
004: This dissertation contains thirteen chapters in addition to five appendices. The 
005: first chapter is an introduction to the Darai language, statement of the problem, 
006: objectives of the study, review of literature, methodology and theoretical framework 
007: of the study. Chapter 2 has two main parts. The first part deals with the Darai people 
008: and their culture, genetic affiliation of the language and demography of Darai 
009: speakers. The second part presents the sociolinguistic situation of the Darai language. 
010: Chapter 3 presents the phonological analysis of the language. Chapters 4-8 are 
011: concerned with nouns and nominal morphology, adjective, verbs and verb 
012: morphology, adverbs and closed word class in that order. Chapter 9 presents the 
013: constituents of the noun phrase. Chapter 10 analyzes basic constituent order, the 
014: structure of a simple sentence and its modifications. Chapter 11 outlines the clause 
015: combining constructions. A note on discourse has been presented in chapter 12 
016: including analyzed texts.  The conclusion (chapter 13) summarizes the major aspects 
017: of the Darai grammar covered in this work, typological implications, proposed genetic 
018: classification of the Darai language and suggests the areas that deserve further 
019: investigation.  
020: Appendix 1 is the sociolinguistic questionnaire used for the sociolinguistic 
021: survey and appendix 2 lists the background information of the respondents. Appendix 
022: 3 provides the verb paradigms and further examples to illustrate the verb paradigms 
023: (morphology). The index of grammatical morphemes is presented in appendix 4. 
024: Appendix 5 includes sample texts.
