021: 2.3.3 Bilingualism/multilingualism 
022: Most of the respondents are found to be bilingual, a few to be multilingual,  
023: but none are monolingual in Darai. Darai is the language of home domains. As a 
024: Darai speaker moves away from home, he becomes bilingual. Darai children grown 
025: up in rural areas of Chitwan, particularly in Kathar, and Jagatpur VDCs are 
026: monolingual before they are enrolled at schools. They gradually turn bilingual for the 
027: reason that the medium of instruction is Nepali, which also serves as lingua franca.
