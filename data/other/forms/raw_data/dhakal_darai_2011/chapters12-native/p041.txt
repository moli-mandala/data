003: 2.3.14 Language endangerment of Darai 
004: Crystal (2001:81) writes, “Languages are not like people, in this respect: it is 
005: not usually possible to write a single cause on the death certificate for a language.” On 
006: the basis of the indexes discussed in the preceding paragraphs, Darai can be 
007: categorized as a ‘definitely endangered language’.  
008:  
009: 2.4 Summary 
010: This chapter provided background information of Darai people outlining their 
011: origin, profession, demography and current status of the language. Darai is a small 
012: community comprising 14,859 number as an ethnic group whereas only 10,210 speak 
013: this as their mother tongue. Darai has some characteristic feature of the South Asian 
014: languages in terms of kinship terms and naming people.  
015: On the basis of the UNESCO criteria, we categorized Darai as a ‘definitely 
016: endangered’ language. The Darai language is well used in all domestic domains, and 
017: it is even presently broadcast by one local FM station. Aside from media, its use in 
018: education and administration has not been attempted. Bilingualism in Nepali seems to 
019: be increasing almost in all places. The degree of bilingualism in Nepali is increasing 
020: with younger people especially in urban areas. Intergenerational language 
021: transmission is good in some places as all children speak the Darai language there. 
022: However, they turn bilingual with their enrolment at schools. Attitudes toward the 
023: continuation of the Darai language are very positive. Pidrahani village of Kathar VDC 
024: from Chitwan would be a good location to learn the Darai language.
