038: 2.3 Sociolinguistic survey of the language  
039: The study of language endangerment pattern in Darai is complicated by a lack 
040: of precise sociolinguistic information. The interaction of linguistic factors with non-
041: linguistic elements needs to be explained in order to assess the language vitality of 
042: Darai. Language endangerment is not an isolated factor but an amalgamation of 
043: various social, cultural, legal and economic elements. Since language is not 
044: independent of its speakers, we need to explain the communicative behavior of 
045: speakers in broader social milieu. Sociolinguistic survey was conducted using the 
046: questionnaire given in Appendix (1). Questionnaires were adapted from Rapcha 
047: (2005).
