010: CHAPTER 2 
011: PEOPLE AND LANGUAGE 
012:  
013: 2.0 Outline 
014: This chapter is about Darai people and their language. It is organized into four 
015: sections. Section 2.1 deals with the information related to Darai people, demography 
016: and culture including the genetic affiliation of the language. Section 2.2 outlines 
017: sociolinguistic aspects such as kinship terms, forms of address, politeness strategies 
018: and taboos. Section 2.3 presents the sociolinguistic survey of the language. The 
019: summary of the findings is presented in section 2.4.   
020:  
021: 2.1 Darai people and language 
022: The aim of this section is to introduce some preliminary information on the 
023: Darai language, its speakers and some cultural practices. The socio-cultural 
024: information presented in this section includes the cultural background of Darai 
025: people. It is hoped that the information provides a basic idea about the Darai people, 
026: their material culture and the language they speak.  
027:  
028: 2.1.1 Origin and name 
029: Darai is one of the nationalities of Nepal (Dahal 2003:114). The term 'Darai' 
030: refers to an ethnic group and the language they speak. Darai people are known by 
031: several names: Darbai, Darhi, Daree, Daroe, Daraie (Gautam and Magar 1994:157). 
032: Another alternative name is also 'Dry' (Lewis 2009:486).  Thus,  the name Darai 
033: constitutes both autoglotonym and heteroglotonym.  
034:  
035: There is not any unanimous explanation why the ethnic group is referred to as 
036: ‘Darai’. Some explanations and stories prevalent in the communities serve to trace the 
037: origin of the term. The anthropological and sociological studies (Sharma 2046 VS; 
038: Khati Chhetri 2057 VS; Darai 1996; Kunwar 2059 VS; Darai 1998) inter alia have 
039: consistently cited Bista (1967), Mainali (1984) and Gautam and Magar (1994) to trace 
040: the origin of the term 'Darai'.
