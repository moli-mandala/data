46 3.1.2 Nasal vowels It is extremely difficult to come up with nasal and non-nasal pairs of words to show contrast. However, we find all six nasal vowels. This can thus be regarded as a marginal suprasegmental feature of a small set of lexemes.  The fact that nasalization is not very robust in Darai is also referred to by Kotapish and Kotapish (1975:47). They note, "Nasalization occurs in Darai but varies from speaker to speaker."  The central nasal vowel /ã/ occurs more frequently than other nasal vowels.
