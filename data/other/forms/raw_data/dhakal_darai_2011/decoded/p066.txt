The onsets found in our corpus are all the consonant sounds in our phonemic inventory. The glide /w/ does not normally occur in word-initial position but is attested in onset position in a handful of words such as kũwar 'month, mid September to mid-October', kewari 'door'.  Velar nasal is neither attested in word-initial position nor in onset position.
