65 3.3 Syllable structure The canonical syllable type consists of a consonantal onset and rhyme. The maximal syllable template is (C)(G)V(C). Consonants are optional. Kotapish and Kotapish (1973: 52) note, "The nucleus which is filled by a single vowel is obligatory."
