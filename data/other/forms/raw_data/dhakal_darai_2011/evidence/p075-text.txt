3.5.5 Vowel harmony
This occurs marginally in Darai. When the causative suffix -a is suffixed to
the verb stem with //, the vowel in the verb root copies the vowel of the suffix and
changed into -a. Thus, there is a harmonic assimilation moving backward to affect the
preceding syllable.5 This is further illustrated in (10.5.1).
