3.5.2 Insertion
If verb stem ending in /i/ or /o/ is suffixed to suffixes beginning with a
consonant, the vowel /u/ is inserted between stem and suffix. This process can be
illustrated by examples (65).4 In example (65) the past tense marker -l is suffixed to
the verb ro- 'weep'. There is an insertion of the vowel /u/ between the stem and past
tense marker. We can see similar example in (66). More examples are given in
Appendix (3a).
(65) mer cahom roul
mer caho-m ro-l
My son-POSS.1SG weep-PST
‘My son cried.’
