3.4 Intonation
There is a grammatical role of intonation to mark the clause types: statement,
question and commands and so on. We discuss a few intonation patterns in Darai. We
will discuss two intonation patterns, namely falling and rising intonation.
3.4.1 Falling intonation
Generally, the statements, command and content question have falling
intonation. They are given in (58), (59) and (60) respectively. Pitch pattern is also
