c. Fricatives
There are 2 fricatives in Darai, i.e. glottal fricative /h/ and alveolar fricative
/s/. Alveolar fricative /s/ occurs in all positions whereas glottal fricative /h/ is found
in word-initial and intervocalic position. Minimal pairs are given in (18) and
distribution of the fricatives is given in Table (3.10).
(18) /s/ vs /h/
/sor/ ‘pig’ /hor/ ‘plow’
/sat/ ‘together, accompany’ /hat/ ‘hand’
