003: Nominative case: -ø 
004: Nominative case in Darai appears in null. We have used the symbol -ø to 
005: indicate the nominative case. Nominative is used for the subject complement in 
006: copular clauses (see section 10.2.1). Nominative nouns appear in subject position in 
007: the intransitive clauses and if the agent is the first and second person pronouns. The 
008: examples in (40a-b) are nominative subjects (also see 56a-d).
