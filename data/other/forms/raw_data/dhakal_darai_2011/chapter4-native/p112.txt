003: 4.2 Pronouns 
004: Pronouns inflect for case and number. They have oblique stems when they 
005: inflect for genitive, accusative-dative and comitative-ablative cases. Ergativity is not 
006: coded in the first and second person pronouns due to the split ergativity.18 The second 
007: person pronouns are characterized by the three levels of honorificity whereas only two 
008: levels of honorificity are found in the third person. Syntactically, pronouns are not 
009: modified by demonstratives and adjectives. 
010:  
011: We discuss personal pronouns, demonstrative pronouns, relative pronouns, 
012: reflexive pronouns, indefinite pronouns and possessive pronouns in this section.  
013:  
014: 4.2.1 Personal pronouns 
015: Darai distinguishes the first, second and third person pronouns. They inflect 
016: for two number: singular and plural and four cases: nominative (NOM), 
017: accusative/dative (ACC/DAT), genitive (GEN) and ablative/comitative (ABL/COM).
