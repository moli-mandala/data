004: CHAPTER 4 
005: NOUNS AND NOUN MORPHOLOGY 
006:  
007: 4.0 Outline 
008: This chapter deals with nouns and noun morphology of Darai. Section 4.1 
009: presents the properties of nouns, noun formation process as well as inflectional 
010: morphology of Darai nouns.1  Section 4.2 discusses the pronouns. The findings of 
011: nominal morphology are presented in section 4.3.  
012:  
013: 4.1 Nouns 
014: We discuss derivational and inflectional characteristics as well the properties 
015: of nouns.  
016:  
017: 4.1.1 Properties of nouns 
018: Nouns are complete semantic units. Givón (2001a:51) states that nouns in any 
019: language "occupies the most time-stable end of the scale." Common nouns in Darai 
020: include the name of things, places, animals and people. Morphologically, nouns in 
021: Darai have inflectional categories such as head-marking, number, indefinite marking 
022: and case marking. The head marking and case marking are syntactic (aside from 
023: morphological) in the language and they constitute distinctive syntactic properties of 
024: nouns.  
025: Nouns in Darai may be consonant or vowel ending, of which the former 
026: category has a large number. The properties of the nouns are based on their syntactic 
027: criteria. The noun functions as subject of an intransitive clause (1), direct object in the 
028: finite verb in (2), indirect object (3), subject complement (4) and object complement 
029: (5).
