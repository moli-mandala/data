005: ii. Number 
006: Darai has two grammatical numbers, viz. singular and plural. Singular number 
007: is unmarked whereas plural is encoded with -səb.6 The separate lexical word səb 'all' 
008: forms a noun into periphrastic plural in Maithili (Yadav 1996:69) but the suffix 
009: -səb functions as a suffix in Darai. This can better be explained with reference to verb 
010: agreement where the same suffix stands for number.
