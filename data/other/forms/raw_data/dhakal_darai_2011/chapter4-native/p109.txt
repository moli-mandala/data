038: Postposition ləɡi 
039: The postposition ləɡi 'motion to/near' is also used for the allative function. It 
040: is used to indicate an action occurring upto a certain location. However, there is also a 
041: distinct postposition for allative in Darai. This is also mediated by genitive marker. 
042: Examples are given in (60).

071: Postposition laɡi   
072: The postposition laɡi functions as a benefactive postposition. This is also 
073: mediated by genitive marker. The benefactive postposition marks the noun phrase that 
074: is the benefactor of the action of the verb.
