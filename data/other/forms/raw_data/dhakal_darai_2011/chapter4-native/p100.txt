043: merged into a single category. Masica (1991:365) notes that there is no distinction 
044: between accusative and dative case marking in NIA. He (1991:365) further adds:  
045: Historically, the Indo-Aryan Accusative merged with the Nominative…. That 
046: is, in the case of non-human nouns, it generally indicates a "definite" object,

048: Patienthood, marked status (human nouns normally being Agents).
