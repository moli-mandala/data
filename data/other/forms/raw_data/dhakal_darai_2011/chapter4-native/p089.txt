063: iii. Pronominal possessive suffixes  
064: Although several Indo-Aryan languages spoken in Nepal are dependent-
065: marked in genitive construction, Darai is both head-marking and dependent-marking 
066: too. In Darai, for example, nominal agreement markers must agree with the possessor. 
067: Kotapish and Kotapish (1975:V) state, “a noted difference between Darai and Nepali 
068: and other Indo Aryan languages and one of the highlights of Darai language is the 
069: pronominal cross reference markings found in both nouns and verbs."7 In this way, 
070: agreement in person and number is obligatory between possessors and possessed if 
071: the pronominal possessive suffixes are present. Table (4.1) contains the pronominal 
072: possessive suffixes attached to the nouns particularly to express kinship relations,
