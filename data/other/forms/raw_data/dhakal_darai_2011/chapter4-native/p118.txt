045: 4.2.5 Reflexive pronouns 
046: Reflexive pronouns are lexical in Darai. The reflexive pronoun is apʰni or 
047: apʰnə. Reflexive pronouns can be used to indicate ‘self’ and can be used with the 
048: first, second and third persons. The form varies with case and number but not with 
049: gender or person. In our database we find that the reflexive pronoun can also take the 
050: plural suffix as can be seen in (74b).
