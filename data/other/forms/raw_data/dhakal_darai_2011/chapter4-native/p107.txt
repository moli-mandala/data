048: A split ergative language is one in which some transitive clauses but not all, 
049: require ergative construction (DeLancey 1979:626). Ergative languages are inclined 
050: to exhibit one of these patterns: (i) The empathy-hierarchy pattern (SAP) (ii) The 
051: aspectual split and (iii) The active/stative split. They are also referred to as the 
052: nominal split, aspectual split and syntactic split respectively. The kind of split we 
053: observe in Darai is a nominal (personal) split. DeLancey(1979:627) reiterates that, 
054: this (nominal split) pattern shows, " hierarchy of 'animacy', 'agentivity', topicality', 
055: 'salience' or 'empathy', by which 1st and 2nd persons outrank human 3rd persons, 
056: which outrank non-human animates, which in turn outrank inanimate."  In Darai the 
057: split begins between the second and the third person pronouns.
