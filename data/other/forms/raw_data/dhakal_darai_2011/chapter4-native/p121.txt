017: 4.2.7 Indefinite pronouns 
018: Darai possesses different pronouns employed for non-specific and/or 
019: indefinite referents. The pronouns can be changed into indefinite by suffixing  -hun to 
020: interrogative pronouns. In some cases they use the oblique forms of kiti 'what' or 
021: kono 'who' such as kəlhəu/kolhəu.  
022: (82) 
023: a. 
024: kiti 
025: 'what'  
026: kjahau/kəlhəu'what-INDEF' 'something' 
027: b. 
028: kiti 
029: 'what'  
030: kiti-hun 
031: 'what-INDEF' 'something' 
032: (83)

036: 'who-INDEF' 'somebody'

043: indefiniteness indicated by kolhəu and konohun  given in (83). These forms simply 
044: imply the speaker's uncertainty regarding who/what the speaker is referring to.
