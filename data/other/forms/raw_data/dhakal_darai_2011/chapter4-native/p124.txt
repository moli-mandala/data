003: 4.3 Summary 
004: This chapter discussed nouns and noun morphology in Darai. Phonologically, 
005: nouns do not have distinct characteristics but bisyllabic nouns are very common. 
006: Nouns may be free lexical items or derived from other categories, largely from verbs 
007: and nouns. Thus, deverbal and denominal nouns are found in Darai. Compounding of 
008: nouns is common in Darai.  We find coordinate, exocentric and endocentric 
009: compounds. Reduplication of nouns is found to show distributiveness, and emphasis. 
010: Nouns inflect for number, pronominal marking, indefinite marking and cases. The 
011: pronominal possessive suffixes are used to mark the kinship relations and ownership. 
012: It is a typologically interesting feature. A closed set of adjectives which end in -o , -a 
013: and -ə ending adjectives inflect for gender. The plural suffix -səb is attached to the 
014: noun stem. There are only two classifiers in Darai to show human being and non-
015: human objects. Additionally, a few quasi-classifiers related to units of measurements 
016: are also found. The case markers used in the language are dative-accusative, ablative, 
017: comitative and ergative-instrumental. Other case relation is specified by postpositions. 
018: The postpositions are also used to show ablative, allative, benefactive, purposive, 
019: comitative and terminative cases. All these postpositions except the allative 
020: compound with the genitive marker before they are attached to the nominal stems 
021: mainly with pronouns. The position classes of the nominal morphemes are as follows:  
022: pronominal possessive suffix, number, case markers, postposition and indefiniteness.  
023: Darai distinguishes the first person, second person and third person pronouns. 
024: There is no formal distinction of gender in pronouns. The third person pronouns are 
025: based on remote demonstratives. Pronouns decline differently for different cases. 
026: Ablative and comitative cases are attached to the oblique stems. Aside from personal 
027: pronouns, there are interrogative pronouns, reflexive pronouns, indefinite pronouns, 
028: reciprocal pronouns, demonstrative pronouns and possessive pronouns. Darai 
029: pronouns are characterized by three levels of honorificity in the second person 
030: pronouns and two levels of honorificity in the third person pronouns.
