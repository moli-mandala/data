045: Ergative-instrumental  case: -i⟦U+F0E2⟧ , -hi⟦U+F0E2⟧ , -ei⟦U+F0E2⟧   
046:  
047: The ergative and instrumental share the same the case marker -i⟦U+F0E2⟧,  or -hi⟦U+F0E2⟧ . The 
048: former form is realized if the nominal stem ends in a consonant and other vowel 
049: except -u . The marker -hi⟦U+F0E2⟧  is realized if the noun ends in -u . Speakers also utter only 
050: -i  without nasalization. If a nominal stem ends in consonant, there is also an 
051: epenthetic vowel inserted thus the ergative marking results in -ei⟦U+F0E2⟧. Consider the 
052: example (55) where the case marker functions as an instrumental case.

056:  
057:  
058: səur-ke  
059: ləuri-ĩ  
060: mar-lə  
061: fish-DAT 
062: stick-INST 
063: kill-PST 
064:  
065: ‘A stick killed Saur (a kind of fish).’ (EI.CND.026) 
066: Ergative marker encodes the agent of the transitive clause. Darai is not a 
067: consistently ergative language. The ergative marker is consistently present with the 
068: subject of the transitive clauses if the subjects are the third person pronouns and other 
069: nouns.  Irrespective of tense, aspect and mood, the first and second person pronouns 
070: do not code ergativity (56). Therefore, the split begins between the second and third 
071: person pronouns.
