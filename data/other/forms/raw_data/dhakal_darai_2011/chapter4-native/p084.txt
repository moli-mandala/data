003:  Kellog (1955:492) maintains, "Any part of speech except a postposition or 
004: conjunction may be repeated, to express, as the case may be the various ideas of 
005: repetition, variety, intensity, or continuance." Two nouns are juxtaposed with an 
006: emphasis marker (particle) -ei intervening between the two nouns which are 
007: reduplicated in Darai. Thus, the first nouns get emphatic marker -ei and the second 
008: noun is unmarked.3 This construction expresses intensity or emphasis as in (18).
