087: Locative: -jə⟦U+F0E2⟧, -hi⟦U+F0E2⟧ 
088: Locative -jə⟦U+F0E2⟧ 'on/in/at' is attached to a noun which ends in a consonant. If the 
089: noun ends in a vowel, an epenthetic vowel occurs between the stem and locative 
090: marker -jə⟦U+F0E2⟧ . The locative is used to locate a person or thing in a physical place at an 
091: instant of time. It refers to static location. This occurs with inanimate nominals in the 
092: corpus. Locative in place is shown in (46a-b) whereas locative in time is shown in 
093: (46c).
