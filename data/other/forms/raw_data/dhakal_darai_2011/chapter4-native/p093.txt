061: Notice that classifier -ṭa  which generally refers to non-human is also used to 
062: refer to human beings. When classifier -ṭa  is used for human being, it agrees with the 
063: gender of the head noun (33c). However, dzena can not be used for the non-human 
064: referents. For example, (33f) is unacceptable.  
065: Additionally, there are also a set of lexical classifiers used in Darai.9 They 
066: occur in numeral + unit+ noun order. They are primarily used to count volume, 
067: weight, distance, time and so on. We present a set of such quasi-classifiers in (34) and 
068: examples are given in (35).
