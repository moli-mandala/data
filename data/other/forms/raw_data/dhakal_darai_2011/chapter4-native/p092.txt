026: iv. Classifiers  
027: The numeral and classifier sequence is necessarily number+ classifier+ noun. 
028: There are only two true classifiers in Darai, e.g. dzena/dzəna and  
029: -ṭa, -oṭa or -ɡoṭa. Choice of classifier is governed by the membership of the head. 
030: The former dzena/dzəna is obligatorily restricted to human nouns (33a) whereas the 
031: other classifier, -otạ is often used with non-human nouns (33b-d). There are instances 
032: where it is often used with human nouns as well. Darai classifiers are given in Table 
033: (4.2) and illustrated in (33).
