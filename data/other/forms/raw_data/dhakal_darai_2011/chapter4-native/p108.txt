051:  
052: Darai can typically be described as a split ergative language in which 
053: ergativity is determined by nominal hierarchy. The third person pronouns and all 
054: other noun phrases with transitive clauses operate on an ergative/absolutive basis.  
055:  
056: ii. Postpositions 
057: Postpositions are generally mediated by the genitive marker in Darai. Masica 
058: (1991:231) argues, "This layer (the third layer) is mediated by Layer II suffixes, most 
059: often a Genitive (in some languages also a Dative, Ablative or even an Agentive or 
060: Locative). It may be considered a postposition." 
061:  
062: Postposition maṭe  
063: The postposition maṭe 'from a specific source' is mediated by genitive marker. 
064: This differs from -se in that the later only indicates the general source. Examples are 
065: given in (59).
