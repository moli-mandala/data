003: 4.1.2 Noun formation processes 
004: Noun formation processes described in this section includes derivation and 
005: compounding. 
006: i. Derivational morphology 
007: In addition to free standing nouns, nouns are derived by using suffixes. A 
008: small set of nouns are derived from verbs and nouns. Each of the process is discussed 
009: in the following paragraphs.  
010:  
011: a. Nominals with -ai    
012: The derivational suffix -ai 'nominalization' is attached to verb stems to form 
013: nouns.  This is similar to action or state nominalization (Comrie and Thompson 1985: 
014: 350). They can be formed from verbs by the suffix -ai. They refer to 'the fact, the act, 
015: the quality, or the occurrence of the verbs' (Comrie and Thompson 1985: 350). Action 
016: nominalization can be illustrated in (6) through (8).
