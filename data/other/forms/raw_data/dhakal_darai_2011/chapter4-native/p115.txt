003: 4.2.2 Demonstrative pronouns  
004: There are two sets of demonstrative pronouns: proximal and distal. Proximal 
005: pronouns are used to refer to the person or thing close to the speaker. By contrast, the 
006: distal pronouns are used mainly to show the distal settings or things. The 
007: demonstrative pronouns are characterized by two levels of honorificity as well. The 
008: third person pronouns are based on remote demonstratives.
