008: 4.1.3 Inflectional morphology 
009: This section discusses inflectional morphology of nouns. Darai has two 
010: grammatical genders: masculine and feminine. Darai nouns also index pronominal 
011: possessive suffixes. Classifiers, indefiniteness, case markers and postpositions are 
012: also discussed in this section.  
013:  
014: i. Gender4 
015:  
016: Darai has a grammatical gender. Two genders, viz. masculine and feminine 
017: are differentiated in Darai. In most cases, a distinction is determined by the sex of the 
018: animate noun. Biological gender in animate nouns is represented by different lexical 
019: items as can be seen in (20). A few lexical nouns are derived from masculine nouns.5 
020: Faminine nouns are derived from masculine counterparts. Such pairs in Darai are 
021: concord sensitive and semantically meaningful. Male-female pairs of some nouns are 
022: given in (20).
