020: Endocentric compound 
021:  
022: Bauer (1988:42) notes, "Endocentric compounds denote a subclass of items 
023: referred to by one of their elements." The semantic relationship between the 
024: components of endocentric compounds can be of genitive or part whole type.

083: iii. Reduplication of nouns 
084: Reduplication is a productive noun formation process in Indo-Aryan 
085: languages.  Marrants (1994:3486) mentions: 
086: Reduplication is most often found to express certain semantic feature on 
087: nouns. Reduplication often indicates plurality; on verbs it often signals 
088: repetitive or intensive aspect. However, cross-linguistically, reduplicating 
089: affixes serve any function that any affix with its own may serve, including all 
090: derivational or inflectional functions.
