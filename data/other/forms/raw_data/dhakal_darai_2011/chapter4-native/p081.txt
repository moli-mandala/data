056: b. Compounding 
057: Compound nouns are defined as the nouns composed of more than one root in 
058: contrast to simple nouns, having one root. They are headed by a noun which is a final 
059: member of the group. A morphological characteristic of Darai compounds is that 
060: postposition is attached to only to the final member. We will categorize the compound 
061: nouns into coordinate compound, endocentric compound and exocentric compound. 
062:  
063: Coordinate compound 
064:  
065: Aikhenvald (2007:30) notes, "Coordinate compounds refers to a unitary 
066: concept of two juxtaposed nouns." It refers to the original entities and is known as 
067: dwanda samasa in Indian grammatical tradition (Abbi 2001:173). They are composed 
068: of semantically related nouns. This is also termed 'copulative compound' (see Bauer 
069: 1988:43; Wali and Koul 1997:285).
