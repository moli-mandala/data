025: Genitive:  -kə, -rə  
026:  
027: Genitives are marked with -kə  or -rə.  It is marked -rə 'GEN' for first person 
028: pronouns, second and third person singular pronouns but it is marked with -kə  with 
029: second and third person plural pronouns as well as other nouns. The genitive 
030: constructions in Darai consist of the possessor and possessed in that order and the 
031: possessor has the genitive case.
