Root extracted and independently viewed the single embedded original image on
each cached alternate PDF page 66–68. Their printed folios are **49, 50, 51**.
Each native image is 2203×3142; TIFF extraction and pixel-preserving PNG views
add roughly 180KB of TIFF data, with no new download, OCR or upsampling. The

`native-grammar-image-manifest.json`. This range's printed+17 mapping is verified
locally; it must not be generalized to other portions of the volume.

The three pages support the earlier identity decision. The p50 grammar says
Gurgaon is its standard; Rohtak/Delhi retain historical mixture qualifications.
The available source scripts are Devanagari and Persian; Gurmukhi specimens
explicitly were not printed. No modern Jhambaus point is inherited by the new
historical lects.

Two additional editorial scope decisions follow from original p51:

- The proposed obliques of `jō` and `kaun` are explicitly hypothetical: the
  author says he has not encountered examples. Preserve each printed proposal
  and its exact source context in the audit, but do not present those proposed
  obliques as attested lexical forms. The source's separately quoted base
  pronouns remain individually eligible.
- The author explicitly claims borrowing from Bagri for the second-person
  plural present copula. Preserve the exact claimed analysis on the relevant
  target alternative. This is different from merely observing mixed languages.
  Assess whether any included outside Bagri comparator supplies a unique exact
  donor endpoint with compatible grammatical function. If the donor is not
  uniquely supported, retain the claimed donor language and ambiguity in the
  audit/analysis, with no fabricated borrowing edge.

Outside comparator cells attributed to stable Sirsa/Hissar survey lects require
their own qualified registered Bagri dialect tags if emitted; the two target
Ahirwati lects do not cover those attestations. Source case suffixes, alternatives,
nasal marks and superscripts need complete fine reading and transcription review.
The six copula cells are physical units, not an assumed six-form output count.
