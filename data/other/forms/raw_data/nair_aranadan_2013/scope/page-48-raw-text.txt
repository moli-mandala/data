There is no copula verb in Mannan, unlike in Malayalam. 
 
itu een pookina kuura 
‘this is the house where I am going’ 
ninattu vantatu en makanu‘ the one who came yesterday is my son’ 
 
Like Malayalam, Mannan also does not have personal terminations.  
 
amma vantu ‘mother came’ 
makanu vantu ‘son came’ 
koocci vantu ‘fowl came’
