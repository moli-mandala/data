Imperative plural is expressed through two suffixes -ŋko and –uŋko. -ŋko occurs after -a and   
-u ending stems: after other stems -uŋko occurs.

Verbal participle forms also resemble Malayalam. -i ending stems do not take any marker in 
participle form.

‘having cried’

‘having seen’

‘having jumped’
