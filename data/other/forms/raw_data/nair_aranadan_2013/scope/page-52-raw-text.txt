Verb 
 
Transitive and causative suffixes of Muduga are different from those  of Malayalam l, d, t, 
and tt are the transitives suffixes. 
 
aat-l-ura 
 aatara ‘(she) made to dance’ 
kaan-d-ura 
 kaattra ‘(she) will make to see’ 
niiŋg-t-ina 
 niikkina ‘(she) made to move’ 
ketu-tt-ina 
 ketuttina ‘she made to put out’ 
 
Causative suffixes are –acc and –icc 
 
kuti-cc-ina 
‘(she) caused to drink’ 
nine-cc-ina 
‘(she) caused to think’ 
oot-icc-ina 
‘(she) caused to run’ 
eer-icc-ina 
‘(she) made to cause to climb’ 
 
Majority of the tense suffixes of Muduga are different from those of Malayalam as well as 
Kannada. 
 
Present tense suffixes are –ø-, -ur-, and –r-
