Three durative forms, viz., past, present and future duratives are found in Betta Kurumba. 
Past and future durative make use of the auxiliary –ir while present durative employs the 
auxiliary ood, both meaning ‘to be’. 
 
bandun-ir-d-i    ->                    banduniddi ‘was coming-I’ 
tindun-ood-a  
‘(is) eating-he/she/it’ 
bandum-ir-p-iya 
‘will be coming-I/we’ 
 
By inserting the oblique base of the reflexive pronoun tan between the verbal participle form 
and the auxiliary verb ir/ood ‘to be’, the reflexive aspect is expressed. 
 
ardun-tan-ir-daa 
-> arduntanidda 
‘he was beating himself’ 
ardun-tan-oo-d-a  
‘he is beating himself’ 
 
Pronominal Suffixes 
 
Betta Kurumba has two sets of pronominal suffixes; one follows past forms while the other 
follows non-past forms. Another important feature is the lack of gender distinction in third 
person.
