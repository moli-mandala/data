Malamuthan does not have any equivalent for the negative alla of Malayalam and both alla 
and illa of Malayalam have illa as their equivalent in Malamuthan. 
 
Malamuthan Malayalam  
oonillaanroo avanalla 
‘not he’ 
oolillaanroo avalalla 
‘not she’
