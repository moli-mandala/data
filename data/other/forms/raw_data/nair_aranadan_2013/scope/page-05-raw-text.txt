The first person pronouns naanu and naa are in free variation. 
 
 
naanu/naa 
poyuley 
‘I went’ 
 
 
The case suffixes are added to the base en-  as in Malayalam. 
 
 
enra kullili uppula ‘there is salt in my hut’ 
 
enaakku beenta      ‘I don’t want” 
 
Through there are two nominative forms for second person singular viz.., nii and niiyu, the 
first one is commonly used. Before accusative, the second person singular pronoun is innu, 
before dative it is na and at all other places incu is used. 
The second person plural form niinŋka is used as honorific singular as well. When used as 
plural, the post position okka (‘all) is also added. 
 
Eg. niŋkalokkaayi mubari   ‘all of you come’ 
 
This Adiyans address kins through marriage, who are elder to oneself as niiŋka and all other 
kins as nii.
