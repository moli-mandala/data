Other comments: Kumarbhag Paharia Malto is spoken by approximately 12,000 speakers in Jharkhand, Odisha and West Bengal states, India. Kumarbhag Paharia Malto has a vigesimal system. Kumarbhag Paharia Malto has not remained any traditional Dravidian numerals now, they adopt Indo-Aryan numeral system for general counting now. 
Note that the transcriptions between traditional phonetic symbols and IPA symbolsː c = t͡ʃ,  j = d͡ʒ, y = j, v = w, N = ɲ, T = ʈ, D = ɖ, N = ɲ, R = ɽ, Ry = ɽJ ?.
