The pp. 226–227 kinship table is headed “My father,” “My mother,” etc., and the first Saora expression explicitly includes `Mian` for “my,” followed by ditto marks. Later rows express nested relations with multiple words. These are attested possessive/recursive expressions and belong in the recovery inventory as complete expressions. The earlier decision to exclude them because they were not simplex lemmas is superseded: all 41 table rows are now under review. Do not strip `Mian` or infer free kin terms from the expressions. The preceding p. 225 personal-name anecdotes also require inspection as source evidence; they have not yet been reviewed in this recovery pass.

Fawcett states immediately below the table on p. 227 that an **unnamed Oriya speaker who knew Saora well first wrote out the tables**. Fawcett then reviewed them with an **unnamed Saora speaker** for pronunciation. He warns that exact pronunciation could not be conveyed and says he used the Missionary alphabet. The table is therefore a mediated, source-specific historical witness, not evidence of direct Fawcett-only elicitation or an IPA transcription. No table-specific speaker locality is given. Its inclusion in the journal is public domain, but the current screening is analysis-only; no forms, page images, or prose from it are installed.

Whole-article scope remains open: only pp. 226–227 are locally verified. The reported extent pp. 206–272 requires a complete source census before installation.
