# Kolami/Basim metadata and display proposal

This is a source-local proposal, not an installed route or an accepted profile.
The ingestion checklist is active, with comparative-table, grammar/interlinear,
and OCR-heavy requirements. The PDF skill applies to original-image review.
No rows, canonical metadata or existing historical holds were changed here.

Use one new scoped citation, `grierson1906kolamibasim`, for the complete original
pp. 561–569 block and independently accepted outside occurrences. Do not redeclare
the existing `grierson1906lsi4`, `grierson1906naiki`, `hislop1866papers`, or `dedr`
settings. These existing sources retain their own definitions and attribution.
The separate 1928 `grierson-lsi1928` Kolami material is overlap evidence, not the
same physical 1906 source occurrence.

The Wun specimen uses canonical Kolami and a proposed historical Wun survey
lect. General grammar pp. 562–563 was synthesized from several supplied texts
and a wordlist; it is not all securely Wun-localized. Such unlocalized target
grammar remains Kolami without a guessed survey tag. The Basim subsection and
specimen use existing Naikri with the historical Basim tag. Page 565 must be
routed by sectional span, not its running header. Existing modern Kolami and
Naiki lects remain unchanged. New lect Glottocodes and coordinates are blank.

Grierson explicitly supplied the Kolami interlinear translation (p. 562).
Retain that translator attribution, the limited-material cautions, qualified
analysis, and ambiguous singular/plural use. The Basim pronunciation paragraph
(p. 566) describes variant correspondences printed A/ū, l/n, final n/m, and n/nd/d.
These are source analysis, not global display replacements or licenses to infer
unprinted variant rows. Preserve every independently printed quantity and
mark; no Standard Telugu/Kolami restoration is justified.

The proposed display profile follows the narrowly reviewed neighboring LSI
Naiki/Kui house convention: lowercase and `ṅ` → `ŋ` (including decomposed and
capital forms). All other source vowel quantities, nasal-vowel marks, `ñ`,
retroflex/other consonant marks, word boundaries and meaningful hyphens remain
literal. Do not map `ch` or `w` globally, interpret damaged marks phonologically,
or duplicate Roman transcription into Native or Phonemic. Phonemic is blank
unless the source supplies a genuinely separate analysis. Roman-only witnesses
have blank Native. The volume's general notation key is supporting context;
local source usage and individual glyph evidence take precedence.

The settings draft is deliberately **not install-ready**: its profile must be
generated from the complete accepted Unicode inventory, then tested on all
forms and NFC/NFD edge cases. An importer command is omitted until an actual
guarded coordinator exists. Bibliographic inclusion counts, final audit paths,
all quoted auxiliary authorities, source-wide closure and independent acceptance
must be refreshed after complete extraction. Full compiled/build/browser gates
are deferred under the current source-stage-only instruction.
