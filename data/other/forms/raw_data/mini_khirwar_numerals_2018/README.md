# Mini 2018 Khirwar numerals

The [Khirwar numeral page](https://lingweb.eva.mpg.de/channumerals/Khirwa.htm) in Eugene Chan's *Numeral Systems of the World's Languages* credits **Suraj Mini**, a Khirwar Bible translator, as data provider on **10 September 2018**. The HTML was downloaded 25 September 2026 and checked in as `Khirwa.htm` (26,673 bytes, SHA256 `d6e7cfe2cee35dfddb843a0206ae294df3070083a327882eb7d7ed0f4de8cbd0`). The [numeralbank/channumerals v1.0.1 archival extraction](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15649312) is CC BY 4.0. The importer parses the checked-in primary HTML directly.

The table has **20 rows × 2 displayed cells = 40 cells**. The 100/200 and 400/800 cells contain two numbered values each, giving **42 explicit numeral prompts**. Items 5 and 50 print `p ã tʃɡo` and `p ã tʃasɡo` with unexplained internal spacing around the nasal vowel. Rather than silently join characters or treat them as separate words, those **two cells are held** as typed transcription uncertainty in `audit.jsonl`. The remaining **40 readings are installed**. The source's `8 00.` and `2 000.` typography is retained in the audit; integer prompt IDs only stabilize keys/locators. The seeded 20-item raw-versus-output audit found zero material errors, and first/last cells, two-item cells, and held cells are tested separately. No other numbered cell is dropped. Chinese commentary and inferred arithmetic components are excluded.

The page explicitly says Khirwar people have completely borrowed their current numerals from neighboring Indo-Aryan speech. Every installed row therefore carries the canonical `loanword` tag, while the unspecified donor language, specific etymon, and graph fields remain blank. The source's claim is preserved verbatim in the audit; no independent etymological judgment is added.

The source names **Khirwar**, also **Palamu**. [Glottolog](https://glottolog.org/resource/languoid/id/khir1237) treats Khirwar as a separate spoken L1 language (`khir1237`), absent from Jambu's prior canonical registry. The page gives only Garhwa/Latehar districts in Jharkhand, no exact elicitation site or separately named variety. A canonical `Khirwar` row uses Jambu's existing `S. Dravidian II` clade, with coordinates blank and no dialect row.

Raw CSV `Form` and `Phonemic` intentionally contain the same source IPA, from which compiled `Original` derives. The display profile maps `tʃ`/`dʒ` → house `c`/`j`, source `ɡ` → `g`, `ɪ`/`ʊ` → `i`/`u`, `ʋ` → `v`, and retroflex/aspirated consonants to house notation. It preserves source `ɑ` and `ɐ` rather than guessing vowel length or collapsing three printed `a`-quality symbols without a transcription key. Their phonemic status remains open to linguistic review. `Phonemic`/`Original` retain source values. `Native` is blank. Numeral IDs supply stable entry keys/locators; `num loanword` are the only tags. No OCR contributed.

The source-local importer, audit, dated CSV/YAML, and profile are installed. The full CLDF build, compiled graph/reference checks, complete test suite, and browser database/app inspection are deferred under the user's prohibition on database builds and remote execution.
