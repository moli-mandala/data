# Nair 2013: complete Kerala Kani chapter

The canonical source inputs contain **36 Kanikkaran rows** from the complete Kani chapter, printed/PDF pages 32–33 of Ravi Sankar S. Nair's *Tribal Languages of Kerala*, *Language in India* 13:7 (July 2013). The [publisher page](https://languageinindia.com/july2013/ravisankarkeralatriballanguages.html) identifies the work; the cached 58-page PDF is pinned by SHA-256 `4d2588987dab5d15c62ecc95330ccf7e925a9bce80d08068b91ca3f48c8cc5af`. No explicit redistribution licence was found in the original. Native PDF text and original-page visual checks were used; OCR did not contribute.

`whole-recovery/` preserves the complete 42-unit census and 66 physical witness slots. The installed audit retains all of them: 36 target witnesses are emitted, while 21 Malayalam controls, two Tamil Nadu Kani controls, two comparisons without an independently stated language, and five community/autonym documentary witnesses remain audit-only. Form-free phonological, grammatical, identity and bibliography paragraphs are also accounted for. No unprinted stems, pronunciation values or graph links were inferred from the comparison prose.

Canonical assets are `data/other/forms/20260927-nair-kani.csv`, its adjacent YAML, and `installed-audit.jsonl`. The new base is Kanikkaran (`kani1275`, ISO `kev`), as the [Glottolog source record](https://glottolog.org/resource/reference/id/320019) maps Nair's chapter. The source-qualified Kerala Kani dialect remains distinct from the explicitly compared Tamil Nadu Kani speech. Coordinates are blank: the chapter lists broad settlement regions without identifying an elicitation site. The existing frontend clade `S. Dravidian I` is reused. Primary `nair2013kani` and reported, uncollated auxiliary `somasekharannairkanikkarude` have separate bibliography/settings records.

The explicit transcription route is `convert: false`, `preserve_hyphens: true`. Printed doubled vowels, underdots, low lines, nasal marks and breves are preserved literally in `Form` and parser-derived `Original`; distinct `Native` and `Phonemic` layers are blank because the source does not supply them. All 22 NFD codepoints are covered by this preservation decision. The negative suffix is printed plain `aa`; its source-supported `suffix neg` analysis does not add an unprinted hyphen. Claims of correspondence and shared morphology remain attributed in the audit rather than becoming ancestry edges.

Its explicit `--install` mode verifies the pinned independent 20-unit/21-row audit, the one-row correction and independent targeted addendum, byte-exact regeneration, accepted audit key closure, metadata and original canonical baselines.
