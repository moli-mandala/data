The pages contain six vowel-correspondence pairs, five consonant pairs, two prose triple contrasts, three l-to-y pairs, three accusative pairs, three present examples with three Malayalam parenthetical comparisons, three negative examples, seven native-word examples, and two bound-morpheme discussions. `prepare_review_candidate.py` emits a headerless 15-column `review-candidate.csv` of those 36 explicit target witnesses and `review-audit.jsonl` retaining all 42 units/66 witness slots and their control or documentary dispositions. The guarded canonical installer uses the independently accepted revised candidate. The registered base is Kanikkaran (Glottolog `kani1275`, ISO `kev`) with a source-qualified Kerala Kani variety tag. [Glottolog's own bibliography maps Nair 2013's Kani chapter to Kanikkaran](https://glottolog.org/resource/reference/id/320019), and [its language record](https://glottolog.org/resource/languoid/id/kani1275) supplies those codes. Nair expressly contrasts Kerala and Tamil Nadu Kani, so they cannot be merged by name. The chapter supplies broad settlement areas only; it cannot support a precise survey-point coordinate or more local dialect divisions. Malayalam forms are controls and the first two “words like” forms in the Tamil Nadu comparison have no explicit independent language label. The source cites P. Somasekharan Nair, *Kanikkarude Samsarabhasha*, *Samsakara Keralam* without year or pages; this is an attributed secondary authority, not a collated source of the printed examples.

The first reading retains printed doubled vowels, underdots, nasals, breves, and underlined consonants as literal Roman typography, rather than silently length-normalizing `aa`/`ee`/`uu`.
