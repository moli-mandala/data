# Kakmara identity evidence and source candidate

Research date: 2026-09-27. No canonical installation or registry change.

Suhrid Kumar Bhowmik, “Some Songs and a Short vocabulary of the Kakmaras,
a Nomadic Dravidian Tribe in Midnapore,” *Janajati Darpan*, issue 7:

https://weblibrary.in/books/Janajati_Darpan_Issue_7/viewstory.php?fn=chapter%2Fchapter-004.html&tt=Some+Songs+and+a+Short+vocabulary+of+the+Kakmaras%2C+a+Nomadic+Dravidian+Tribe+in+Midnapore++-++Suhrid+Kumar+Bhowmik

The indexed article text was inspected through web search; direct page opening
failed twice. The author reports field collection in 1971–72 and subsequent
work until 1995. He locates the community in Midnapore, particularly Contai and
Tamluk, and describes its language as closely related to Telugu. This is primary
field-research evidence, not a name-based inference from community identity.

The article contains a short English–Kakmara vocabulary in two table sections.
It is a promising diversity source. Before ingestion, obtain the complete
original article, check images and any songs omitted from indexed text, verify
publication metadata, and inventory every linguistic item. Do not call the
indexed table a complete source snapshot. The author's broader etymological
claims are not independently established by this identity evidence.

For Niranjan 2010, retain the literal label কাকমারা (দ্রাবিড়) and compare its
forms with Bhowmik before proposing a Telugu-based named variety. The available
evidence does not yet establish that both publications describe the same lect.
Do not invent a precise field site from the regional description.
