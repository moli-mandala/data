# Discovery and selection audit

The 2026-08-29 audit compared the local Glottolog bibliography snapshot (`hh.bib`) and the SIL
archive/search index against `sil_survey_sources.md`, restricted to India, Pakistan, and Bangladesh.
The selected gap is Alexander Kondakov's *A Sociolinguistic Survey of the Rabha Dialects of
Meghalaya and Assam*, SIL Electronic Survey Report 2013-016. It is clearly in scope by publisher,
series, archive URL, and content, and it publishes complete phonetic tables for Rongdani and Maituri.

Other missing candidates were triaged as follows:

- Ahirwal 2003, *Dadra and Nagar Haveli*: openly archived by Census of India and valuable, but no SIL
  author, commission, series, or archive connection was found; adjacent and excluded from this lane.
- Schmidt and Kaul 2008, Shina--Kashmiri vocabularies: accessible comparative source, but published
  in *Acta Orientalia*, not an SIL survey or archive publication; adjacent and excluded from this lane.
- Bhattacharya 1976, *The Tribal Languages of South Kerala*: high-value Glottolog wordlist source,
  but no verifiable accessible full scan was found.
- Beine 1994/2013 Gondi: SIL republished the survey and its large wordlist matrix, but the same data
  are already represented in the census and installed via the Rama--Çöltekin--Sofroniev digitisation.

The Rabha report was therefore the highest-value clearly SIL, accessible, non-duplicative lexical
source found in this audit.
