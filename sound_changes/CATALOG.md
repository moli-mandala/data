# Shared sound-change tags

Every rule line in the family files `rules/*.sc` ends with a comment carrying one or more tags from this list,
then a short note: `s Stop > $2-asp $2+asp   ; @s-stop-aspirate — hasta > hattʰa`.
Tags are what the cross-family synthesis reads (which lects share which change, and in which
relative order), so tag the *change*, not the notation, and reuse an existing tag whenever the
change is the same process even if its exact phonetic outcome differs (the outcome is read from the
rule itself). A rule that is several changes at once gets several tags.

Need a tag that is not here? Use `@x-<group>-<short-name>` (e.g. `@x-dardic-north-r-metathesis`)
and define it in your group's notes file. Do not edit this file.

## Non-phonological (excluded from the synthesis)
| tag | meaning |
|---|---|
| `@notation` | converting to a source's transcription (digraph aspirates, schwa letters, length marks, glyph variants) |
| `@morph` | citation-form morphology (infinitive -nā/-ik/-ṇẽ, -aka > -ā extensions, verb endings) |
| `@acc-loss` | removing Vedic accent marks (a notational necessity in every file; keep it separate) |

## Sister-branch correspondences (Nuristani)
Nuristani is not descended from OIA; its rules are correspondences from the OIA side. Tag them
with the tags above where the correspondence *looks like* the same change, plus
`@nur-archaism` where OIA has merged what Nuristani keeps apart (e.g. the PIIr palatals) and
`@nur-innovation` for Nuristani-internal changes.

### Conventions settled by reconciliation (round 2)
- `@notation` rules belong at the **chain tail** (source spelling conventions) or the **chain head**
  (headword normalisation: `ṁ Stop` → homorganic nasal, OIA `ē ō` → `e o` where the chain tracks
  mid-vowel length itself, root/homonym markers). `reconcile.py notation` moved 55 mid-chain notation
  rules to the tail with unchanged train NED; 26 stay in place because later rules depend on them
  (head normalisation, Awadhi's macron drop before compensatory lengthening, Nuristani ă/ĭ
  placeholder unmarking). All `@notation` rules are excluded from synthesis wherever they sit.
- Citation-form rules conditioned on `if:starred` / `if:!accented` (Turner's reconstructed a-stems
  cited with -o/-ā) are `@morph`, never sound changes.
- Class names are file-local. `G` (geminable consonants) legitimately differs by lect (with or
  without ś, ʦ, ḷ); `Nasal` includes `ṁ` in 7 files that keep anusvāra as a segment.
- Word-final citation rules for bare headwords (`if:starred a > o / C _ #`, `∅ > o / C _ #`, …)
  carry `!ext` so they do not fire on the extension-aware score's extended variants (which get
  their ending from the suffix). Rules that are the extension's own derivation (`a a > o`,
  `a k a > o`) must not carry it.
